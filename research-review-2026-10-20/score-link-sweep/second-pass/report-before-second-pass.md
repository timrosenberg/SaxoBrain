# Score Link Sweep

Checked October 20, 2026.

The catalog contains **1,140 work records**. **253 records lack a purchase URL**. Every one of those records has a research entry below.

Found **147 verified purchase sources** and **15 explicit composer or publisher ordering routes**. The remaining **91 records** have edition conflicts, rental or free sources, incomplete leads, or unresolved availability.

This inventory uses the `purchase` field. Some included records already have a free download, perusal score, local attachment or library reference. Existing purchase URLs were outside this sweep and were not checked for broken links.

Composer websites were checked for difficult cases. Each entry records the checked site when one was identified. Failed pages, missing personal sites and publisher fallbacks are described in the evidence or caveat.

A verified purchase source does not imply a complete performance set. Read the edition and accompaniment caveats before ordering. Stock, prices, shipping and fulfillment can change. No purchases or inquiries were sent.

**Catalog files were left unchanged.** This private report is outside the public `content` directory. The machine-readable manifest preserves URLs, queries, evidence and review provenance.

## Checks worth attention

- Cecilia Sung: Sur La Memoire has a sale candidate attributed to Stella Sung. Composer identity and accompaniment need review.
- Ben-Haim: Three Songs Without Words uses the tenor-compatible IMI7363 edition. The alto IMI7362 edition is different.
- Fox: Annexus has a direct Dorn DP0796 purchase page. Hear Again in Memory is sold as a solo work and conflicts with the catalog title and piano scoring.
- Goldstaub: the alto and piano purchase is Dorn DP0168. The separate DP502 Graphic IV listing uses celeste.
- Lauba: several studies are sold in collected volumes. Stan offers piano or CD accompaniment. XYL and Worksong have separate CD products.
- Albright: Heater is sold as a full score. Mozart KV314 is sold as an individual soprano part. Neither link supplies a complete performance set.
- Snyder: the Juggernaut repository item states that the paper has been withdrawn. It is retained as a lead.

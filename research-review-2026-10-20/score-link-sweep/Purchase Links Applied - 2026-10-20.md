# Purchase Links Applied

Applied October 20, 2026.

**131 purchase URLs added to 130 work records.** The Vaughan Williams entry has separate links for its saxophone part and piano accompaniment.

**17 additional candidates were held** because of edition, identity, accompaniment or availability concerns. All 106 results outside the verified-purchase group were left unchanged. The catalog now has **123 works without a purchase URL**.

Only the `purchase` fields changed. Existing titles, composers, instrumentation, levels, recordings, research and note bodies were preserved. Edition and format labels distinguish collections, reductions, study scores and individual parts.

The Fasch Sonata had a non-URL placeholder in its purchase list. It was replaced with the verified Rascher edition link. Its former value is preserved in the application manifest.

[Original research report](Score%20Link%20Sweep%20-%202026-10-20.md) · [Application manifest](purchase-application-applied.json)

## Validation

- Fresh Hugo builds before and after the edits both passed.
- All 131 URLs render as links on the 130 corresponding work pages.
- Comparison with a snapshot of every work confirms that only the 130 approved purchase fields changed.
- All 17 held candidates and all other work records match the snapshot.
- The changed files pass `git diff --check`.
- The normalized before/after address lists are identical: 2,559 addresses.
- The live-address check fails on the same eight missing addresses in both the before and after builds. These pre-existing gaps are listed below.
- A broader whitespace check flags existing trailing whitespace in David Amram, Ode to Lord Buckley. That file was unchanged.

No commit, push or deployment was performed.

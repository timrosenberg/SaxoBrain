# Randall Snyder score sources

Checked 2026-10-20. All ten existing Snyder works were investigated with three research agents. Searches covered Dorn, Southern, J.P. Publications, Brixton, the Vienna publisher lead, Universal Edition, and the former repository locations.

## Applied to the catalog

**Seven Epigrams:** added separate Universal Edition purchase links for the [score, UES108775-000](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/c71a1f4c6b6541c3bdeaf77d23878b4d) and [alto saxophone part, UES108775-262](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/c1c5ab8c0119a8eb947b48647b9ba670). Each product offers an instant PDF and print on demand, with an active cart control. The score was €23.95 and the part €7.95 when checked. The work identity matches the downloaded score's title, composer, 1971 date, instrumentation, and Fred Hemke association. This verifies a current edition of the work; it does not claim that the modern engraving is identical to a historical Southern edition.

The public work body explains that the score and part are sold separately. Its existing local PDF link remains intact.

**Variations:** Tim subsequently confirmed that his older *Variations* score is the same work as *Tritonal Variations*. Added the [Universal Edition score, UES107318-262](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/d84d917f813b8ba4480bde06351a44fe), with a public note explaining the newer title. The product page was rechecked and offers instant PDF download and print on demand, each €7.95. The existing catalog title and address remain.

## Why the old links fail

[Elsevier's official retirement notice](https://digitalcommons.elsevier.com/egs-works-redirect) says SelectedWorks and Expert Gallery Suite were discontinued in April 2025. Exact legacy SelectedWorks links for Chance Designs, Improvisations, Seven Epigrams, and Variations now redirect there.

The University of Nebraska–Lincoln records for [Chance Designs](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/241/), [Improvisations](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/269/), [Juggernaut](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/37/), [Seven Epigrams](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/312/), and [Variations](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/341/) explicitly say the papers were withdrawn. The [duplicate Improvisations record](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/155/) is also withdrawn. The notices give no reason or replacement location. Historical search results and collection pages still show score listings, so their PDF labels cannot establish present download availability.

No replacement institutional score host was verified. The four existing local score PDFs were preserved. Variations has an old attachment-style reference, but no corresponding PDF was found in the repository's score assets.

## Publisher leads

- **Dorn:** current Groth searches returned 43 Snyder products across two pages. The [June 2025 Dorn catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/images/DP%20Catalog%20June%202025.pdf) and the live products confirmed Tristesse; the live shop also confirmed Excursions. No exact listing for the other five works missing purchase links was found. The PDF omits some live products, so absence from that PDF is inconclusive.
- **Southern:** the [current Keiser search](https://keiserproductions.com/?s=Snyder) and [Hal Leonard composer catalog](https://www.halleonard.com/search/composer/181523/snyder-randall) did not supply an exact current route for those five works. Seven Epigrams has historical Southern publication evidence. A current Southern S224 product or order route was not verified. Another publisher's similarly titled work was excluded.
- **J.P. Publications:** the [current JP Zālo collection at American Composers Alliance](https://composers.com/collections/jp-zalo-publications?filter.p.m.custom.aca_composer=Randall+Snyder) lists four Snyder works, all unrelated flute repertoire. This does not establish the fate of every historical JP publication.
- **Brixton:** its current catalog is at [Cimarron Music Press](https://www.cimarronmusic.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=Randall+Snyder). Some Snyder products were accessible. The exact composer filter and further pagination met a security block, so the check remains incomplete. No exact target score was verified there.
- **Vienna lead:** the exact name Vienna Modern Masterworks appears in the UNL biography. [Mica's music database identifies Vienna Modern Masters as a recording label](https://db.musicaustria.at/node/41424). A [separate work record](https://db.musicaustria.at/node/129008) distinguishes a Vienna Masterworks score imprint from that recording label. A current exact Snyder score catalog or ordering route under the supplied name remains unverified.
- **Miltmore Press:** a new historical lead for Improvisations. The [UNL abstract](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/musicsnyder/155/) identifies its copyright and permission. A current publisher site or explicit ordering route was not verified.

## Universal Edition title and edition comparisons

The Variations identity question was resolved by Tim's confirmation below. Excursions and Tristesse remain held for edition comparison, so their alternate purchase links were not applied.

### Variations / Tritonal Variations

[UE work page](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/navigation/7ce6fb95141a93dc2affdbc9301085fd), [part product](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/d84d917f813b8ba4480bde06351a44fe), [publisher preview](https://www.universaledition.com/media/e1/34/a3/1716798670/6a345fbb5fde805df40151632c67322c.pdf).

The inspected preview says “Tritonal Variations” and “1972 rev. 2013.” The UE description specifies eleven episodes and performance on any size saxophone. UNL's generic Variations record also specifies any size saxophone and a 2013 revision. The initial research held this match pending confirmation. Tim then stated: “Tritonal Variations = Variations. Seems that he changed the title from the score that I have.” This resolves the title identity for the existing catalog entry. The exact UE product page was rechecked before applying its purchase link. Unrelated saxophone-trio variations remain excluded.

### Excursions / Excursions of the Third Kind

[UE work page](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/navigation/2d2e9a87dadb5bd106a24a79e241fe5f), [score](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/271085408c0e947fc31684852bbb3e9c), [alto part](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/87a09ef30af53bb7eecaa24254b46093), [publisher preview](https://www.universaledition.com/media/bf/f3/5e/1714501557/8622a42a0c0695b0c308839886b2eb40.pdf).

The preview confirms 2005, alto and piano, and dedication to Paul Haar. These agree with the existing work's evidence. No direct comparison of its opening notation against the Dorn edition was completed. The existing verified Dorn source already provides a purchase route.

### Tristesse

[UE work page](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/navigation/880ed4ef3834f744649d0412d746cc17), [score](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/b91a3cacb1672ef7835b76a9326109e2), [alto part](https://www.universaledition.com/en/index.php/detail/4b189e3e071147b375db5f1077e423a4), [publisher preview](https://www.universaledition.com/media/cf/ab/ec/1729602156/5815036f6aa56440a0f891f74c02616b.pdf).

The UE score says 2001. Its description associates the work with Snyder's father, who died in 2001. Existing catalog score evidence records 1999. The discrepancy remains open. No composition date or edition identity was silently changed.

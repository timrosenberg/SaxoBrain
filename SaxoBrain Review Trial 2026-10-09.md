## Context for the French guide

*10 ans avec le saxophone* is a teaching catalog by Claude Delangle, Frédéric Juranville, François Léclaircie and Jean-Pierre Magnac. Its years count instrumental study from the beginning. It places the three works above toward the third year in that system. See the introduction, [PDF page 4](http://catalogue.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf#page=4). The scanned edition carries 1993 and 1997 copyright dates.

## Research coverage and limits

Searches used titles, composer names, arranger names and catalog numbers. Sources checked included composer materials, publisher catalogs, the French pedagogical guide, Royal Conservatory syllabi, a school curriculum, library finding aids and recording metadata. Existing links and local album relations were checked for the Mayrand and Shapiro works. Neither had a matching local album relation in that check.

Composer-provided previews were inspected visually. Relevant guide pages were rendered and inspected. Syllabus headings and specific entries were checked in the PDFs. No complete saxophone part was inspected for these ten works. No absence of extended techniques is inferred from missing evidence.

This packet preserves unsuccessful searches as gaps. It does not claim that a score or recording cannot exist. Tim-approved level assignments are recorded above. No title changes, composer corrections or recording replacements have been applied.

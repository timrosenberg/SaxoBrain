The publisher-distributor description characterizes its jazz harmony and modal material. One page of the E-flat alto saxophone part for movement I, Black Light, was inspected; the piano part and later movements were not.

The description says the first movement uses driving 6/8 and imitation, the middle alternates cadenza-like saxophone writing, transparent themes and a darker section, and the finale builds.

**Score observations.**
- The preview is explicitly the E-flat alto saxophone part for movement I, Black Light, so its notes are in the saxophone part rather than concert pitch. After a full-measure opening rest in 6/8 at Vivo, quarter=120, the line moves into a sustained passage with slurred groups, frequent chromatic accidentals, and dynamics ranging from forte to piano. The excerpt later shows register expansion with ledger-line notes and renewed faster note groups. Only this one solo-part page was inspected; it does not show the piano accompaniment or later movements.

**Score/sample.**
- [Alfred public E-flat alto saxophone preview of movement I; one page visually inspected.](https://content.alfred.com/catpages/01-ADV7032.pdf) (inspected).

**Review focus.** Inference from the inspected part: assess fluency through chromatic, slurred passages at a brisk compound-meter pulse, register control, and long dynamic shaping; compare with the piano texture and other movements in the full score.

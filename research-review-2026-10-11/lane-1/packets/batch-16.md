## 381. Parable XI

Catalog: [Vincent Persichetti - Parable XI.md](</Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Vincent Persichetti - Parable XI.md>)

- Identity: Persichetti’s Parable XI, Op. 123, is a solo alto saxophone work commissioned by Brian Minor and premiered by him in Kalamazoo on 14 April 1973. A University of North Texas dissertation describes an ABA' design with a very fast, more articulated B section and a three-line cadenza; it reports a 2.5-octave span and several color techniques. - Archive lists Parable XI for solo alto saxophone, commissioned by Brian Minor; manuscript 1972 and published score 1973. [New York Public Library archive finding aid](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/pdf_finding_aid/muspersi.pdf) (opened).
- Work list reports 4:30 duration and Brian Minor premiere in Kalamazoo, April 14, 1973. [Vincent Persichetti Association work list](https://www.associazionevincentpersichetti.com/pagine.web/italiano/persichetti.htm) (search_index_only).
- Indiana University repertoire resource lists Parable XI for alto saxophone solo. [Indiana University Jacobs School of Music composition resource](https://blogs.iu.edu/jsomcomposition/idiomatic-writing/) (search_index_only).
- Recording track listing includes Parable XI performed by alto saxophonist Brian Minor. [Apple Music Classical](https://classical.music.apple.com/us/album/1672459945) (search_index_only).
- University of North Texas dissertation describes Parable XI as ABA' with changing meters and barlines, except for a three-line cadenza in the middle of B; A sections are slow and lyrical, B is very fast and articulated. It reports a 2.5-octave range, pitch bends, fast vibrato, and approximately ten requested tone colors. These are the dissertation's descriptions, not independent score observations. [University of North Texas doctoral dissertation on Persichetti’s Parables](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc277610/m2/1/high_res_d/1002720499-zoloth.pdf) (printed 71-72 / PDF 84-85; opened).
- Dissertation discography lists three recordings of Parable XI: Brian Minor with Chicago Symphony String Quartet and Lawson Lunde (Crystal S-151), James Stoltie (Coronet LPS-3036), and Dale Underwood (Golden Crest RE-091). The audio was not auditioned. [University of North Texas doctoral dissertation discography](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc277610/m2/1/high_res_d/1002720499-zoloth.pdf) (printed 94 / PDF 107; opened).
- Recording: [Apple Music Classical album listing with Parable XI performed by Brian Minor.](https://classical.music.apple.com/us/album/1672459945) Metadata listing; playback not auditioned.

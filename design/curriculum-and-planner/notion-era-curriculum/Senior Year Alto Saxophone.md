By the time a student reaches their fourth year of study they should have advanced enough in their technique to play most of the saxophone repertoire. The following pieces are merely a suggestion of possible pieces a fourth-year student might pursue. Many other pieces can by found in Fourth-Year Répertoire and Advanced Répertoire.

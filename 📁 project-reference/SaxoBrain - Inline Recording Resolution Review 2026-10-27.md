## Recording Limits to Review

- Alex Shapiro, Of Wind and Touch: verified publisher excerpt. No complete baritone performance was selected.
- Jaromír Weinberger, Concerto: first movement only.
- Pierre-Max DuBois, Dix figures à danser: eight selected dances. The recording omits two dances and renumbers the others. Public labels avoid the misleading movement numbers.
- Béla Bartók, Evening in the Country: verified alto/piano performance; the cataloged edition is Floyd O. Harris’s tenor/piano arrangement.
- Jacques Offenbach, Barcarolle: verified alto/piano performance; the cataloged edition is Calvin Grooms’s tenor/piano arrangement.
- Charles Koechlin cycles: one full album player per work. The labels and public note identify disc 1 tracks 12-35 for the 24 Duos, and disc 2 tracks 16-22 for the Seven Pieces.
- Marc Eychenne, Sonate: verified France Apple release; regional availability can differ. A release numbering error for the third movement is corrected in the player label.
- Performer uploads for Grainger and some student recitals identify the work and saxophone, but the exact published edition is not always credited. Those limits are retained in each work note.
- Apple Music and Spotify players can require sign-in or a subscription for complete playback. Their public metadata verifies the selected tracks; full playback was not auditioned for every subscription service.
- Vincent David: the old Selmer MP3 is only 28.51 seconds. It was replaced with a complete composer-uploaded YouTube performance.
- Kenji Sakai: the former Vimeo page returns matching metadata but its player has access restrictions. A verified Apple Music recording replaces it.

## Scores

The [official Fana Music Publications catalog](https://yuseflateef.com/compositions/) identifies the publisher and links the product pages. Fetched six product pages directly after the web reader failed to retrieve them. Klockology opened in the web reader.

- [Klockology](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/568/Klockology): unaccompanied soprano, alto, tenor, or baritone saxophone. PDF only. Updated the purchase link.
- [Romance](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/687/Romance+for+Harp+%26+Oboe+d%27Amore+or+Harp+%26+Soprano+Saxophone): harp and oboe d'amore or soprano saxophone. Print and PDF editions. Cataloged the soprano saxophone version. The listed 1999 premiere does not establish the composition date. [Noa Even's repertoire repository](https://www.noaevenmusic.com/mixedchamber) gives 1991. The composer-linked Duo Eolienne video description independently gives 1991.
- [Sonata for Saxophone Quartet](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/588/Sonata+for+Saxophone+Quartet): SATB, orchestrated by Rick Hirsch, premiered at Eastman in 1996. The product page lists the score and every part as sold out despite prices in the general catalog. Retained the supplied product link and added a short public availability note.
- [Sonata No. 1](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/614/Sonata+No.+1+for+Alto+Saxophone+and+Piano): alto saxophone and piano, all parts, PDF only. Updated the purchase link. Retained Fourth Year and the existing recording.
- [Sonata No. 2](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/606/Sonata+No.+2+for+Eb+Alto+Saxophone+and+Piano): alto saxophone and piano, PDF only. The store offers a transposed score and separate piano and saxophone parts. Added the purchase link. Retained Fourth Year. Changed its Bandcamp album URL to the specific Sonata No. 2 track.
- [Trio for Malcolm](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/817/Trio+for+Malcolm): soprano saxophone, bass, and piano. Commissioned by Ann Malcolm. PDF score and all-parts options are listed separately. Noa Even's repository gives 1997.
- [Trio in December 1998 Op. 2, No. 2 (Elan Vital)](https://yuseflateef.com/store/index.php?product/page/576/Trio+in+December+1998+Op.+2,+No.+2+(Elan+Vital)): soprano, alto, and baritone saxophones. Print score and individual parts are offered in limited quantities.

Evan Smith's University of Washington dissertation, [Compositional Techniques in the Saxophone Works of Yusef Lateef](https://digital.lib.washington.edu/researchworks/bitstreams/9a6c9aa8-e34e-43ea-bf7d-7c80f6a578ee/download), lists the quartet's composition year as 1996, Trio for Malcolm as 1997, and Trio in December as 1998. Chapter excerpts were available through web search; direct PDF retrieval returned 403. No study levels were inferred from the dissertation.

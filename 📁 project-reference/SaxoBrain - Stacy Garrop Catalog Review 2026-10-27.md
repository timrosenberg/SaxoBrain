# Stacy Garrop Catalog Review

Reviewed 2026-10-27 for Tim’s request to add the saxophone repertoire and estimate difficulty.

## Scope

The [composer’s saxophone catalog](https://garrop.com/Catalog/ChamberEnsembles/saxophone/) contains 20 distinct eligible titles after excluding its Quartet, Quintet, and Saxophone Choir sections. Sixteen titles were missing from SaxoBrain. Fragmented Spirit, Pieces of Sanity, Tantrum, and Wrath already existed and received metadata or listening-link updates. Concertos and their piano concert versions share one record per title, following the existing catalog convention. Related duo and trio arrangements also share their work’s record, with their exact alternatives spelled out in `multi-instruments`.

Composition years follow the composer’s original-work dates. Stubborn as Hell is dated 2011 with its 2016 saxophone arrangement identified in the body. My Dearest Ruth is dated 2013 with its 2020 saxophone arrangement identified in the body. The Solitude of Stars uses 2020 for the duo and trio arrangements. Its source movement from Postcards from Wyoming predates them.

## Listening Checks and Flags

All 20 titles now have at least one listening destination. YouTube’s public oEmbed endpoint returned valid titles and creator metadata for all 28 composer-linked videos selected in this pass. This confirms that metadata is available. Playback in every region and device remains unverified. The existing supplementary Fragmented Spirit recording also returned valid metadata, crediting Jennifer Bill.

- **Hot Flash:** absent from Garrop’s streaming links, but found on Spotify as [Hot Flash, Stacy Garrop and Carrie Koffman](https://open.spotify.com/track/0Ld77xxSxrrnuj1HBkqYl9?utm_source=openai&utm_medium=chatgpt&go=1&nap_web=1&request_id=6609455c-d0c3-4498-aeee-5ac2b455e1d9). Spotify reports it playable and credits both composer and performer. Duration metadata is 7:28.837, while the composer’s catalog gives six minutes. No duration field was added.
- **Noir Vignettes:** composer-supplied Frant Duo video uses cello and piano. The recording label and public note identify that scoring. No tenor-saxophone recording was located in targeted web and Spotify searches. Flag for a tenor recording.
- **Silver Dagger:** composer-supplied Lincoln Trio recording uses violin, cello, and piano. The recording label and public note identify that scoring. No recording of the soprano/tenor/piano edition was located in targeted web and Spotify searches. Flag for that edition. An [Arizona PBS program](https://azpbs.org/arizonaencore/2026/02/saxophone-and-friends-2/) documents a saxophone/cello/piano performance, which is a further instrumentation variant and does not verify a recording of the two-saxophone edition.
- **Slipstream:** Garrop supplies three MP3 movements of the original clarinet, horn, and piano version. All three public audio URLs returned HTTP 200 and `audio/mpeg`. Recording labels and the public note name that scoring. No recording of the soprano/tenor/piano arrangement was located in targeted web and Spotify searches. Flag for that edition. At Tim’s request on 2026-10-27, direct MP3 links now render as inline browser audio players, streaming from Garrop’s site with no automatic preloading.
- **Repair the World:** retained Garrop’s Lincoln Trio recording with its scoring labeled. Also linked the [Arizona State University library’s Paige Walker recital](https://keep.lib.asu.edu/items/202865), which lists the work as track 6 and credits Walker and Hannah Creviston in a saxophone/piano recital. The archive is an additional listening destination; its audio was not auditioned. The archive calls the work 2024, while the composer’s original-work date is 2023. The catalog follows the composer.
- **The Solitude of Stars:** Garrop supplies Robert Young and Polina Khatsko’s soprano/piano video. Its label identifies that arrangement. This does not document recordings for every other arrangement in the same work record.
- **Silver Linings:** retained the composer-linked premiere at its 413-second start. Garrop warns that the piece was revised after that performance. The public note preserves that warning.
- **Tantrum:** replaced the obsolete catalog-page URL with all three composer-linked Ambassador Duo tracks.
- **Pieces of Sanity:** expanded the former movement-I-only link to all five composer-linked Creviston Duo tracks.
- **Phoenix Rising:** linked both composer-supplied movement tracks.
- **Quicksilver:** linked all three piano-version tracks plus the complete wind-ensemble performance, with its 12-second start preserved.

## Edition Checks

- **Krakatoa:** the saxophone-page accordion’s hidden purchase anchor points to 114-41986, the earlier viola material. Used [Presser 114-42425](https://www.presser.com/114-42425-krakatoa.html), which explicitly identifies the alto saxophone concerto and its piano reduction. Garrop’s supplied Lulloff video is the saxophone version. Presser lists 21 minutes while Garrop lists 19; no duration field was added.
- **Slipstream:** Garrop’s heading still says “coming soon,” and its purchase link targets the original clarinet/horn edition 114-42364. Used the currently listed [soprano/tenor/piano edition 114-42473](https://www.presser.com/114-42473-slipstream.html).
- **Archangels:** [Presser’s search](https://www.presser.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=archangels) distinguishes saxophone trio 114-41996 from clarinet trio 114-42378 and flute trio 114-42232. Linked the saxophone edition.
- **Sueños de Flamenco:** [Presser’s search](https://www.presser.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=suenos+de+flamenco+garrop) confirms saxophone/guitar edition 114-41997. Its canonical URL encodes the accented title as `sue-c3-b1os-de-flamenco`.
- **The Solitude of Stars:** included all four saxophone duo/trio scorings described in Garrop’s accordion: soprano/piano, alto/piano, soprano/marimba, and soprano/tenor/piano. [Presser search page 1](https://www.presser.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=solitude+of+stars+garrop) additionally lists flute/tenor/piano 114-42361. [Page 2](https://www.presser.com/catalogsearch/result/?p=2&q=solitude+of+stars+garrop) lists oboe/tenor/piano 114-42467. Included both. Quartet edition 114-42414 is excluded.
- **Repair the World:** Garrop’s ordering options also list soprano saxophone, cello, and piano. Included this alongside the alto/piano version. Excluded the quartet arrangement.
- **Alpenglow:** Garrop lists the wind-ensemble double concerto and a concert version for saxophone, tuba, and piano. Presser additionally lists a [chamber ensemble version, 114-42552](https://www.presser.com/114-42552-alpenglow.html), for the two soloists, piano, timpani, and four percussionists. All three versions are represented in one record.
- **Inkjar works:** Hot Flash and Repair the World link to Garrop’s catalog for the composer’s score-ordering instructions. No email was sent.

## Program Information

The short poem for unseen is reproduced in full. The other six program notes are attributed summaries in blockquotes, with links to the composer’s full text.

## Instrumentation and Recordings

- The composer explicitly identifies one saxophonist doubling soprano and alto in chronoma. The unseen work page and recording describe saxophone (alto and baritone) with double bass; the catalog identifies the saxophone doubling to distinguish the duo from a trio.
- Full percussion setup for hyd(e)r0sion is recorded in its instrumentation property. The chronoma work page lists hi-hat, water gong, three large gongs, large metal can, triangle, six unpitched metal tubes, two tomtoms and 32-inch timpani. Its product page omits the water gong from that list.
- b(locked.orders) includes video by Michiko Saiki. The video requirement is included in the instrumentation display.
- All seven recording URLs were extracted from the composer’s embedded players. YouTube oEmbed metadata verified the titles and Emily Koh as the channel. Playback was not auditioned.
- hyd(e)r0sion is spelled hyd(e)r0tion in the recording title. The catalog uses the spelling on the work and score pages.
- The embedded bridging : isolation recording is the clarinet, violin and piano version by Talea Ensemble. It is explicitly labeled Clarinet version. The composer lists this alternate instrumentation. No saxophone recording was verified.

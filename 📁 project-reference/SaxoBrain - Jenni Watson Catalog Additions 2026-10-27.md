# Jenni Watson Catalog Additions

Checked 2026-10-27 for Tim’s request to add Jenni Watson and all nineteen supplied works, obtain embedded audio and estimate difficulty.

## Sources and Editions

The nineteen supplied shop pages were fetched directly. Eighteen public score samples were visually inspected. Four movement samples were inspected for The Dardanelles. Urban Species had no relevant notation on the suite page; a recommendation image for Bleaching was excluded. Chasing Angels has only a small, low-resolution sample, limiting assessment. No complete recording was auditioned.

Jenni’s demographics and portrait were supplied by Tim. Her [official biography](https://www.jenniwatson.com/about/) independently confirms the UK identity and 1985 birth year. No race inference was made from the portrait.

7 Hours credits Erin Royer. The Dardanelles credits Martin Jacoby. Around Sound’s shop and score sample name Watson and Royer, while the [official premiere program](https://www.zagrebsaxcongress.com/index.php/participants/artists/item/325-erin-royer-jenni-watson-sax-duo) additionally names Martin Jacoby. All three premiere credits are retained for Around Sound. Minimal composer records preserve those relationships.

The requested saxophone-duo edition of Gone Tomorrow links recordings of the original alto saxophone/horn version. Both listening entries identify that version. Enveloped uses the original 2021 composition date and identifies its 2024 duo edition separately. Appalachian Visions retains its changing instrumentation across the four movements.

## Audio and Purchase Links

Each work has at least one listening source taken from its shop page. Preferred full performances are used where available. Direct MP3 players are retained for Adjusting Parameters, Cloud Cover on Erie, Enniscorthy, Meliai, The Dardanelles, On the Wall, Urban Species, Around Sound and Enveloped. Movement titles distinguish suite excerpts and tracks. MIDI demos and the horn version are labeled only where the distinction matters. Other streaming entries use plain URLs.

Chasing Angels uses Astute Music’s actual score page, rather than the composer shop’s zero-price link-only PDF. All purchase links use the normal Buy the score label.

Direct MP3 URLs remain hosted by the composer. Audio is not copied into the repository. Full listening was not performed. YouTube metadata and SoundCloud oEmbed were checked independently. Verification of the selected MP3 endpoints and rendered pages is recorded below after the build.

## Date Handling

Only explicit composition dates were populated. Copyright dates, premiere years and album release years remain evidence in individual private notes. They were not silently converted into composition dates. On the Wall’s page and sample disagree about duration, so duration was omitted.

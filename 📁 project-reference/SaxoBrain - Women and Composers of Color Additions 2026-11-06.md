# Women and Composers of Color: Approved Catalog Additions

Checked November 6, 2026.

Tim approved all ten shortlisted works, including Fronds and BIG Talk. All composer profiles already existed. Their demographic fields were reused without changes. Year of Study is unassigned for every addition.

Public work notes contain metadata only. Research and source qualifications remain here.

## Catherine Likhuta: Snapshots

- The [composer's woodwind catalog](https://www.catherinelikhuta.com/Woodwinds.htm) identifies the tenor saxophone and piano version as a 2014 transcription of the 2012 horn work. It offers the PDF score directly.
- The composer links [Emma Di Marco's performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjJIISgJxKk). YouTube oEmbed confirms the title and performer channel.
- A duration has not been assigned from the horn version or recording length.

## Jennifer Higdon: Yes, No, Maybe?

- [Navona Records' Second Flight page](https://www.navonarecords.com/catalog/nv6634/) identifies the 2021 work, alto saxophone and piano, and its commission for the 2022 North American Saxophone Alliance collegiate competition finals.
- [Ficks Music](https://www.ficksmusic.com/collections/jennifer-higdon/products/higdon-yes-no-maybe-lawdon) lists the Lawdon Press edition.
- [The competition guidelines](https://www.saxophonealliance.org/assets/2022/NASA-2022-Saxophone-Collegiate-Solo-Competition-Guidelines-v2.pdf) give an approximate duration of 7 minutes 30 seconds.
- The label links Apple Music album 1746722834. Apple's public lookup endpoint identifies [track 1746722960](https://music.apple.com/us/album/yes-no-maybe/1746722834?i=1746722960) as this work, performed by Dan Goble and Russell Hirshfield. Retained the individual track rather than an unrelated full-album player.
- No independently verified individual YouTube video was found in this pass.

## Catherine McMichael: Michigama Suite

- [C. Alan Publications' printed edition](https://c-alanpublications.com/michigama-suite/) and [digital edition](https://c-alanpublications.com/michigama-suite-digital/) supply score access, duration 11:05, and instrumentation.
- The publisher's score sample explicitly says saxophone (soprano and alto) and piano. Steel Blue uses soprano; Chrome and Forest Green use alto.
- The composer's general works list also mentions sopranino. The catalog entry describes the specific publisher edition inspected here.
- Copyright 2006 appears on the sample. Composition year remains unknown, so no composed field was added.
- The [publisher's SoundCloud playlist](https://soundcloud.com/c-alanpublications/sets/michigama-suite) contains all three movements in order: Steel Blue, Chrome, Forest Green. Playlist metadata and oEmbed identify C. Alan Publications.
- The publisher-linked [YouTube performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=svD1hgmhAn8) covers only movements I and II. The complete SoundCloud playlist was chosen.

## Libby Larsen: Bid Call

- The [composer's work page](https://libbylarsen.com/works/bid-call/) gives 2003, approximately 13 minutes, and alto saxophone with cello. It states that an alternate viola part is available from Libby Larsen Publishing.
- The work page links the [Oxford University Press score sold by Sheet Music Plus](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/bid-call-22829079.html).
- Retained the three Bro-Fowler Duo recordings in order from the composer's page. Direct MP3 files are hosted on assets.ctfassets.net.
- The composer also hosts three recordings of the viola version. Those duplicate performances were omitted.
- The page gives a November 2002 premiere alongside its 2003 year-written field. The entry follows the composer's explicit year-written field.
- The multi-instruments property distinguishes the cello version and alternate viola part.

## Viet Cuong: Prized Possessions

- The [composer's page](https://vietcuongmusic.com/prized-possessions/) confirms SATB quartet, approximately 12 minutes, and the 2015 premiere.
- [Murphy Music Press](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/q-153) supplies the score.
- The [composer's SoundCloud playlist](https://soundcloud.com/vietcuong/sets/prized-possessions) contains both movements: Mother's Monster and Beggar's Lace.
- SoundCloud oEmbed identifies the composer and a PRISM Quartet performance at Symphony Space on May 23, 2015.

## Libby Larsen: Confluence

- The [composer's page](https://libbylarsen.com/works/confluence/) gives 2014, approximately 14 minutes, four movements, score preview, and recordings by the Ancia Saxophone Quartet.
- The composer's Get this score link points to a nonworking preview-site contact route.
- [Ficks Music's Libby Larsen Publishing edition](https://www.ficksmusic.com/collections/libby-larsen/products/larsen-confluence-libby-larsen) is used instead. It confirms SATB instrumentation.
- Retained the four direct MP3 recordings from the composer's page in source order. Movement numbers distinguish the players without adding descriptive public copy.
- The [Ancia album](https://www.albanyrecords.com/catalog/troy1919/) also documents the recording.

## Chen Yi: Not Alone

- The [published edition](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/not-alone-20470288.html) confirms quartet instrumentation and a duration of approximately 14 minutes.
- [PRISM Quartet's Paradigm Lost page](https://www.prismquartet.com/recording/paradigm-lost/) dates the work to 2014 and identifies the recording and players.
- Used the [individual Bandcamp track](https://prismquartet.bandcamp.com/track/not-alone), linked from PRISM's album source, rather than the whole album.
- The score edition also includes Happy Birthday to PRISM. That separate work was not added under this approval.
- The publisher's description confirms Not Alone can be performed independently of the dance production.

## Evan Williams: Quartet for Saxophones

- The [composer's work list](https://www.evanwilliamsmusic.info/works.html) gives 2014 and approximately 16 minutes.
- The [work page](https://www.evanwilliamsmusic.info/quartet-for-saxophones.html) describes seven movements and identifies the Hocta Quartet's soprano, alto, tenor, and baritone players.
- [Murphy Music Press](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/q-143) supplies the score.
- The composer embeds [the score-following recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kBeHqup1zD8). YouTube oEmbed confirms the title and incipitsify channel.
- The [Coalescent Quartet album](https://coalescentquartet.bandcamp.com/album/the-wall-between-us-is-where-we-meet) supplies an independently documented complete seven-movement recording. No duplicate player was added.

## Adrienne Albert: Fronds

- The [composer's solo catalog](https://www.adriennealbert.com/solos) confirms unaccompanied baritone saxophone and Wayne Leechford's commission.
- Its published page data identifies the work's duration as 5 minutes and the audio sample as 78 seconds. The Fronds audio is distinct from the adjacent cello transcription, Fluttering Fronds.
- Resolved the Buy Now button's embedded product URL to [Fronds (2012-2013)](https://www.adriennealbert.com/product-page/fronds-2012-2013). The product page returned successfully and advertises the score as in stock.
- Used the title's 2012-2013 date range rather than treating the webpage's creation date as a composition date.
- Included the composer's direct MP3 with an explicit Excerpt label. A complete baritone recording remains unverified.

## Shelley Washington: BIG Talk

- The [composer's chamber list](https://www.shelleywashington.com/chamber-music) identifies two baritone saxophones.
- Resolved a current [PSNY score page](https://www.eamdc.com/psny/composers/shelley-washington/works/big-talk/), which offers print and digital performance scores and says two copies are required for performance.
- The publisher gives 2017 and approximately 11 minutes. The composer's chamber list gives 2016. Composition year is left unset pending resolution of that discrepancy.
- The publisher embeds [the score-following recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P7WqLWWLe50). YouTube oEmbed confirms Shelley Washington, BIG Talk, and the incipitsify channel.
- The [composer's Bandcamp release](https://shelleywashington.bandcamp.com/album/big-talk) independently documents the full recording and performers.
- Used the verified publisher score route and YouTube recording instead of the outdated score-bundle claim on the composer's older music page.

# SaxoBrain - Nancy Van de Vate Catalog Additions

Verified October 27, 2026. Tim assigned Third Year to both works.

## Quiet Piece for Solo Saxophone

- [American Composers Alliance listing](https://composers.com/products/van-de-vate-quiet-piece-for-solo-saxophone?variant=45598459691228), inspected in the browser: authored or revised 2009, four minutes, score ACA-VANN-008. Physical and digital editions are offered. Preserved the supplied physical-edition variant link.
- [mica – music austria's saxophone work record](https://db.musicaustria.at/node/171542) specifies unaccompanied alto saxophone and 2009. ACA's title is retained in the catalog; mica calls the work A Quiet Piece.
- [mica's separate 2001 work record](https://db.musicaustria.at/node/152066) describes English horn and piano. It does not establish a 2001 composition date for the solo saxophone work.

## Suite for Alto Saxophone

- [American Composers Alliance listing](https://composers.com/products/van-de-vate-suite-for-alto-saxophone), inspected in the browser: alto saxophone alone, authored or revised 2009, eight minutes, score ACA-VANN-010. Physical score offered.
- [Score sample hosted by mica](https://db.musicaustria.at/sites/default/files/upload/node/170982/edit/Suite_For_Alto_Saxophone_sample.pdf) has a 2009 Vienna Masterworks copyright. [mica's composer record](https://db.musicaustria.at/en/node/66568) also records a 2009 premiere by Bill Perconti, although its works index gives 2010. Used ACA's 2009 date, supported by the score copyright and premiere record.

## Composer

- [American Composers Alliance profile](https://composers.com/collections/nancy-van-de-vate) identifies her as American and gives 1930–2023.

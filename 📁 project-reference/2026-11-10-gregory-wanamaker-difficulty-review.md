## Catalog And Recording Checks

The 27 new entries use the composer’s [contact form](https://gregorywanamaker.com/contact/) for score requests, except for the unavailable work below. The form’s FAQ confirms that purchasers should request availability and price there. No inquiry or purchase was submitted.

Composition years follow the repository’s original-work convention for arrangements. The composer’s [full catalog](https://gregorywanamaker.com/catalogue/full-catalogue/) and [saxophone catalog](https://gregorywanamaker.com/catalogue/music-with-saxophone/) supply years where individual work pages omit them. Public work bodies contain only practical version, equipment, or availability information.

Nineteen new entries have recording links: eighteen use YouTube and Monologue uses the composer’s SoundCloud recording. Duo Sonata retains its existing recording. Still Moving uses an ordered two-video queue. Video titles, descriptions, public availability, and runtimes were checked with yt-dlp. SoundCloud metadata was checked through its oEmbed endpoint. Playback was not auditioned.

No matching recording was verified for BariSuite, Progsody in C Minus, Desolation, Vigil, Slink, Meditation and Dance for alto saxophone and harp, Tarantasia, or still life is life still. Each available work without a video received an initial and a refined YouTube search, plus web searches for the unresolved titles. The unavailable orchestral work was retained with no recording or purchase link.

## Recording Exceptions

- **Night Set:** The composer’s three Younglove/Ames YouTube track IDs currently report unavailable. The catalog uses the complete [Stephen Page and Alex Maynegre performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4QLzp7QxRM4) from the same work page. The isolated Night Song recording was omitted to keep one complete player.
- **Run:** Replaced the work page’s short promotional excerpt with the complete [Capitol Quartet album track](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXzI1OyXZQw).
- **Meditation and Dance:** The located [composer-posted video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zF940fvSUg4) explicitly identifies sopranino saxophone and piano. It belongs to the [separate adaptation](https://gregorywanamaker.com/works/meddancesop/). It was not attached to the requested alto saxophone and harp entry.
- **Elegy:** Used the [Iridium Quartet album track](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rsu9KDNkRq4), identified by the composer’s page as a saxophone quartet recording. Reed-quintet and string-quartet recordings found in searches were excluded. The composer’s embedded SoundCloud recording identifies a 2013 Donald Sinta Quartet performance, which conflicts with the work page’s 2016 premiere claim.
- **des ondes et les temps:** The linked [Casey Grev recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPN40yTnkZ0) is identified as tenor saxophone in the composer’s album notes. Approximately six minutes is supported by the composer-hosted [2019 repertoire profile](https://gregorywanamaker.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Meet-the-Composer_Gregory-Wanamaker.pdf).
- **Monologue:** The [canonical SoundCloud link](https://soundcloud.com/gregory-wanamaker/monologue) resolves to the same Arno Bornkamp recording embedded by the composer. It is distinct from Monology.
- **Still Moving:** [Still](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3vSVZ8h8vdM) precedes [Moving](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vo81sPn4Bx4) in the queue. Both uploads identify Wanamaker and the movement number. The descriptions give only the performers’ first names, Heather and Anna.

## Recording Checks

Bonneau: Nathan Nabb official studio video. Jolas: uploader description identifies Geoffrey Deibel on tenor saxophone. Turnage: Geoffrey Landman upload describes both movements. Quate: Andorra Sax Fest official competition upload. Still: Robert Young description explicitly identifies alto saxophone and piano,1954. Havel: Proxima Centauri upload names Marie-Bernadette Charrier. Scelsi: Kevin Baldwin performer page explicitly identifies soprano saxophone and complete movements I–III; MP3 returned206 audio/mpeg.

Rejected the repository’s Takemitsu recording because it is Heinz Holliger on oboe. Rejected partial Piazzolla movements as complete-work recordings. Existing Rueff body already links Wonki Lee’s three movements in order; all three titles and performer descriptions were verified and preserved.

## Date Conflicts Left Open

Noa’s Holy Roller date1998 is premiere year; composer gives1997,used in the blank date field. Sequenza VIIb source1969 is original oboe work; publisher identifies saxophone arrangement1993,used. Sequenza IXb sources disagree between original1980 and adaptation1981; its date remains blank. Gotkovsky dates and several transcription dates were left blank rather than transferring ambiguous original,orchestration or publication dates.

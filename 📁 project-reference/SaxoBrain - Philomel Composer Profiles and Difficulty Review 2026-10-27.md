## Approved Difficulty Ratings

The scale uses SaxoBrain’s existing study-year categories. These are judgments about likely preparation, not externally certified grades. Existing anchors include Bozza’s Aria and Rueff’s Chanson et Passepied at First Year, Maurice’s Tableaux de Provence at Second Year, Creston’s Sonata and Glazunov’s Concerto at Third Year, and Bond’s Notes from the Underground at Fourth Year.

High confidence means strong work-specific evidence for the broad placement. Moderate confidence means useful evidence with incomplete notation coverage. Low confidence means the exact saxophone part or enough technical evidence was unavailable. Low-confidence proposals should be reviewed first.

## Difficulty Evidence and Limits

The placements began as provisional research estimates. Tim approved all twenty-five on 2026-10-27. The edition matters. Claims specific to clarinet, oboe, or viola were not transferred to a saxophone version without qualification. Ensemble grades were not converted into solo grades. An ABRSM diploma listing provides comparative context and is not a direct conversion to a university year.

### Kati Agócs: As Biddeth Thy Tongue

**Advanced**, high confidence.

Irregular tuplets, frequent meter changes, exposed high-register writing, and detailed color and dynamic control.

Evidence inspected: Visually inspected the composer-hosted first page of the transposed alto score, edited August 2019. It shows 7/8, 5/8, 6/8, 3/8 and changing simple meters, ratios including 5:3 and 7:6, high-register phrases, and a three-timbre instruction. The composer describes a virtuosic nine-minute vocal-style fantasy.

Limitations: Only the first score page was inspected. The placement remains an editorial estimate, not a publisher grade.

### Camila Agosto: Paracusia

**Advanced**, low confidence.

An eleven-minute interactive electroacoustic solo with a sustained theatrical role. Specialist preparation is likely.

Evidence inspected: The composer’s individual work page specifies solo alto saxophone with live electronics, eleven minutes, and a staged relationship between the player and electronic voices. The project description treats the work as a standalone part of a larger collaboration.

Limitations: No score was available. This is an estimate of preparation and interaction demands. The evidence does not establish specific finger technique, range, or a need for particular extended techniques. A prepared electronics operator could reduce the player’s setup burden. ### Chaya Czernowin: The Last Leaf

**Advanced**, high confidence.

Quarter-tones, timbral trills, soft slap tongue, and very precise sopranino tone and pitch control.

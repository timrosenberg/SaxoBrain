# Hans Kox: Saxophone Quartet No. 2

Added at Tim's request on 2026-11-24 with his Advanced rating.

- [Donemus, catalogue no. 03437](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/3437): Saxophone quartet no. 2, composed in 1987 and revised in 1988; four saxophones; approximately 17 minutes; dedicated to the Raschèr Saxophone Quartet. The numeric `composed` field uses the revised version's year, 1988.
- [Hans Kox Foundation, chamber music catalogue](https://www.hanskox.nl/chamber-music.html): independently confirms the 1987 work, 1988 revision, and 17-minute duration.
- [Kohinoor Saxophone Quartet, Saxophone quartets from the Netherlands](https://kohinoorsaxophonequartet.bandcamp.com/album/saxophone-quartets-from-the-netherlands): tracks 8 through 12 are the complete Kox work, dated 1988 in the ensemble's notes. The four published movements occupy five tracks because Allegro martellato and Intermezzo are separate tracks.

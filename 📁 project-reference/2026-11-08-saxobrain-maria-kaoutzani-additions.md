# Maria Kaoutzani Catalog Additions

Added 2026-11-08 at Tim’s request. All three works are Fourth Year, as specified by Tim.

## Composer

- The composer’s [official biography](https://www.mariakaoutzani.com/about) identifies her as from Limassol and describes her Cypriot cultural background. Added `🇨🇾 Cypriot` after checking the catalog for existing Cypriot or Cyprus labels. Her biography uses she/her pronouns; gender recorded as Female.
- The [2018 Society of Composers, Inc. Student National Conference program](https://www.navidbargrizan.com/uploads/5/4/8/1/54814981/sci-2018-program.pdf), printed pages 21 and 23, gives her birth year as 1993.
- The portrait graphic was copied from the [official biography page](https://www.mariakaoutzani.com/about): [source image](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/c8bf6b_c1d1348c04c24023895602620410dd68~mv2.png). No photographer credit was verified, so the caption credits the website.
- Race left empty because no explicit self-identification was verified.

## Recordings

- Hydra: [UChicago Arts exhibition video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tWSCyUNrchQ), approximately 2:21. The title names the composer and work. The description identifies the 2021 Maps of Form exhibition. Labeled “Exhibition video” because the description does not establish that this is a full performance. No duration is assigned to the work from this video.
- Count Me In: [~Nois recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-A5k_XDgYQ), approximately 7:51. The official YouTube metadata credits ~Nois and Maria Kaoutzani, with a Bright Shiny Things release date of 2024-02-23.
- Shore to Shore: [~Nois recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KKkuncFjM8U), approximately 9:51. The official YouTube metadata credits ~Nois and Maria Kaoutzani and identifies the album Kinds of ~Nois, released 2024-03-15.

YouTube titles, descriptions, channels, and durations were checked with yt-dlp. Recordings were not auditioned. The approximate work durations follow the ensemble’s program rather than recording runtimes.

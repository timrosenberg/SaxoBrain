# SaxoBrain - Evan Williams Breathe Addition

Verified November 6, 2026. Added at Advanced, as requested by Tim.

- [Composer's catalog](https://www.evanwilliamsmusic.info/works.html) lists Breathe (2016) for alto saxophone and drum set, with a duration of six minutes.
- [Composer's work page](https://www.evanwilliamsmusic.info/breathe.html), inspected in the browser, identifies the commissioning duo Patchwork and performers Noa Even (saxophone) and Stephen Klunk (drum set). It links to the physical score at Murphy Music Press and a digital edition at Adjective New Music.
- [Publisher's product page](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/c-162), inspected in the browser, confirms Evan Williams and alto saxophone with drum set. Added this purchase link with the default button text.
- [Bowling Green State University's media page](https://www.bgsu.edu/musical-arts/maccm/media) embeds [Evan Williams - Breathe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKtvF-Wq_rc) under the 2020 New Music Festival. Its [festival program](https://www.bgsu.edu/content/dam/BGSU/musical-arts/documents/maccm/NMF41.pdf) identifies Christopher Murphy (saxophone) and Nick Fox (percussion). Added the YouTube link without a custom title. Playback was not auditioned; YouTube embeds returned connection errors in this browser session.
- The composer's work page also has a current [SoundCloud recording](https://soundcloud.com/evan-williams-16/breathe). The publisher's older SoundCloud embed reports that its track cannot be found. Retained the current composer-hosted link here as an alternative.
- Reused the existing Evan Williams composer profile. Public body text was left empty.

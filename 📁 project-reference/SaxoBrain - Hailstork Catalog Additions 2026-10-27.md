# SaxoBrain - Hailstork Catalog Additions

Verified October 27, 2026. Tim assigned Fourth Year to the concerto and Third Year to the other three works.

## Concerto for Alto Saxophone and Strings

- [Penders listing](https://www.penders.com/p-1143036-concerto-for-alto-saxophone-and-strings.aspx), inspected in the browser: Theodore Presser 116-42223L, 80-page large score, solo alto saxophone with violin I, violin II, viola, cello and double bass. The supplied purchase link is labeled as a large score to distinguish its format.
- [Timothy McAllister's concerto page](https://timothymcallister.com/concerto-offerings/adolphus-hailstork/): identifies the composition as 2024 and describes the February 2, 2025 world premiere. No piano reduction was verified.

## Siciliana

- [Ficks Music listing](https://www.ficksmusic.com/products/hailstork-siciliana-arr-for-saxophone-presser), inspected in the browser: Theodore Presser 114-42497, six pages, unaccompanied saxophone version of an English horn work. No saxophone voice or arranger is specified.

## Griot Song

- [Ficks Music listing](https://www.ficksmusic.com/products/hailstork-griot-song-presser), inspected in the browser: Theodore Presser 114-42310, alto saxophone and marimba. Printed and digital parts are offered.
- [Publisher's March 2026 releases](https://www.presser.com/2026-march-new-releases) confirm the instrumentation and catalog number. Publication date was not treated as composition date.

## Eleven Studies

- [Ficks Music listing](https://www.ficksmusic.com/products/hailstork-eleven-studies-arr-for-saxophone-presser), inspected in the browser: Theodore Presser 114-42496, saxophone version of 11 Studies for Oboe. No saxophone voice or arranger is specified. Removed the tracking query from the supplied URL.

## Composer

- [Theodore Presser composer profile](https://www.presser.com/adolphus-hailstork) gives April 17, 1941 as the birth date.
- [Theodore Presser's Black composers feature](https://www.presser.com/12-amazing-black-composers) identifies Hailstork among its American composers and supplies biographical context.

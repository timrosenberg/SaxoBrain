# Tonia Ko Catalog Additions

Added Tonia Ko, Flock, and the soprano saxophone version of Highwire on 2026-11-08. Tim requested Advanced for both works.

## Composer

- [Composer biography](https://toniako.com/biography): composer born in Hong Kong and raised in Honolulu, Hawai‘i; uses she/her pronouns.
- [Composers Edition biography](https://composersedition.com/toniako/): born in 1988.
- [Huddersfield Contemporary Music Festival composer programme](https://hcmf.co.uk/programme/zeitgeist-commissions-tonia-ko/): identifies Ko as USA / Hong Kong, 1988. Reused the existing American and Hongkonger nationality labels.
- Race left empty because these sources do not establish documented self-identification.

## Flock

- [Publisher listing](https://composersedition.com/tonia-ko-flock/): 2019, approximately 10 minutes, soprano/alto/tenor/baritone quartet and electronics; catalogue ID ce-tk1f1. Additional media are supplied after purchase.
- [Composer's YouTube recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=btZwvuvdP1I): embedded by the publisher and titled Flock for saxophone quartet (2019) by Tonia Ko. Linked as the work's recording. Playback was not auditioned.
- [Composer's full catalogue](https://toniako.com/catalog): instead lists 2018 and 9 minutes. Retained the publisher's 2019 and approximate duration because the composer's own recording also identifies the work as 2019. The discrepancy remains documented here.

## Highwire

- [Publisher's soprano saxophone version](https://composersedition.com/highwire-soprano-saxophone-version/): soprano saxophone and electronics, approximately 10 minutes, 2017/2020, catalogue ID ce-tk1h2. Created with Sarah Hetrick and additional input from Joel Ferst. Additional media are required.
- [Composer's full catalogue](https://toniako.com/catalog): identifies the original oboe version as 2017 and the saxophone version as 2017/24. The adaptation dates differ across sources, so the edition field does not assert a revision year. Composed remains numeric 2017 under the repository convention of retaining an arrangement's original composition year.
- The publisher embeds an oboe performance by Olivier Stankiewicz. It was omitted from the saxophone work's recording field. No matching saxophone recording was verified during this addition.

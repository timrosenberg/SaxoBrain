### ID 21 Marilyn Shrude Kantada

- Composer work page confirms 2004, alto saxophone and piano, commission by John Sampen, and publication by contacting composer mshrude@bgsu.edu. Supplied ACA attribution could not be verified in current publisher catalog. Work page offers linked MP3 EXCERPT and PDF score EXCERPT. The recording URL must be labeled as an excerpt. [Source](https://www.marilynshrude.com/works/kantada/).
- Composer full worklist instead estimates 2 minutes. Individual work page estimates ca. 3 minutes, so duration is approximate. [Source](https://www.marilynshrude.com/works-full/).

### ID 48 Gunther Schuller Sonata

- Current publisher confirms 1999 alto saxophone and piano Sonata, duration 11 minutes, printed score and digital purchase options. Source list AMP is superseded by verified current RCEditions listing. [Source](https://www.rceditions.com/item-details/124/sonata).
- Schuller Society corroborates 1999 instrumentation and RCEditions, and links the selected recording. Society estimates duration 12:30. [Source](https://www.guntherschullersociety.org/100-pieces-list).
- Direct YouTube player metadata verified status OK, title and description identify Schuller Sonata played by Juan Alzate, alto saxophone, and Heriberto Cruz, piano, at Conservatorio de Las Rosas, Morelia. Complete video 12:18. [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SNe6hiTUohA).

### ID 76 Bernard Rands Memo 6

- Publisher confirms solo alto saxophone, composition year 1998, 9 minutes, catalog EA 798, print and purchasable PDF. Some secondary references incorrectly date 1999; prefer publisher. [Source](https://www.schott-music.com/en/memo-6-noc231664.html).
- EAM publisher notice independently confirms Memo 6 (1998), solo alto saxophone, 9 minutes. [Source](https://www.eamdc.com/news/works-by-bernard-rands-available-in-digital-format-for-the-first-time/).
- Direct YouTube player metadata verified status OK. Official John Sampen Topic upload supplied by Orchard: Memo 6 by Bernard Rands, John Sampen, Visions in Metaphor, Albany Records, duration 9:53. [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iFJQ_1c9Cfc).

### ID 106 Edward J. Miller Fantasy-Concerto

- ACA identifies Edward J. Miller, Fantasy-Concerto, 1971, 10 minutes, for Don Sinta, alto saxophone and band. Conductor score for sale; instrumental parts available on rental from same page. Detailed band scoring: 2 flutes, 2 oboes, 2 clarinets, 2 bassoons, 2 C trumpets, 2 horns, trombone, bass trombone, baritone horn/euphonium, tuba. No piano reduction verified. [Source](https://composers.com/products/millerej-fantasy-concerto).
- Publisher biography confirms composer identity, 1930-2013, born Miami, Florida, August 4, 1930; Oberlin composition faculty 1971-1998. [Source](https://composers.com/collections/edward-j-miller).
- Family obituary confirms dates August 4, 1930 to August 31, 2013. [Source](https://www.getzfuneralhome.com/obituaries/edward-j-miller).
- Commercial recording exists: Howie Smith with Oberlin Wind Ensemble, Robert Ponto, Opus One CD 154. No public streaming verified. [Source](https://nwr-site-liner-notes.s3.amazonaws.com/80511.pdf).
- Foundation fellowship source identifies Edward J. Miller (USA), Oberlin College. [Source](https://www.rockefellerfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-1993-1.pdf).

### ID 121 Henry Brant Concerto for Alto Saxophone and Orchestra

- Direct live publisher HTML verified W2659, Henry Brant composer, Liz/Elizabeth Ames arranger, Noah Getz foreword. Alto saxophone and piano reduction. Publisher description confirms composed 1941 for Sigurd Rascher; critical piano reduction available print and digital. Distinguish separate nine-instrument/trumpet-alternative adaptation O5319. [Source](https://www.carlfischer.com/w2659-concerto-for-alto-sax-and-orchestra.html).
- Soloist who studied concerto with Brant confirms full orchestra and 2014 Ames piano reduction. Orchestral scoring listed as 2d1.2.2dBass.2/3.2/timp,3perc,pno/strings; duration 21 minutes. [Source](https://www.noahgetz.com/brant-concerto).
- Direct YouTube player metadata verified status OK. Thomas A. Giles upload identifies concerto (1941), Giles with Brooklyn Symphony Orchestra conducted Nicholas Armstrong, Brooklyn Museum April 22, 2018. Complete 21:49 recording. [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jJx5LVPCxQ8).
- Estate biography confirms 1913-2008 and American composer born Montreal to American parents. Use birth year only: Carl Fischer composer page has an apparent September 5 date typo. [Source](https://henrybrant.com/biography/).
- Estate confirms Carl Fischer/Theodore Presser as primary publisher. [Source](https://henrybrant.com/publishers/).

## Research And Source Rules

Composer and publisher work pages take precedence over the repository when they disagree. Purchase links point to verified editions or explicitly labelled score-request routes. Ceremonias has a verified free institutional score download. Audio samples and incomplete movements are labelled. Matching titles alone were insufficient to establish saxophone scoring. Unknown score and recording links remain blank.

Public work bodies contain only practical version or performance information. Research evidence stays in this folder. Minimal composer records do not infer demographic information from names or photographs.

The 64 approximate durations added centrally to existing entries are attributed to Noa Even’s performance estimates unless an independently checked composer or publisher source replaces them. Uncertain arrangement or publication dates remain blank. Complete collections were not assigned dates or durations from a single component.

## Important Corrections

- Chasing Shadows uses the composer’s current name Chia-Yu Hsu. Vocalise II uses James Díaz. Notwithstanding uses Laura M. Kramer.
- Prayer (T’filah) has separately published soprano, alto, tenor and baritone versions. Alto dates from 2009. The other saxophone versions date from 2020.
- Longevity is dated 2013 by the composer. The repository lists 2014.
- Arc is for baritone saxophone, not unrestricted saxophone. Floating Underwater is a four-piece series originally for birbynė and suitable for soprano saxophone.
- Second Flight is dated 2017 by its publisher. The repository lists 1981.
- For Evelyn is verified by its composer for baritone saxophone.
- The repository’s Arianna URL points to Desnudo. A matching Arianna performance was found in another verified video.
- The repository’s Rock Steady URL points to Shelley Washington’s profile. A matching Ryan Muncy recording was found.
- The repository’s Grisey video is the contrabass-clarinet version. The Sampen import supplied a matching saxophone recording, which was retained.
- Lacrimosa previously pointed to a Caritas recording. Renewing the Myth previously pointed to Visions in Metaphor. Both now have verified replacements.
- Four score requests for the complete Floating Underwater series were not verified. Only its three verified SAMRO movement request pages were added.
- Ambiguous Fournier and Rossé composition dates remain blank. The current Lacy Melodia product is not used as a saxophone score because its edition could not be established.

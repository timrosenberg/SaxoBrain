# SaxoBrain - Dana Wilson Catalog Additions

Verified November 7, 2026. Tim requested Wilson's other music for the saxophone database and hotlinked recordings where available. Added nine distinct works. The catalog now contains fourteen Dana Wilson work pages. New works have no assigned Year of Study.

## Scope and Sources

- [Wilson's saxophone catalog](https://www.danawilson.org/featured_instruments?instrument=Sax) supplied titles, dates, versions, and recording links.
- [Wilson's chamber catalog](https://www.danawilson.org/chamber_music) also lists Sati, which is omitted from his saxophone filter.
- Inspected the current catalogs and ordering pages in the browser because several pages were unavailable to the web fetcher.
- Included works featuring saxophone. Ordinary band repertoire with saxophones in the ensemble was outside this import.

## Editorial and Verification Notes

- Liquid Gold uses composed: 2002 for the piano work. Its separate 2005 wind ensemble version is recorded in arranger-edition. These are edition dates, rather than a composition date range.
- The Liquid Gold band listing refers to a clarinet recording of Liquid Ebony. That recording set was omitted to avoid presenting it as soprano saxophone.
- Added eighteen MP3 links across ten work pages. Single recordings use the default player title. Multiple movements and accompaniment alternatives have distinguishing labels.
- All MP3 URLs were copied from the composer's current page links and retained on his server. No audio files were downloaded into the repository.
- Playback remains unverified. In the preceding Calling, Ever Calling work, direct automated MP3 requests returned HTTP 406 from the host's security module. Browser navigation to MP3 was blocked by the browser client. The catalog links themselves were verified in the current browser view.
- New public work notes contain metadata only. Research stays in this project-reference note.

## Movement Recording Labels

On November 7, 2026, Tim requested movement titles above the players, following the existing Calling, Ever Calling format: Roman numeral, colon, and movement title. Updated twelve labels across five works without changing recording URLs.

| Work | Movement labels | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breathing the Water | I: Mountain Lake; II: Torrents | Duo Montagnard's artist-issued [Interspheres track listing](https://music.amazon.es/albums/B00PJWMTLM). Apple Music's catalog independently identifies Mountain Lake as a Dana Wilson composition. Wilson's [program note](https://www.danawilson.org/notes/101820) describes the lake and torrents. |
| Come Sunday Mornin’ | I: Callin' Out; II: Callin' Back | The [Dorn edition at Groth](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-139717-come-sunday-mornin-sax-quartet-satb.aspx) lists both names. They were already present in the note; standardized their punctuation to match Tim's example. |
| Howling at the Moon | I: Wailing; II: Singing; III: Conjuring | The [Fischoff competition's 2003 program](https://www.fischoff.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2003-Competition-Program-Book.pdf) lists these in order for the New Heritage Saxophone Quartet. Wilson's [program note](https://www.danawilson.org/notes/167) corroborates the three movement concepts. |
| Liquid Gold | I: Call; II: Prayer; III: Dance of Not Pretending | Apple Music's catalog lists all three titles for Liquid Ebony on Jana Starling and Gail Novak's Mythos. The [2025 Orford festival program](https://www.orford.mu/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/BCR-24-juillet-2025-16h30-1.pdf) independently confirms II and III. Wilson's [note for Liquid Gold](https://www.danawilson.org/notes/179) identifies it as the soprano saxophone version of Liquid Ebony. Applied that source work's movement names to the transcription. |
| We Sing to Each Other | I: Of beauty and sadness; II: Of darkness and joy | UNT's [March 5, 2001 Jeremy Preston Phillips doctoral recital record](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc177042/) lists both tracks in order. The indexed institutional record supplied the titles; direct browser access encountered the site's request-validation page. |

The remaining recordings are single tracks or alternative instrumentation recordings without separately verified movement names. Luminescence retains its two accompaniment labels. Calling, Ever Calling already had the requested labels and was used as the rendered reference.

Verified all twelve updated movement headings on the five localhost pages. The minified Hugo build passed. A before/after comparison confirmed that the work-note edits changed only streaming labels, preserving every recording URL and all other fields. Changes remain uncommitted.

## Movement Label Punctuation

Tim subsequently chose periods after Roman numerals. The current format is `I. Movement title`. Changed all fifteen movement labels across the six Wilson works, including Calling, Ever Calling, to this format.

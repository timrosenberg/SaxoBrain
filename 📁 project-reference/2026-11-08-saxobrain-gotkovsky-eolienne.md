# Ida Gotkovsky: Eolienne

Added at Tim’s request on 2026-11-08, with Third Year assigned by Tim.

- The [composer’s official chamber music catalog](https://www.gotkovsky.com/textes_versionFR/txt_catalogue/musique_de_chambre.html) lists Eolienne as 1970, published by Billaudot. It identifies flute and harp or piano, with saxophone and clarinet alternatives. The catalog year is used for `composed`.
- The [composer’s work page](https://www.gotkovsky.com/textes_versionFR/txt_fiches_des_oeuvres/ff_eolienne.htm) confirms five movements and identifies a saxophone and harp recording. The public entry focuses on the saxophone and harp edition.
- [Ficks Music’s saxophone and harp edition](https://www.ficksmusic.com/products/gotkovsky-eolienne-version-for-sax-harp-billaudot) identifies Gérard Billaudot, GB3703, ISMN 9790043037033. Purchase and fulfillment were not tested.
- [Dylan Pich’s full performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebWXrKjEcT4) identifies alto saxophone and harp in its title. The performer’s description credits Pich on saxophone and Victoria Gonzalez on harp, recorded at the University of North Texas on 2023-09-30. It supplies timestamps for all five movements. One unlabelled player links to the complete performance. Playback was not auditioned.
- Dates differ between sources. A [University of North Texas dissertation](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark%3A/67531/metadc28482/m2/1/high_res_d/dissertation.pdf) dates the original flute work to 1969. A [library record for the saxophone edition](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB08187711) gives a 1983 copyright. Neither is substituted for the official catalog’s work date.

# Gemma Peacocke Catalog Additions

Added 2026-11-08 at Tim’s request. All Year of Study ratings came from Tim: Aglow Second Year; Imbrium Fourth Year; Skin Third Year; Hazel Fourth Year; Dwalm Advanced.

## Composer

- [Peacocke’s introduction](https://www.gemmapeacocke.com/about) identifies her as a composer from Aotearoa New Zealand. Reused the existing `🇳🇿 New Zealander` nationality label.
- [Her biography](https://www.gemmapeacocke.com/biography) uses she/her pronouns. Gender recorded as Female.
- The [Auckland Philharmonia concert programme](https://aucklandphil.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024_NZH1_PassionMystery_Programme_Spreads.pdf) gives her birth year as 1984.
- Portrait copied locally from [her photo page](https://www.gemmapeacocke.com/photos): [source image](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/172eea_d405e9e6136d4146886de85d107fa8b7~mv2.jpg). The caption credits the website because no photographer credit was verified for this image.
- Race left empty because no explicit self-identification was verified.

Each work links to the purchase page linked by Peacocke’s own work page. Aglow’s product target was verified from the live work page; its store content was not independently verified. No transaction or fulfillment was tested.

Aglow’s general catalog gives 25 minutes, while its dedicated page gives 22:30. Used the dedicated page. Hazel’s general catalog gives 9:30, while the dedicated page and store give 9:40. Used 9:40. Skin’s dedicated page combines saxophone and trumpet versions under “2016 / 2018”; the catalog and store explicitly give 2016 for the saxophone work, so that version’s date is recorded as 2016.

## Recordings

- Aglow: [composer’s SoundCloud, first movement only](https://soundcloud.com/gemmapeacocke/aglow-first-movement). The description credits Shelley Washington, baritone saxophone; Borah Han, piano; and Pat Swoboda, bass, at Roulette in June 2018. Labeled “First movement” to make coverage clear.
- Imbrium: [Lindsey Welp’s recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a_NTPgMxWDc), from *Stories We Tell*. YouTube metadata credits Peacocke and Welp and gives the release date as 2025-03-01. Welp’s [recording-project dissertation](https://openscholar.uga.edu/record/2040/files/STORIES%20WE%20TELL_WELP_ETD%20FINAL.pdf) identifies her as the tenor saxophonist for Imbrium.
- Skin: [Nick Suosso’s saxophone performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2R0-4_l2Cl0), approximately 6:06. The performer’s title and description identify the work, composer, and instrument. The video embedded on Peacocke’s work page is instead [Kate Amrine’s trumpet excerpt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ePyGB0Vdvlw), so it was excluded.
- Hazel: [~Nois performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6aA8VjHbN1M), embedded on Peacocke’s work page and uploaded by the composer.
- Dwalm: [~Nois performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ULl1fXwpx0o), embedded on Peacocke’s work page and uploaded by the composer.

The Wix page-data feeds were read to resolve embedded recording URLs. YouTube metadata was checked with oEmbed and yt-dlp. Recordings were not auditioned.

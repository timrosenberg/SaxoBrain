# Hans Kox: Concertino

Added at Tim's request on 2026-11-24 with his Advanced rating and supplied Spotify recording.

- [Donemus, catalogue no. 02208](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/2208): Concertino for alto saxophone and ten wind instruments, composed in 1982 and revised in 1991; approximately 10 minutes; dedicated to John-Edward Kelly. Accompanying instruments are piccolo, flute, alto flute, bass clarinet, two horns, two trumpets, trombone, and tuba. The publisher also sells a piano reduction and solo part. The numeric `composed` field uses the revised version's year, 1991.

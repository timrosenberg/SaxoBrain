# SaxoBrain - Katahj Copley Saxophone Catalog Research

Verified November 6, 2026. Originally added a composer profile and 14 work notes. At Tim's request later that day, removed nine of those notes. Five works remain in the catalog: Constellations No. 1, reFRAME, KRONO, Gradient, and Grosso Damn. The initial import left Year of Study unassigned and public work bodies empty. Research and unresolved source conflicts remain here.

## Requested Removals

Tim requested removal of breathe, Spectra, Grosso Strange, Pavane, Serenade for Wind Nonet, Shea Butter Suite, These Violent Delights, there are stars..., and Finish Line on November 6, 2026. Removed those nine public work notes. Their source research below remains as a private record.

All nine notes were untracked additions when removed. No published-address retirement entries were added.

## Scope and Sources

Covered solo saxophone, saxophone duets and ensembles, saxophone concertos, and mixed chamber or vocal works with explicitly documented saxophone parts. Ordinary band works with sectional saxophones were excluded.

The composer's [older chamber index](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-projects), [new chamber index](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-ensemble-projects), [older large-ensemble index](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/large-ensemble-music), [store](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-works), and public XML sitemaps expose different selections. The newer chamber sitemap supplied 34 work pages. Relevant pages were read directly, including pages unavailable through web search.

This is a verified catalog addition with documented gaps. It does not establish an exhaustive list of unpublished works.

## Composer Profile

- [Copley's official biography](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/about-9) supplies his name, pronouns, and website.
- His [first-person artist statement](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/) explicitly identifies him as Black. Reused the existing `Black` category.
- The [University of North Texas concert program](https://music.unt.edu/files/default/files/digital-programs/2026.03.30_concert-symphonic-university-winds_452.pdf) identifies him as American and gives his birth year as 1998. Reused `🇺🇸 American` and `Male` from the existing catalog. No portrait was added.

## Source Conflicts and Broken Navigation

- The old chamber index's Kronos link opens Imaginarium, a glockenspiel and vibraphone duet. The [new K-R-O-N-O-S page](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-ensemble-projects/k-r-o-n-o-s) lists 2025 and 5 minutes; the old index lists 2022 and 7 minutes. The publisher spells the title KRONO. Used the publisher's title and verified alto/tenor instrumentation. Left the composition year and duration blank. No separate duplicate was created from the inconsistent index entries.
- Constellations has conflicting metadata: the old work page says 2019 and 10 minutes; the old chamber index says 2019 and 6 minutes; the [new Constellation page](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-ensemble-projects/constellation) says 2021 and 8 minutes; Theodore Front's catalog describes a 2020 tenor sonata. Used the clearly identified tenor edition and left the year and duration blank.
- Gradient's [older work page](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/gradient) links its score button to the Halcyon Hearts band score. The [newer Gradient page](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-ensemble-projects/gradient) displays score buttons without working product links. Left purchase empty. Its 10-minute estimate differs from the complete 15:06 performance, so no duration was entered.
- Spectra displays a score-and-parts price without an active purchase link. Its duration varies between 16:30 on the work page and 18 minutes in the index. Left purchase and duration empty.
- Grosso Strange's old index misidentifies the accompaniment as wind ensemble. Both detailed work pages identify saxophone ensemble, piano, and percussion. The older work page dates it 2018–2019; the newer page lists 2018. Left the composition year blank.
- Grosso Damn's [older page](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/grosso-damn) gives 24 minutes and a first-movement demo. The newer page gives 25 minutes and future score-availability notices. Used the complete official performance and left purchase and duration empty.
- Pavane's older page gives 5:30; its newer page gives 10 minutes. Left duration blank.
- Shea Butter Suite's [composer-store edition](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/product-page/shea-butter-suite-vol-1-digital-score-and-parts) is explicitly Vol. 1. Used the publisher's chamber-winds edition. No claim was made that the composer's instrumentation list is exhaustive.

## Confirmed Leads Held Outside the Public Catalog

- [Mirages](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-ensemble-projects/mirages): official page identifies a 2021 duet for two like saxophones in B-flat or E-flat, estimated at 5 minutes. Its displayed score buttons have no working product links. No recording was found. Retained here pending a usable score or recording.
- [No Strings Attached](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/chamber-ensemble-projects/no-strings-attached): official page identifies soprano voice, two trumpets, tenor saxophone, two trombones, sousaphone, and drumset; 2023; 4 minutes. Its score buttons have no working product links. No recording was found. Retained here pending usable materials.
- [Watercolors](https://www.katahjcopleymusic.com/watercolors): the composer page currently has no work information or score/recording links. The [Institute for Composer Diversity's Fischoff repertoire resource](https://www.composerdiversity.com/fischoff) identifies an 8-minute saxophone quartet. Retained as a lead pending a usable edition or recording and confirmed saxophone voicing.
- [Nick May's commission page](https://www.nickmaysax.com/katahj-copley) announces an untitled soprano-saxophone-and-wind-ensemble work for June 2028. No completed title, score, or recording was verified, so no work note was created.
- Beautiful Lies is labeled only as solo and piano in the old index. A saxophone edition was not established. No saxophone entry was created from that generic listing.

## Verification Limits

Recording titles, artist credits, movement lists, and embedded-source URLs were inspected where available. Grosso Strange's first player was briefly activated to recover the audio URL. The recordings were not auditioned in full. Checkout and delivery of purchased scores were not tested.

Initially, the Spectra and Constellations recording links opened the composer's pages. Spectra was subsequently removed at Tim's request. Constellations now uses direct MP3 sources in native SaxoBrain players. The other retained recording destinations use supported YouTube or SoundCloud formats.

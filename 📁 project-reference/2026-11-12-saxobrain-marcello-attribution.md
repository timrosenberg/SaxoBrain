# Marcello Concerto Attribution

Corrected at Tim’s request on November 12, 2026. The concerto and composer notes now use Alessandro Marcello. Original notes attributed to Benedetto are archived. Both former public addresses redirect to their corrected counterparts. The existing Rousseau edition, purchase link, Third Year assignment, and other work metadata are preserved.

- The [existing Groth score listing](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-71689-concerto-in-d-minor-soprano-sax-and-piano.aspx) explicitly identifies Alessandro Marcello, Eugene Rousseau, soprano saxophone and piano, and Jeanne Inc. JP4003.
- [Jeanne’s edition page](https://jeanne-inc.com/products/marcello-rousseau-concerto-soprano-saxophone-piano) confirms Rousseau’s soprano saxophone and piano edition, JP4003. This is the same edition already selected in the work note.
- [Indiana University’s catalog](https://iucat.iu.edu/iub/1475410) identifies Benedetto as a historical misattribution of the oboe concerto and supplies Alessandro as the corrected composer.
- [Alessandro Marcello’s current biographical entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Marcello) identifies the Italian composer’s dates as 1673–1747. These replace Benedetto’s 1686–1739 in the active composer profile. Older reference works give differing dates; no full birth or death dates are stored in SaxoBrain.
- The former Wikidata identifier Q312784 and portrait source explicitly identify Benedetto. They remain in the archived original. Alessandro’s Wikidata and photo fields are blank. His race field is left unassigned.

The older Sampen repertoire-reference link now points to Alessandro’s renamed concerto note. No score or recording links were replaced.

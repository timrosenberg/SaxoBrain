# Launch Album Additions

Added starshine & moonfall, Thaumaturgy, and Still Here at Tim’s request on 2026-11-09. Year of Study is empty for all three. Work bodies are empty. Existing catalog notes were preserved.

## Works and Scores

- **Natalie Moller, starshine & moonfall:** The [official works page](https://www.nataliemollermusic.com/) gives 2014, alto saxophone and harp, approximately four minutes, and identifies the Admiral Launch Duo commission. The [composer’s PDF product page](https://www.nataliemollermusic.com/purchases/starshine-amp-moonfall-pdf) confirms the instrumentation, duration, PDF format, and an Add To Cart control. Direct retrieval succeeded on 2026-11-09.
- **Patrick O’Malley, Thaumaturgy:** The [composer’s work page](https://www.musicbypatrickomalley.com/thaumaturgy) gives soprano saxophone and harp, 2014, approximately 15 minutes, and three movements: I. Cast and Bend; II. Ripple - Reflect; III. Holy Meteor. The album publicity uses 2015. The composer’s date is used. The [composer’s store](https://www.musicbypatrickomalley.com/purchase-thaumaturgy) offers physical and PDF performance scores. Both players read from the score. Direct retrieval succeeded. One unlabelled score button leads to the available format choices.
- **Angélica Negrón, Still Here:** The [composer’s works list](https://www.angelicanegron.com/works) gives 2017, tenor saxophone, harp, electronics, and approximately nine minutes. Her site identifies Decca Publishing and directs chamber music sales to Subito. [Sheet Music Plus](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/still-here-23075886.html) lists the matching tenor saxophone, harp, and electronics edition, with part and online audio, catalog DP 000066. Its indexed description gives 2016 and a separate 2025 date. The composer’s work date is used. The retailer’s indexed listing was checked; direct retrieval was blocked with HTTP 403. [Subito’s harp catalog](https://subitomusic.com/product-category/harp/) independently lists the same work for those instruments, but a direct product URL was not verified.

Score purchase and fulfillment were not tested.

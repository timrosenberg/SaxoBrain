## Six Uncertain Matches

These entries are excluded from the missing totals to avoid adding duplicate or misidentified works.

- **Bach/Teal: Sarabande (from Teal’s Solos)** (Freshman Literature, ID 26). Identity unresolved. No Bach Sarabande record. Hal Leonard’s Teal alto collection lists Sicilienne and Allegro from Flute Sonata No. 2. Its tenor collection assigns Sarabande to Debussy. Do not create a Bach record until the intended piece is identified.
- **Bach/Sampen: Sonata No. 2 (flute)** (Junior Repertoire, ID 83). No Sampen edition or Sonata No. 2 record. The existing E-flat-major flute sonata, BWV 1031, is a possible underlying-work match, but the source supplies no key or BWV. Do not count a new work until identified.
- **Demersseman: Fantaisie (1862)** (Historical Solos, ID 156). Probable match: Fantaisie sur un thème original. The source’s shortened Fantaisie and 1862 date do not establish identity conclusively. Held out of the confirmed-missing total.
- **Gould: Concerto** (STB Repertoire, ID 178). Probable match: Diversions. Wise Music’s composer note describes the tenor work’s concerto-scale demands and explains the title Diversions. Sampen only says Concerto, so the equivalence remains unconfirmed.
- **Karlins: Sonata** (STB Repertoire, ID 183). Possible match: Music for Tenor Saxophone and Piano by William Karlins, Southern Music. The source says Sonata. No record with that title or a verified equivalence was found.
- **Wilder: Suite** (STB Repertoire, ID 191). Possible match: Suite No. 1, Three Ballads for Stan Getz. The source does not number the Suite. Wise Music confirms Suite No. 1, but the source’s intended suite remains uncertain.

The Teal anthology discrepancy can be checked against Hal Leonard’s [alto collection](https://www.halleonard.com/product/50330580/solos-for-the-alto-saxophone-player) and [tenor collection](https://www.halleonard.com/product/50490436/solos-for-the-tenor-saxophone-player?promotion=6440028promotion%3D6440028). The probable Gould match is supported by [Wise Music’s composer note](https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/28311/Diversions-tenor-saxophone-and-orchestra--Morton-Gould/). The possible Wilder match is [Suite No. 1 in Wise Music’s catalog](https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/34446/Suite-No-1-for-Tenor-Saxophone-and-Strings-Three-Ballads-for-Stan-Getz--Alec-Wilder/).

## Identity and Edition Findings

- **Lauba, Hard (ID 184):** absent. The existing [Christian Lauba - Étude No. 10 Hard too Hard](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Christian%20Lauba%20-%20%C3%89tude%20No.%2010%20Hard%20too%20Hard.md) is a separate composition. [Stephen F. Austin State University’s syllabus](https://orion.sfasu.edu/courseinformation/syl/201401/MUP11940.pdf?635558600793872248=) lists both works.

- **Singelée, Solo de concert (ID 159):** the source’s 1860 cue points to **Op. 74**. [The Bibliothèque nationale de France record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark%3A/12148/cb43273888v) identifies the alto work and its 1860 Conservatoire use. The catalog’s [Jean-Baptiste Singelée - Solo de Concert, Op. 77](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Jean-Baptiste%20Singel%C3%A9e%20-%20Solo%20de%20Concert%2C%20Op.%2077.md) is a different baritone work.

- **Singelée, Fantaisie pastorale (ID 160):** absent as **Op. 56**. [The Bibliothèque nationale de France catalog](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=TOUS3&numNotice=13954920&typeNotice=p) identifies the 1858 B-flat saxophone and piano work. The existing Op. 89 and Op. 102 titles do not cover it.

- **Furman (ID 146):** the source prints “Music for Alto Sax & Piano” in the electronics list. [Sampen’s commissions list](https://www.johnwsampen.com/commissions-premieres) identifies Pablo Furman’s **Music for Alto Saxophone and Tape**. That title also appears in the existing [The Electric Saxophone album record](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/recordings/The%20Electric%20Saxophone.md). A work record is absent.

- **Other works mentioned elsewhere:** Mobberly’s Spontaneous Combustion appears on The Electric Saxophone album. Hurel’s Opcit appears in the 2011 Gaudeamus list. Neither has a work record.

- **Schuller, Sonata (ID 48):** [RCEditions, its publisher](https://www.rceditions.com/item-details/124/sonata) confirms the alto-and-piano work. The existing Schuller Concerto does not cover it.

- **Mahler/Hemke (ID 56):** [Hal Leonard’s Southern Music listing](https://www.halleonard.com/product/3775310/a-ruckert-song-ich-bin-der-welt-abhanden-gekom?menuid=2565) identifies **A Rückert Song**, arranged by Fred Hemke, catalog ST574.

- **Bach/Mule, Aria (ID 24):** the underlying work is present as [Johann Sebastian Bach - Air From Suite No. 3](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Johann%20Sebastian%20Bach%20-%20Air%20From%20Suite%20No.%203.md). [Leduc’s publisher catalog, entry AL19576](https://casamusicaledelrio.it/casamusicale/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/CAT-18-107-Leduc-Catalogue-2018-ss-GS-v6-NO-CROPS-LO-RESv2.pdf) identifies Mule’s Aria as BWV 1068. The database currently lists Cecil Leeson’s edition.

- **Bach/Harle, G-minor Sonata (ID 49):** present under [Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach - Sonata in G minor, H.542.5 - BWV 1020](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Carl%20Philipp%20Emanuel%20Bach%20-%20Sonata%20in%20G%20minor%2C%20H.542.5%20-%20BWV%201020.md). The record names Harle and links BWV 1020.

- **Bach/Londeix, Suite No. 3 (ID 82):** covered at work level by [Johann Sebastian Bach - Six Suites for Violoncello Solo](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Johann%20Sebastian%20Bach%20-%20Six%20Suites%20for%20Violoncello%20Solo.md). [The National Institute of Informatics library record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA89731735) identifies Londeix’s separate edition as the third cello suite, BWV 1009. The catalog lists Kynaston’s complete collection.

- **Ravel/Bettoney, Pavane (ID 30):** the underlying work is present as [Maurice Ravel - Pavane pour une Infante Defunte](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Maurice%20Ravel%20-%20Pavane%20pour%20une%20Infante%20Defunte.md). The database specifies Martino’s soprano edition. Sampen’s Bettoney version remains an edition gap.

- **Other edition gaps:** Rachmaninoff/Teal’s Vocalise is represented by the underlying work. The catalog does not identify the Teal anthology edition. The source Koechlin études are represented by Op. 188 in a different edition.

- **Broad collection references:** Lauba’s unspecified “Etudes” are represented by the nine individual Neuf Études records. Karg-Elert’s 25 Caprices are present. The source’s additional words “and Sonatas” do not identify another specific composition.

- **Campagnoli, Études variées (ID 32):** covered by the existing [Marcel Mule - Études Variées](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Marcel%20Mule%20-%20%C3%89tudes%20Vari%C3%A9es.md). The [Conservatoire de musique et d’art dramatique du Québec syllabus](https://www.conservatoire.gouv.qc.ca/media/0oulvph0/cmadq-progexternes-saxophone.pdf) explicitly identifies Campagnoli among the composers represented in Mule’s collection.

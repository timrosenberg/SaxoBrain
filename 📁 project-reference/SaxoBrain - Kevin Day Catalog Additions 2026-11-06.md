# SaxoBrain - Kevin Day Catalog Additions

Verified November 6, 2026. Added six distinct saxophone works from Tim's supplied Murphy Music Press links and Kevin Day's chamber music store. The three accompaniment versions of Unquiet Waters share one work page. Year of Study remains unassigned for all six works.

## Composer

- Tim supplied Male, Black, American, and the official website. Reused the catalog's existing `🇺🇸 American`, `Male`, and `Black` values.
- [Kevin Day's official biography](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/bio) confirms the birth year 1996. No portrait was added.

## Blitz Circuit

- [Composer's store product](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/sheet-music-store/p/blitz-circuit-for-alto-sax-bass-clarinet), inspected in the browser: alto saxophone and bass clarinet; PDF score and parts supplied upon purchase.
- [Clairvoyant Records' Impulse release](https://clairvoyantrecords.bandcamp.com/album/impulse) identifies Blitz Circuit as a Kevin Day commission for Nicki Roman and Jeff Anderle. The credits specify alto saxophone and bass clarinet. Linked the individual [Blitz Circuit track](https://clairvoyantrecords.bandcamp.com/track/blitz-circuit).
- Composition year was not established. The album release date was not treated as the composition date.

## Converging Spectrums

- [Murphy Music Press Q-180](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/q-180) and the [composer's PDF edition](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/sheet-music-store/p/converging-spectrums) provide separate purchase options. The composer explicitly specifies SATB saxophone quartet.
- [University of North Texas recital program](https://music.unt.edu/special-event-programs/files/2026.02.12_myriad-quartet_67.pdf) dates the work to 2018. [Theodore Front's score listing](https://www.tfront.com/p-530783-converging-spectrums-for-saxophone-quartet-2018) agrees and specifies score and four parts.
- The publisher embeds SoundCloud track 1525116598. Its player exposes the canonical recording link [Converging Spectrums-Kevin Day](https://soundcloud.com/user-353618625/converging-spectrums-kevin-day1). Used that recording.
- [Millennium Composers Initiative's composer interview](https://www.mcicomposers.com/news/day-spotlight-cs) describes a multi-movement plan and a first-movement premiere. The catalog does not claim that the published recording covers a completed multi-movement cycle.

## Enter Data Sequence

- [Murphy Music Press Q-172](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/q-172): saxophone quartet.
- [Kevin Day's official YouTube recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XxvENCvhOc0) identifies the work as 2022 and credits the Estrella Consort with soprano, alto, tenor, and baritone saxophones. Added this recording and composition year.
- [Northern Illinois University's Estrella Consort recital listing](https://calendar.niu.edu/event/thomas_snydacker_-_saxophone_quartet_recital) independently lists the same work as 2022.

## Metrofusion

- [Murphy Music Press Q-192](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/q-192) and the [composer's PDF edition](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/sheet-music-store/p/metrofusion-for-saxophone-quartet) provide separate purchase options.
- [Sacramento State's November 5, 2025 program](https://www.csus.edu/college/arts-letters/music/_internal/_documents/programs/11.5.25-local-voices.pdf) lists soprano, alto, tenor, and baritone saxophones. It dates the work to 2024, while [Earsense](https://www.earsense.org/chamber-music/composer/Kevin-Day/) lists 2023. Left the composition year blank because these sources disagree.
- The publisher embeds SoundCloud track 2114982627. Its player exposes the canonical recording link [Metrofusion-Kevin Day](https://soundcloud.com/user-353618625/01-metrofusion). Used that recording.

## Solace in the Wind

- [Composer's PDF edition](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/sheet-music-store/p/solace-in-the-wind): saxophone choir, string bass, and percussion.
- [Capital Wind Symphony's YouTube performance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tYUhe-y_ejA) credits the saxophone ensemble, bass, and percussion. Added this recording.
- Added the saxophone version only. [The composer's works list](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/music) also lists band and string-orchestra arrangements by Josh Trentadue.

## Unquiet Waters

- [Murphy Music Press S-141](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/s-141): alto saxophone and piano.
- [Murphy Music Press CB6-1135](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/cb6-1135): wind ensemble parts rental. Its separate [CB6-1135A score](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/cb6-1135a) can be purchased. Both options are labeled explicitly.
- [Murphy Music Press C-333](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/c-333): solo alto saxophone and saxophone ensemble, arranged by Josh Trentadue.
- [The composer's works list](https://www.kevindaymusic.com/music) confirms all three saxophone versions. The clarinet version in the store was excluded.
- [Kevin Day's official YouTube recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_rIiRh86o1k) identifies Nicki Roman, the University of Southern Mississippi Wind Ensemble, and Catherine Rand. Its description lists all three movements. Added one labeled wind-ensemble player.
- The piano product embeds SoundCloud track 662713241, which its player identifies as [I. fast, turbulent, Dan Phipps and Sonny Yoo](https://soundcloud.com/kevindaymusic/unquiet-waters-i-fast-turbulent). This is a first-movement recording, so it was not added alongside the complete wind-ensemble performance.
- [Ravello Records' album page](https://www.ravellorecords.com/catalog/rr8055/) separately verifies the three movements in Nicki Roman and Casey Dierlam Tse's piano recording. No album catalog entry was created.
- Composition year remains blank. The 2020 competition award was not treated as proof of the composition date.

## Result

- Researched all 38 profiles.
- Added a local, visually inspected photograph and an editable source-credit caption to every profile.
- Verified nationality descriptors for 37 profiles.
- Verified birth years for 35 profiles.
- Confirmed Grisey’s existing birth and death years, 1946 and 1998.
- Preserved titles, slugs, gender and race fields, existing body material, and the Composer Works Base embeds.
- No commit or push was performed.

## Remaining Gaps

- **David Dean Mendoza: nationality.** His official biography confirms identity and birth year, but does not state nationality. A secondary directory lists USA without defining the relationship. The field remains blank rather than treating education, residence, or an ambiguous country label as citizenship evidence.
- **Chia-Yu Hsu: birth year.** NCAF identifies the same composer and uses 1971. Curtis and a Taiwanese American archive use 1975. Her current official and faculty biographies omit a birth year. The conflicting dates remain in the evidence report, with the catalog field blank.
- **Viviana Dal Santo: birth year.** No verified year was found in the searched official and institutional sources.
- **Yaz Lancaster: birth year.** The current official biography explicitly permits 199x. The site uses an integer year field, so it remains blank.

Death years were entered only when verified. An empty death field does not add an assertion about a person’s current status. Fuller dates are retained in the batch evidence where available.

## Portrait Review

All 38 downloaded images were decoded and inspected. The university’s Guzmán-Muñoz image was a generic silhouette and was rejected. The Ministry of Culture’s identified portrait was used instead. An orchestral image initially supplied by a Santos page was also rejected in favor of his USC portrait. Both rejected assets were moved to temporary review storage outside public static assets.

Known source limitations: the ministry portrait of Guzmán-Muñoz is small and has an official duotone treatment. Lacy’s official portrait has substantial black padding. These source characteristics were retained.

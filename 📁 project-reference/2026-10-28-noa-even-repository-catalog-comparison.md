# Noa Even Repository Catalog Comparison

Checked October 28, 2026 against the current local SaxoBrain catalog, including unpublished files.

**65 candidate works are absent: 62 solo works and 3 works with piano.** One additional missing listing is an Oliveros anthology that needs selection of individual works. Of the 165 source listings, 99 are already represented.

## Scope And Method

- [Noa Even: Solo Works](https://www.noaevenmusic.com/solo-works): 96 listings.
- [Noa Even: Solo With Piano](https://www.noaevenmusic.com/saxandpiano): 69 listings.
- Compared composer and work identity with all 1,260 local Markdown records in `content/works/`.
- Checked alternate spellings, shortened titles, subtitles, and compositions represented inside collections.
- Catalog entries were not added or edited. This note is outside the public Hugo content tree.

“Candidate” means absent from the current catalog. It does not mean every publication detail has been independently established. Years and instrumentation below follow the repository unless a correction is explicitly noted. Score availability, difficulty, and most linked recordings still need verification before addition. “Repository link” preserves the source’s destination as a research lead. It is not an endorsement of its accuracy or current availability.

## Corrections And Addition Checks

- **Lera Auerbach: The Prayer.** Composer confirms Prayer (T’filah), four separately published saxophone versions. Alto: 2009; soprano/tenor/baritone: 2020. Composer gives 6 minutes.
- **Marcos Balter: Cardinal.** Composer confirms baritone saxophone and reverb. Requires an effects setup.
- **Andrew Mead: Sonata Brevis.** Distinct from the cataloged Bertold Hummel work of the same name. Murphy Music Press lists Mead’s solo alto version.
- **François Rossé: Arianna.** Repository recording URL points to Luis Quintana’s Desnudo. Replace before addition.
- **Marilyn Shrude: River Song. A Postcard from the Sichuan Provence.** Check the full title and the repository spelling “Provence” against a composer or publisher source.
- **Joan Tower: Second Flight.** Repository says 1981; publisher confirms 2017, alto saxophone, 8 minutes.
- **Evan Williams: Rock Steady.** Repository recording link points to Shelley Washington’s general SoundCloud profile. Do not reuse without finding the correct recording.
- **Clare Loveday: Wind-Play.** Repository says soprano or alto. South African Saxophone Catalogue specifies soprano. Verify the alto version before cataloging it.
- **Gary Nash: Valse and Intermezzo.** Repository omits piano in its scoring line. Vanderbilt concert program confirms saxophone with piano.

Supporting checks:

- [Lera Auerbach’s official Prayer page](https://leraauerbach.com/compositions/prayer-for-saxophone-solo/) identifies the separately published saxophone versions and dates.
- [Marcos Balter’s official works catalog](https://marcosbalter.com/works/) lists Cardinal for baritone saxophone and reverb, dated 2013.
- [Joan Tower’s publisher](https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/57414/Second-Flight--Joan-Tower/) dates Second Flight to 2017 and identifies solo alto saxophone.
- [Marc Mellits’s official Frost page](https://www.marcmellits.com/frost) confirms solo tenor saxophone, 2011, and twelve minutes.
- [Vanderbilt University’s concert announcement](https://blair.vanderbilt.edu/2021/01/20/vanderbilt-blair-school-celebrates-grand-opening-of-national-museum-of-african-american-music/) identifies Valse and Intermezzo by Gary Powell Nash with saxophone and piano performers.
- [South African Saxophone Catalogue: Clare Loveday](https://southafricansaxophonecatalogue.com/loveday-clare) identifies Just a Bite for alto saxophone and piano and Wind-play for soprano saxophone and piano.
- [Murphy Music Press catalog search](https://murphymusicpress.com/search?query=Sonata+Op.+19) lists Sonata Brevis by Andrew Mead for solo alto saxophone.
- [James Umble’s faculty biography at Youngstown State University](https://ysu.edu/people/james-umble) independently identifies Mead’s Sonata Brevis for solo saxophone.

## Limits

No judgment is made about whether the repository has been abandoned. Its current pages are used as discovery sources. This audit concerns repertoire coverage in the local catalog on the check date. It does not certify the accuracy of every existing catalog record or the completeness of Noa Even’s repository.

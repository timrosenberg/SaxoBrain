## Scope and Profile

Added one composer and six works. The concerto versions share one record per title, following the existing catalog convention. Each score and recording link identifies its instrumentation where needed.

The [official biography](https://hilarytann.com/about/) verifies Tann’s dates as November 2, 1947 and February 8, 2023. It identifies her as Welsh-born and as John Howard Payne Professor of Music Emerita at Union College. The nationality property uses the existing British category for Welsh identity. The [New York Women Composers member profile](https://newyorkwomencomposers.org/author/htann) supports the gender field. Race remains unknown. The portrait copied from her official biography carries a visible © 2017 Javier Monjas credit.

## Edition and Date Checks

The composer’s [Selected Works by Instrument](https://hilarytann.com/assets/pdf_files/Selected-Works-by-Instrument.pdf) explicitly lists the requested saxophone versions of all six titles. The current [compositions page](https://hilarytann.com/compositions/) often gives the oboe original as its primary scoring. The instrumentation PDF and the [official discography](https://hilarytann.com/discography/) resolve that difference.

- **In the First, Spinning Place:** retained all three OUP links supplied by Tim, labeled piano, orchestra rental, and band rental. Both ensemble versions and the piano transcription are confirmed by the composer’s current catalog. The OUP product pages returned no usable body during direct retrieval and were inaccessible through the web tool. Current stock and rental terms are unknown. [Union College’s contemporaneous 2000 notice](https://muse.union.edu/newsarchives/2000/03/10/faculty-staff-works-listed-33/) documents the original concerto premiere. Its [2009 notice](https://muse.union.edu/newsarchives/2009/05/27/people-in-the-news-46/) documents the piano reduction premiere. The composed property uses 2000.
- **Shakkei:** [Rowanberry Music’s order form](https://hilarytann.com/rowanberry-music/) lists the orchestral score, parts, and piano transcription with solo part. Its catalog explicitly permits soprano saxophone for the piano version. The instrumentation PDF confirms soprano with small orchestra. The NYWC work entry says 2008, while its member biography says the premiere was August 2007. The score copyright also says 2007. The composed property uses 2007. No claim about saxophone-specific notation is inferred from the orchestral preview.
- **Shôji:** the inspected score says © 2010 Rowanberry Music and spells the title Shôji. The discography uses Shōji. The unaccented slug is stable as hilary-tann-shoji. Rowanberry’s ordering menu labels its stock item flute/oboe. The public note tells users to specify the soprano saxophone version when requesting materials.
- **Songs of the Cotton Grass:** the [Deux-Elles booklet](https://deux-elles.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/DXL1132_booklet_www.pdf) documents development from the first song in 1999 through Vale of Feathers in 2005. The composed property uses 1999, the first original-work date. The [National Library of Wales score record](https://archives.library.wales/index.php/songs-of-the-cotton-grass) verifies OUP ISBN 9780193364547 and four titles: A Girl’s Song to Her Mother, Slave’s Song, Wings of the Grasses, and Vale of Feathers. The NYWC entry agrees on four titles. The OUP purchase destination identifies this edition by its ISBN; current availability is unknown.
- **Like Lightnings:** the composer’s [indexed program note](https://hilarytann.com/compositions/?series=instrumental+solos) says it was composed in June 2004 for Jinny Shaw. The score copyright is 2005. The composed property uses 2004. The purchase link is explicitly labeled as the oboe edition. Saxophone materials should be requested from the estate. The retailer page was located through its indexed listing; current stock is unknown. An attempted Peters edition lookup failed because that host’s TLS certificate had expired. No certificate bypass was used.
- **Windhover:** the composer’s program note and [British Music Collection](https://britishmusiccollection.org.uk/score/windhover-4) date the soprano saxophone original to 1985. The linked [Forsyths edition](https://www.forsyths.co.uk/sheet-music/instruments/woodwind/saxophone-sheet-music/120985-tann-hilary-windhover-9780193858701.html) is the soprano saxophone score, ISBN 9780193858701. Its indexed listing confirmed the edition. Current stock is unknown.

## Recording Checks

Used Spotify’s connected catalog and album-content tools. Selected tracks returned PLAYABLE at the time of the check. This verifies service metadata and reported playability, not a complete audition or availability in every region. URLs are stored exactly as returned by Spotify.

- **In the First, Spinning Place:** Debra Richtmeyer with the Slovak Radio Symphony Orchestra and Kirk Trevor on Here, The Cliffs. Also linked Richtmeyer with Leng-leng Lam on World Without Words. The composer’s discography confirms both releases.
- **Shakkei:** both movements from Susan Fancher’s Of Erthe and Air recording. The track titles explicitly identify soprano saxophone and orchestra.
- **Shôji:** Erin Lesser and Susan Fancher on Of Erthe and Air. The composer’s discography confirms flute and soprano saxophone.
- **Songs of the Cotton Grass:** three tracks on Of Erthe and Air, credited to Clara O’Brien and Susan Fancher. The discography identifies the forces as mezzo-soprano and soprano saxophone. No separate Slave’s Song track appears on this album. The public work note discloses the three-song selection and the published four-title collection.
- **Like Lightnings:** Virginia Shaw’s oboe recording on Metamorphosis - Collaboration III. The official program note identifies Shaw and this album. No soprano saxophone recording was located in targeted web and Spotify searches. The recording is labeled as the oboe original in the work’s player.
- **Windhover:** Susan Fancher’s solo soprano saxophone recording on Of Erthe and Air. The official discography confirms the instrument.

The numeric composed values use 2023 for the revised Inflorescence and 2026 for the completion of the publisher’s Heroine date range. The two movement titles, The Flowers and The Poet, are corroborated by Kyle Horch’s complete performance description and his album’s track list.

The publisher describes ONRUSH’s multiphonics and virtuosic writing. The current Heroine notes describe improvisation around Charlotte Mew’s poem, with optional images. These facts were recorded in private research properties without assigning difficulty levels.

## Differences and Unresolved Information

- **Glass Ceiling recording:** no verified saxophone recording found.
- **Current Heroine edition recording:** the publisher names Leymah Gbowee, Ruth Bader Ginsburg, Charlotte Mew, Mary Helen MacKillop, and Susan B. Anthony. The [2024 UCLA program](https://schoolofmusic.ucla.edu/event/anne-lebaron/program/) and premiere video include Julieta Dobles and omit Charlotte Mew. The added player is labeled “2024 premiere version,” and a short public note explains the differing portrait selection. No complete recording of the current published selection was verified. The linked concert continues into other works after the saxophone set.
- **Current Heroine score details:** its preview URL returned 404, so the order and individual saxophone assignments in the current edition were not checked against the score. No movement list was added from the older premiere program.
- **With Eyes Open duration:** the composer gives 8 minutes; Composers Edition gives approximately 9. The catalog retains the 8–9 minute range.
- **O Vos duration:** British Music Collection gives 2 minutes; Composers Edition gives approximately 3. The catalog retains the 2–3 minute range. The verified performance lasts approximately 3:07.
- **Punch, echo date wording:** the performance description says 2020/21 and carries a 2021 copyright. The publisher and composer’s work list give 2020. In the composer’s [2021 first-person account](https://www.illuminatewomensmusic.co.uk/illuminate-blog/angela-elizabeth-slater-discusses-her-compositional-works), she says the piece was finished before the pandemic and was not subsequently edited. The catalog uses 2020.
- **Race:** no documented self-identification verified for the five composers.
- **Photographers:** no photographer verified for the particular Plowman and Slater portraits used.
- **Score previews:** Inflorescence’s linked preview was accessible. The linked previews for the other six editions returned 404 during direct requests. Their purchase listings supplied the basic metadata; no unavailable preview was added as a download.
- **Year of Study:** deliberately unassigned for all seven works. This remains Tim’s pedagogical decision.

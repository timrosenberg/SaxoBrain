# SaxoBrain - Benjamin Horne REM Addition

Verified November 6, 2026. Added at Third Year, as requested by Tim.

- [Composer's works catalog](https://hornemusic.weebly.com/works.html) lists REM (2018, revised 2020) for solo alto saxophone, approximately five minutes. It credits Zachary McDonald with the commission and dates the premiere and recording to May 2018. The numeric `composed` field retains the original composition year, 2018; the revision date is recorded here.
- The catalog links to [REM for Alto Saxophone on SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/benmhorne21/rem). SoundCloud's public oEmbed endpoint returned this title, the composer account BenMHorne21, and the credit "Recorded by Jared Cummings". Its player resolves to track 509169438. Playback was not auditioned. No matching saxophone YouTube recording was verified.
- The catalog's separate trumpet version has purchase links. [Murphy Music Press's Benjamin Horne search](https://murphymusicpress.com/search?query=benjamin+horne) lists REM for C trumpet with cup mute. A saxophone purchase listing and publisher were not verified, so those properties remain empty.
- The composer's [contact page](https://hornemusic.weebly.com/contact.html) explicitly accepts purchasing and licensing inquiries. Added a short score-inquiry link in the public work body.
- The composer's [biography](https://hornemusic.weebly.com/bio.html) confirms his name and 1995 birth year and uses he/his pronouns. The [University of North Texas concert program](https://music.unt.edu/files/default/files/digital-programs/2025.11.10_symphonic-concert-winds_161.pdf) identifies him as American. Reused the catalog's existing American nationality label. Race remains unassigned because documented self-identification was not verified.
- Added a composer profile and left its biography body empty apart from the standard works view.

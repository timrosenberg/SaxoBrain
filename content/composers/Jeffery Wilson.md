## Research notes (private)

Composer identity corrected from Robert Wilson to Jeffery Wilson at Tim’s request on October 15, 2026. Saxtet credits Silver Sonatina to Jeffery Wilson; the composer’s own chamber catalog includes Tango in D. Both catalog works were removed under the same approval. The permanent slug robert-wilson is retained.

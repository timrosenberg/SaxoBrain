Hilary Tann (1947–2023) was a Welsh-born composer based in upstate New York. She was John Howard Payne Professor of Music Emerita at Union College. Her [official biography](https://hilarytann.com/about/) describes the influence of nature, Welsh landscapes, and Japanese music on her work.

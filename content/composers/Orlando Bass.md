Orlando Bass is a French and British pianist and composer. His [official website](https://www.orlandobassmusic.com/) describes his work as a pianist, composer, harpsichordist and improviser. A [2021 concert program from the Colegio de España in Paris](https://www.colesp.org/wp-content/uploads/jevents/60cca172bd4707.89413835.pdf) confirms his birth year as 1994.

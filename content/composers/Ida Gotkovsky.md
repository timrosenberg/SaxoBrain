> Gotkovsky, a student of Olivier Messiaen and Nadia Boulanger at the Paris Conservatory, frequently utilizes a distinct artistic device called intertextual self-borrowing. Rather than simply reusing material across her catalog, she transforms, re-harmonizes, and evolves existing melodies to weave a macro-dialogue throughout her life’s work. For instance, motifs from the _Désinvolte_ movement are heavily mirrored in her _Quatuor pour saxophones_, while thematic material from the blazing _Final_ was later expanded into the second movement of her massive wind ensemble piece, _Poème du Feu_ (1978).
>

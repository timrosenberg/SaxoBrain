Repertoire for the 2026 Andorra SaxFest solo saxophone competition, from the supplied program excerpt (printed page 15).

[Competition repertoire (PDF)](/media/lists/competitions/2026-andorra-sax-fest-competition/Andorra-SaxFest-2026-Repertoire.pdf)

All three rounds take place in the auditorium of the Centre de Congressos d'Andorra la Vella.

## First Round

**Dates:** March 29–31, 2026.

- [[Ida Gotkovsky - Brillance|Brilliance]] by [[Ida Gotkovsky]], **third movement, Dolcissimo**. Performed without a prior piano rehearsal.
- **Babioles** by Marc Monnet, **I. Jongleurs de sons** and **VI. Gnomes fantaisies**, for solo saxophone.

## Second Round

**Date:** April 2, 2026.

- [[Claude Pascal - Sonatine|Sonatine]] by [[Claude Pascal]] (Durand).
- A published original solo work for any member of the saxophone family, chosen by the contestant. A transcription is permitted only if it was made by the composer and is published.

## Final Round

**Date:** April 4, 2026.

- [[Pierre-Max DuBois - Concerto|Concerto]] by [[Pierre-Max DuBois]].
- **Lutte** by [[Thierry Escaich]], an original solo saxophone work.

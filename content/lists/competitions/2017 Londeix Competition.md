## The First Elimination Round

Solo saxophone, maximum time: 20 minutes. Each competitor will present three works, two from list 1 and one from list 2.

### List 1

- Two of the [[Sigfrid Karg-Elert - 25 Capricen|25 Capricen]] *op. 153a* (1929) by [[Sigfrid Karg-Elert|Sigfrid Karg-Elert]]  (1877-1933). Choice of saxophones at the discretion of the candidate based upon the character of the Caprices chosen (Editions Zimmerman ( [www.zimmermann-frankfurt.de](http://www.zimmermann-frankfurt.de/) ) or Southern Music.

### List 2

- One of *9 Études* for solo saxophone by [[Christian Lauba|Christian Lauba]] (Alphonse Leduc Music Publications)
    - [[Christian Lauba - Neuf Études, Book 1 - No. 1 Balafon|Neuf Études, Book 1: No. 1 Balafon]]

    - No. 8 excluded because it is for two soprano saxophones
    - [[Christian Lauba - Neuf Études, Book 4 - No. 9 Bat|Neuf Études, Book 4: No. 9 Bat]]

## The Second Elimination Round

One piece with piano accompaniment (either personal pianist or staff pianist of competition), *and* one piece with electroacoustic accompaniment. A staff technology specialist will be available for consultation, as needed. Each competitor will choose one piece from list A and one piece from list B, two pieces in total, maximum time: 35 minutes.

### List A

- [[André Caplet - Légende|Légende (Caplet)]] (version for alto saxophone and piano) by [[André Caplet|André Caplet]] (Resolute Music Publications)
- [[Florent Schmitt - Légende, Op. 66|Légende, Op. 66]] (alto saxophone) by [[Florent Schmitt|Florent Schmitt]] (Édition Durand)
- [[Paul Hindemith - Sonata|Sonata (Hindemith)]] (alto saxophone, *with Finale by Jean Marie LONDEIX* ), by [[Paul Hindemith|Paul Hindemith]] (Édition Schott)
- [[Pierre-Philippe Bauzin - Sonate No. 1|Sonate No. 1 (Bauzin)]] (alto saxophone) by [[Pierre-Philippe Bauzin|Pierre-Philippe Bauzin]]  (Édition Leduc)
- [[Edison Denisov - Sonata|Sonata (Denisov)]] (alto saxophone) by [[Edison Denisov|Edison Denisov]] (Édition Leduc)
- [[Marcel Mihalovici - Chant Premier, Op. 103|Chant Premier, Op. 103]] (tenor saxophone) by [[Marcel Mihalovici|Marcel Mihalovici]] (Édition Heugel)
- [[William Karlins - Music for Tenor Saxophone and Piano|Music for Tenor Saxophone and Piano]] by [[William Karlins|William Karlins]] (Southern)

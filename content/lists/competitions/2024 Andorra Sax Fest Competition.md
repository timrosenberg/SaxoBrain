## The candidate must submit the following video:

- 24 Caprices for Solo Violin Op. 1, Caprice No. 3 by Niccolò Paganini.

    [Schumann Märchenbilder Op. 113 for Alto Sax.pdf](/media/lists/competitions/2024-andorra-sax-fest-competition/Schumann_Marchenbilder_Op._113_for_Alto_Sax.pdf)

- [[Leonard Bernstein - Sonata|Sonata (Bernstein)]] by [[Leonard Bernstein|Leonard Bernstein]] (second movement)

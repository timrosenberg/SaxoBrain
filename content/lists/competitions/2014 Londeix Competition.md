## The First elimination round:

Each competitor will present three works, two from list 1 and one from list 2:

### List 1

Choice of saxophones at the discretion of the candidate based upon by the character of the Caprices chosen (Editions Zimmerman ([www.zimmermann-frankfurt.de](http://www.zimmermann-frankfurt.de/)) or Southern Music.

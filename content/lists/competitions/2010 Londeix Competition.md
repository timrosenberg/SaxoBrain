## The First eliminatory round

Solo saxophone, maximum time: 20 minutes. Each competitor will present two works, one from each list:

A baroque or a classical composition written before the 20th century transcribed for solo saxophone (the choice of saxophone is at the discretion of the candidate).

### Choose one

- Neuf Études pour saxophone seul by Christian LAUBA (pub. Leduc)
- Hard too Hard (tenor saxophone) by Christian LAUBA (pub. Leduc)
- Etude N° 12 Xyl (Balafon 2) (alto saxophone) by Christian LAUBA (pub. Leduc)
- Work Song (alto saxophone) by Christian LAUBA (pub. Leduc)
- Hard (tenor saxophone) by Christian LAUBA (pub. Fuzeau)
- Oxyton (baritone saxophone) by Christophe HAVEL (pub. P.J. Tonger- Germany)
- Le Frêne Égaré (alto saxophone) by François ROSSÉ (pub. Billaudot)
- Digital (soprano saxophone) by Thierry ALLA (pub. Leduc)

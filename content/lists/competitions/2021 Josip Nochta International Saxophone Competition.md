Repertoire for the third Josip Nochta International Saxophone Competition, Zagreb, June 5–10, 2021.

Sources: [Adolphesax announcement](https://adolphesax.com/en/june-2021-3rd-josip-nochta-international-saxophone-competition-05-06-2021-zagreb-croatia/) and [official 2021 booklet](https://nochta-saxcompetition.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/NOCHTA-2021-booklet.pdf#page=13).

## First Round

Perform two works in this order, within 20 minutes:

1. *[[Johann Sebastian Bach - Six Suites for Violoncello Solo|Cello Suite, BWV 1011: Prelude]]* by [[Johann Sebastian Bach]]. Use an urtext reference; transposition and phrasing are the candidate's choice.
2. One work from the following list:

- *[[Roger Boutry - Divertimento|Divertimento]]* by [[Roger Boutry]] (Leduc).
- *[[Alfred Desenclos - Prelude, Cadence et Finale|Prélude, cadence et finale]]* by [[Alfred Desenclos]] (Leduc).
- *[[Claude Pascal - Sonatine|Sonatine]]* by [[Claude Pascal]] (Durand).
- *[[Lucie Robert-Diessel - Cadenza|Cadenza]]* by [[Lucie Robert-Diessel|Lucie Robert]] (Billaudot).
- *[[Pierre Sancan - Lamento et Rondo|Lamento et Rondo]]* by [[Pierre Sancan]] (Durand).

## Second Round

Perform three works in any order, within 45 minutes:

1. *Portraits* by Krešimir Seletković (Academy of Music, University of Zagreb).
2. One sonata from the following list.
3. A work of the candidate's choice, unaccompanied or with piano.

**Sonata choices:**

- *[[William Albright - Sonata|Sonata]]* by [[William Albright]] (Peters).
- *[[Fernande Decruck - Sonata in C sharp minor|Sonata]]* by [[Fernande Decruck]] (Billaudot).
- *[[Edison Denisov - Sonata|Sonata]]* by [[Edison Denisov]] (Leduc).
- *[[Jindřich Feld - Sonata|Sonata]]* by [[Jindřich Feld]] (Leduc).
- *[[Jeanine Rueff - Sonate|Sonata for Alto Saxophone Solo]]* by [[Jeanine Rueff]] (Leduc).
- *[[Anđelko Klobučar - Sonata|Sonata]]* by [[Anđelko Klobučar]] (HDS).

If the pianist considers one rehearsal insufficient for the choice work, the candidate must propose another piece.

## Final Round

Perform both works in this order:

1. *[[Boris Papandopulo - Concerto for Saxophone and Orchestra|Saxophone Concerto]]* by [[Boris Papandopulo]] (MIC), with the Zagreb Philharmonic Orchestra.
2. *[[Paul Bonneau - Caprice en Forme da Valse|Caprice en forme de valse]]* by [[Paul Bonneau]] (Leduc).

The Scale Series is a saxophone technique builder that covers the most common scales, triads, and seventh chords found in music. Mastery of the series is ***required*** by the end of the third semester.

[Scales Series.pdf](/media/resources/the-scale-series/Scales_Series.pdf)

# The Extended Scale Series

The Extended Scale Series is a *work-in-progress*. It contains less-common scales that are, nonetheless, found in more advanced saxophone repertoire. These exercises can be used to further extend the saxophonist's technique.

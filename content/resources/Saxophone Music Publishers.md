Explore these selected catalogs for recital pieces, studies, and chamber music beyond the works listed in SaxoBrain. Each entry includes a starting point from the existing catalog.

For a particular piece, follow the score links on its work page.

## Alphonse Leduc

A starting point for French recital literature, saxophone studies, and solo music using extended techniques. The catalog includes works by Eugène Bozza, Edison Denisov, Christian Lauba, and Ryo Noda.

Browse: [Leduc saxophone scores at Hal Leonard](https://www.halleonard.com/search/search.action?keywords=saxophone&dt=item&_f%5B%27publisher_facet%27%5D=Alphonse%20Leduc).

## Billaudot

Explore studies and methods alongside contemporary concert pieces. Guy Lacour’s teaching collections and the saxophone collections directed by Jean-Yves Fourmeau and Vincent David offer several routes into the catalog.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog](https://www.billaudot.com/saxophone.html).

## Donemus

A useful place to explore music by Dutch composers, although other countries are represented in the catalog too. Search results often have score previews.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog search](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/search?fulltext=saxophone).

## Henry Lemoine

Explore saxophone teaching collections, recital music with piano, and contemporary unaccompanied writing. Jean Matitia’s character pieces and Bruno Mantovani’s solo repertoire provide contrasting starting points.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog](https://www.henry-lemoine.com/en/131-saxophone).

## Just a Theory Press

A source of contemporary solo and chamber music with filters for individual instruments and ensemble types. Kristin Kuster’s *Jellyfish* is available as a piano score and saxophone part in print or PDF.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog filter](https://justatheorypress.com/collections/all?filter.p.m.custom.instruments_for_filters=Saxophone) · [Publisher website](https://justatheorypress.com/). Use the instrument filters to explore individual members of the saxophone family.

## Murphy Music Press

Dedicated sections separate solo saxophone, saxophone and piano, chamber music, studies, and quartets. Steven Banks’s music provides an entry into both the unaccompanied and piano-accompanied repertoire.

Browse: [Solo saxophone](https://murphymusicpress.com/catalog/solo-saxophone) · [Saxophone and piano](https://murphymusicpress.com/catalog/saxophone-and-piano) · [Quartets](https://murphymusicpress.com/catalog/saxophone-quartets) · [Publisher website](https://murphymusicpress.com/).

## New Consonant Music

An entry into Michel Lysight’s repertoire, including sonatas for alto and baritone saxophone with piano. The broader catalog includes wind and chamber music, with instrumentation listed for each title.

Browse: [Wind music](https://www.newconsonantmusic.com/classes/winds/) · [Full catalog](https://www.newconsonantmusic.com/catalogue/) · [Publisher website](https://www.newconsonantmusic.com/).

## Reed Music

Explore Barry Cockcroft’s solo repertoire alongside music for saxophone and piano, duets, and larger ensembles. The instrument categories make it easy to follow a particular saxophone into different settings.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog](https://www.reedmusic.com/store/woodwind/saxophone/).

## Resolute Music Publications

A useful starting point for Christian Lauba’s concert studies and music by Jean Matitia, Robert Lemay, and David Salleras. The catalog separates individual saxophones, duos, quartets, and chamber combinations.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog](https://resolute-music.com/collections/all-saxophone) · [Publisher website](https://resolute-music.com/).

## Schott Music

A broad catalog spanning concert music, teaching material, arrangements, and play-along collections. Filter by saxophone type and publisher when narrowing a search, since the shop includes several publishing catalogs.

Browse: [Saxophone catalog](https://www.schott-music.com/en/sheet-music/wind-instruments/saxophone.html).

## Theodore Presser Company

Explore concert solos and saxophone-and-piano repertoire by composers such as Stacy Garrop, Carter Pann, and Shulamit Ran. The saxophone section includes filters for accompaniment and available score formats.

Browse: [Saxophone solos and works with accompaniment](https://www.presser.com/tp/solos/woodwinds/saxophone.html).

## Zen-On

A starting point for Japanese concert repertoire, alongside teaching collections and arrangements. The English catalog separates solo saxophone, saxophone with other instruments, and saxophone ensembles.

Browse: [Solo saxophone](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/en/products/search_catalog/?fw=&category%5B%5D=9-6-22-) · [Saxophone with other instruments](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/en/products/search_catalog/?fw=&category%5B%5D=9-6-23-) · [Ensembles](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/en/products/search_catalog/?fw=&category%5B%5D=9-6-25-).

## Research notes (private)

Expanded October 21, 2026. Selection combines the four original publishers with eight additional catalogs that connect to existing SaxoBrain works. Descriptions summarize publisher catalogs and product pages. They do not assign difficulty or claim that every edition is currently in stock.

The public browse links are also sources for the catalog descriptions. Additional primary evidence for the featured works:

- **Alphonse Leduc:** Hal Leonard’s filtered publisher catalog lists Denisov’s Sonata, Bozza, Lauba, and Noda. [Improvisation I](https://www.halleonard.com/product/48183189/improvisation-i) identifies Alphonse Leduc as publisher and solo alto saxophone as the instrumentation.
- **Billaudot:** [Lacour, volume 1, saxophone edition](https://www.billaudot.com/50-etudes-faciles-et-progressives-volume-1-1.html) and [Lauba, Steady study on the boogie](https://www.billaudot.com/gb5692-6.html). The catalog describes teaching and contemporary repertoire and names the collection directors.
- **Donemus:** [People and Things, D20245](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/20245) confirms violin and soprano saxophone. [Fracture IV, D13451](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/13451) confirms solo baritone. The fulltext=saxophone search was tested in the browser and returns saxophone results through a shareable GET URL.
- **Henry Lemoine:** [Solo, D1671](https://www.henry-lemoine.com/en/partitions-par-instrument/21031-solo-pdf.html) confirms soprano saxophone and publisher Lemoine. [Publisher saxophone catalog, 2024 PDF](https://www.henry-lemoine.com/assets/pdf/CATASAX.pdf), indexed text, lists Matitia’s Au bonheur des dames. The current instrument category was opened in the browser.
- **Just a Theory Press:** [Jellyfish](https://justatheorypress.com/products/jellyfish) confirms alto saxophone and piano, score and part, print and PDF. The Saxophone filter URL was obtained by checking the catalog’s instrument filter. Individual alto, soprano, tenor, and baritone filters are separate choices.
- **Murphy Music Press:** [Come As You Are, S-152](https://murphymusicpress.com/products/s-152) confirms tenor saxophone and piano. The solo saxophone category lists Salleras Variation by Steven Banks. Replaced the old Composers List link, which duplicated the homepage, with instrument categories.
- **New Consonant Music:** [Alto sonata](https://www.newconsonantmusic.com/catalogue/alto-saxophone-sonata/) and [baritone sonata](https://www.newconsonantmusic.com/catalogue/sonata-for-baritone-saxophone-and-piano/) confirm the two instrumentations. Winds is the publisher’s broader category, rather than a saxophone-only catalog.
- **Reed Music:** The current woodwind store links to the saxophone category and lists Cockcroft’s [Ku Ku](https://www.reedmusic.com/store/woodwind/saxophone/saxophone-solo/sax-solo-soprano/ku-ku/). The existing [Beat Me edition](https://www.reedmusic.com/store/woodwind/saxophone/saxophone-solo/sax-solo-tenor/beat-me/) is for solo tenor.
- **Resolute:** [Bebop](https://resolute-music.com/products/bebop-etude-22) confirms Lauba, solo alto, and étude number 22. The publisher homepage lists [Samba, étude 31](https://resolute-music.com/products/samba-etude-31), plus Matitia, Lemay, and Salleras. Category links separate solo instruments and ensembles.
- **Schott:** [Sarabande, ED12417](https://www.schott-music.com/en/sarabande-noc42226.html) and [Pièce concertante, ED5893](https://www.schott-music.com/de/piece-concertante-noc36087.html) identify Schott editions and confirm the featured instrumentations. The saxophone catalog exposes publisher and instrument filters.
- **Presser:** The saxophone category was opened in the browser and lists Garrop, Pann, and Ran, with accompaniment and format filters. [Fragmented Spirit, 114-41106](https://www.presser.com/114-41106-fragmented-spirit.html) confirms Theodore Presser Company and alto saxophone with piano.
- **Zen-On:** The current English product directory supplies the three category URLs. Its saxophone-with-other-instruments category lists Dreaming Stone for A(/T)-SAX & piano. [Halahala, 560029](https://shop.zen-on.co.jp/item/detail/560029/) confirms Nishimura and solo alto saxophone.

Several sites blocked the text fetcher but loaded in the browser. Universal Edition was considered but left out after its browser page presented an access challenge. No prices, delivery promises, difficulty classifications, or catalog-size claims were copied into the public guide.

### Alto:

- Charlie Parker: Brilhart Tonalin Streamline (White), Brilhart Ebolin, Runyon Model 22
- Cannonball Adderley: Meyer NY 5
- Phil Woods: Meyer NY 5M
- Gerald Albright: Beechler Studio Diamond Inlay Hard Rubber 8
- Miguel Zenon: Meyer 5M
- Steve Coleman: Vandoren A55
- Paul Desmond: Gregory 4A-18M
- Paquito D’rivera: Selmer F Metal
- Kenny Garret: Selmer E
- Vinent Herring: Meyer NY 5M
- Dave Koz: Beechler 7 Metal
- Dick Oatts: Berg Larsen 90/2
- Maceo Parker: Brilhart Ebolin

### Tenor:

- Chris Potter: Otto Link FL or Otto Link No USA 8*
- Joshua Redman: Otto Link Hard Rubber 7 or 7*
- Bob Mintzer: [Raphael Navarro Bob Mintzer BeBop Special](http://rafaelnavarro.com/#/tenor/bob-mintzer)
- Michael Brecker: [Guardala MB I with a smooth baffle](http://www.neffmusic.com/blog/2016/07/will-the-real-michael-breckers-sax-mouthpiece-please-stand-up/)
- Jeff Coffin: Freddie Gregory Custom HR tenor mouthpiece
- Seamus Blake: [Ted Klum FocusTone Tonamax HR Tenor 8*](https://www.tedklum.com/focustone-tonamax-hr-tenor/)
- Mark Turner: [Ted Klum Florida Model Hard Rubber](https://www.tedklum.com/florida-model-hard-rubber-tenor/)
- Joel Frahm: [Marantz Slant Legacy HR](https://www.marantzcustommouthpieces.com/product-page/slant-legacy-hr-tenor-sax-jet-black)
- Dexter Gordon: various Otto Links (at the beginning of his career he used a 4 or 5, but in later years was seen using an 8)
- John Coltrane: various Otto Link Tonemasters
- Stan Getz: Otto Link Tone Edge 5*
- Joe Lovano: [François Louis T 350](http://francois-louis.com/mouthpieces/saxophone-tenor-mouthpiece)
- James Carter: Lawton Metal 7* BB
- Clarence Clemons: Dukoff D9, D11
- Dennis Diblasio: Lawton 8* B
- Joe Henderson: Semer Soloist D (Short Shank refaced to H or I?)
- James Moody: RPC 150M
- Lenny Pickett: Berg Larsen 130/0 SMS
- Sonny Rollins: Berg Larsen 130/2
- Chris Vadala: Otto Link 8

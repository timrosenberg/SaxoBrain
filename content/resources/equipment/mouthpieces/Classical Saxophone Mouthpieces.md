There is an enormous, and growing, selection of classical saxophone mouthpieces. Choosing one is a personal experiment. Each player will respond differently to each mouthpiece. Here are some that I recommend:

(⭐ denotes Dr. Rosenberg’s current equipment)

There are some obvious selections that are missing from this list. I haven't had an opportunity to try every mouthpiece available. However, if I do find others that I like, I will update the list.

Tip opening is the gap between the reed tip and the mouthpiece tip. Facing length is the distance from the tip to where the reed meets the flat table. Manufacturers publish either a length in millimetres or a relative category such as Medium Long. These categories are retained as published.

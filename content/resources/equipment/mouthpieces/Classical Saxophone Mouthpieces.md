There is an enormous, and growing, selection of classical saxophone mouthpieces. Choosing one is a personal experiment. Each player will respond differently to each mouthpiece. Here are some that I recommend:

(⭐ denotes Dr. Rosenberg’s current equipment)

There are some obvious selections that are missing from this list. I haven't had an opportunity to try every mouthpiece available. However, if I do find others that I like, I will update the list.

Tip opening is the gap between the reed tip and the mouthpiece tip. Facing length is the distance from the tip to where the reed meets the flat table. Manufacturers publish either a length in millimetres or a relative category such as Medium Long. These categories are retained as published.

Specifications checked October 21, 2026. [Compare models side by side](/resources/equipment/mouthpieces/saxophone-mouthpiece-comparisons/).

## Soprano Saxophone

- [Selmer S90 170](https://www.meridianwinds.com/shop/c/p/Selmer-S90-Series-Soprano-Saxophone-Mouthpieces-x38304560.htm)

	Tip opening: **1.07 mm** · Facing: **18 mm**.

	Focused. Slightly bright when pushed.

- ⭐ [Selmer Concept](https://www.amazon.com/Selmer-Paris-Concept-Saxophone-Mouthpiece/dp/B010IFVGHC)

	Tip opening: **1.06 mm** · Facing: **21 mm**.

	Dark and focused. Narrow tip can feel stuffy.

- [Vandoren SL3](https://www.amazon.com/Vandoren-SM701-Optimum-Saxophone-Mouthpiece/dp/B000EEJBB6?tag=timothyrosenb-20)

	Tip opening: **1.14 mm** · Facing: **Medium Long**.

	Bright. Comfortable to play.

- [Vandoren S27](https://www.prowinds.com/product/SM401/Vandoren-Mouthpieces)

	Tip opening: **1.11 mm** · Facing: **Medium**.

	Colorful. Verging on bright at times. Needs strong reeds.

- [Playnick LM2](http://www.innoledy.com/saxophone_supp-msps_playnick.html)

	Specifications unknown for soprano.

	More open than LM1, and darker. Not a 1:1 comparison to the alto models.

## Alto Saxophone

- [Selmer S90 180](https://www.amazon.com/Selmer-hard-rubber-alto-saxophone/dp/B000RVYNBE/ref=sr_1_1?tag=timothyrosenb-20)

	Tip opening: **1.45 mm** · Facing: **24 mm**.

	Focused and clear; but, needs finesse to avoid brightness.

- [Selmer S80 C**](https://www.kesslerandsons.com/product/selmer-s80-alto-sax-mouthpiece/)

	Tip opening: **1.65 mm** · Facing: **24 mm**.

	Comfortable and easy to play. Can get a edgy when pushed.

- [Selmer Concept](https://www.amazon.com/SELMER-CONCEPT-Saxophone-saxophone-mouthpiece/dp/B00DUWMRYW/ref=pd_sbs_267_1/132-4838070-3234209?tag=timothyrosenb-20)

	Tip opening: **1.52 mm** · Facing: **24 mm**.

	Dark. Slightly stuffy due to narrow tip.

- [Selmer Claude Delangle](https://www.prowinds.com/product/11830/New-Products-from-Selmer-Paris)

	Tip opening: **1.65 mm** · Facing: **24 mm**.

	Dark and focused. Comfortable to play. *Everyone* sounds good on this (except me).

- [Vandoren AL3](https://www.amazon.com/Vandoren-SM711-Optimum-Saxophone-Mouthpiece/dp/B0002D0KR8/ref=sr_1_1?tag=timothyrosenb-20)

	Tip opening: **1.52 mm** · Facing: **Medium Long**.

	Dark, round sound. Can feel stuffy. Limited in loudness for some players.

- [Vandoren AL5](https://www.amazon.com/Vandoren-SM711-Optimum-Saxophone-Mouthpiece/dp/B00DOS8RLO/ref=sr_1_1?tag=timothyrosenb-20)

	Tip opening: **1.68 mm** · Facing: **Medium Long**.

	An "improved" AL3. Can still get a little strident when pushed. Easy to play.

- [D’Addario Reserve D150](https://www.amazon.com/DAddario-Woodwinds-Saxophone-Mouthpiece-MJR-D150/dp/B07B16J3N3/ref=sr_1_1?tag=timothyrosenb-20)

	Tip opening: **1.50 mm** · Facing: **Medium Long**.

	Focused and colorful.

- ⭐ [Playnick LM1](http://www.innoledy.com/saxophone_supp-msps_playnick.html)

	Tip opening: **1.60 mm** · Facing: **Not published**.

	Dark, round sound. Colorful when pushed. Easy to play.

- [Backun Vocalise TM2](https://backunmusical.com/products/vocalise-alto-saxophone-mouthpieces?variant=40040947089493)

	Tip opening: **1.53 mm** · Facing: **Not published**.

	Dark round sound. Easy to play.

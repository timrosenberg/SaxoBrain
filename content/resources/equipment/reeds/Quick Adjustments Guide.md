# Too Soft

- Area: Tip
- Tool: Trimmer
- Clip small amount. Test after each clip.

# Buzzy or edgy

- Area: Tip
- Tool: Trimmer
- Clip small amount. Test after each clip.

# Lack of resonance

- Area: 1 & 2
- Tool: Rush
- Balance.

# Dull sound when playing softly

![](/media/resources/equipment/reeds/quick-adjustments-guide/Saxophone_Reed_Adjustments.png)

- Area: 1 & 2
- Tool: Rush
- Balance. Take more of both sides if still too hard.

# Blows Hard

- Area: 2
- Tool: Rush
- Thin both sides and balance.

# Lower register lacks resonance

- Area: 2
- Tool: Rush
- Balance and thin if necessary.

# Tip too thick after clipping.

- Area: Underside of tip
- Tool: Sandpaper
- Lay sandpaper on glass and stroke lightly with grain on the flat table side of reed to about 3/8 of an inch back from tip.

# Reed whistles

- Area: 2
- Tool: Rush
- Balance.

# High tones difficult to attack

- Area: 2 & 1
- Tool: Rush
- Thin gradually with a light stroke.

# Thin high register

- Area: 3
- Tool: Rush
- Test after each few strokes.

# Lacks projection in upper register

- Area: 3
- Tool: Rush
- Move three back from the tip. This may shorten the life of the reed.

# Lack of resonance in middle register

- Area: 4 & 3
- Tool: Rush
- Lightly on 3.

# Heavy low register

- Area: 6
- Tool: scraping knife
- Finish with rush.

# General lack of resonance

- Area: 7 & 8
- Tool: Sandpaper on glass
- Sand rails of reed if reed is too wide for the mouthpiece.

# After balancing, reed plays well but blows hard

- Area: 6, 5, 4, 3
- Tool: Scraper
- Evenly thin all areas.

# Table not flat

- Area: Table
- Tool: razor blade
- Stroke lightly towards the tip.

# Table not smooth

- Area: Table
- Tool: Sandpaper on glass
- Rub lightly back and forth, always in the direction of the grain.

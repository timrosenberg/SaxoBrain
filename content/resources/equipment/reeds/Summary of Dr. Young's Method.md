- Date, number, tape over
- Wet reed for about 30 seconds
- Play for about 30 seconds
- Mark on heel indicating it was played once
- Store
- Make notes
- after 5 markings, play 5 minutes for 3-4 days
- then, 10 mins for 3-4 days
- select players

## Rules

- do not play on a reed two days in a row
- only play a reed for 45 mins to 1 hour

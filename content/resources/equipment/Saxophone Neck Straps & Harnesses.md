The most important things about a neck strap or harness are:

1. It is comfortable to wear.
2. **It does not stretch**.

Point #2 **rules out** the very popular Neotech neck strap. Below is a list of excellent neck straps and harnesses that adhere to both rules. (List is not in any particular order.)

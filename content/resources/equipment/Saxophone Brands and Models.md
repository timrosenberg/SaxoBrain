## Alto Saxophones

### [Yamaha YAS-875 EX II Custom Series](https://usa.yamaha.com/products/musical_instruments/winds/saxophones/yas-875exii/index.html)

- Laser-guided, precision-made in Japan
- Consistent tone: dark and warm
- Easy to control
- Easy intonation

### [Selmer Paris Supreme 92DL](https://www.selmer.fr/en-int/products/saxophone-alto-supreme)

- Hand-made in Paris, France
- Colorful tone, easy expression
- Easy to control

### [Selmer Paris Series III Model 62J (a.k.a. Jubilee Edition)](https://www.selmer.fr/en/products/saxophone-alto-serie-iii)

- Hand-made in Paris, France
- Colorful tone, easy expression
- More difficult to control
- Less consistent from one to another – all are a bit different
- **Discontinued**; but, can be found used.

### [Yanagisawa WO20](https://www.yanagisawasax.co.jp/en/saxophones/view/97)

- Brand owned by Selmer
- Factory made in Japan
- Colorful tone; made from bronze

### [Buffet Crampon SENZO](https://www.buffetcrampon.com/en-us/products/senzo-alto-saxophone-red-copper-varnish-bc2525-7b-0)

- Made from "Red Brass" (higher copper content in the alloy)
- Body made in France; key-work made by Keilwerth in Germany; assembled in China.
- Very accurate intonation
- Dark tone
- Easy to control

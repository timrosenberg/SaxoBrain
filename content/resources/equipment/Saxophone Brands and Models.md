## Alto Saxophones

- [Yamaha YAS-875 EX II Custom Series](https://www.amazon.com/Yamaha-YAS-875EXII-Custom-Saxophone-Lacquer/dp/B0164S4TG2?tag=timothyrosenb-20) - $4,200
    - Laser-guided, precision-made in Japan
    - Consistent tone: dark and warm
    - Easy to control
    - Easy intonation
- [Selmer Paris Supreme 92DL](https://www.prowinds.com/product/12356/New-Products-from-Selmer-Paris) - $7,600
    - Hand-made in Paris, France
    - Colorful tone, easy expression
    - Easy to control
- [Selmer Paris Series III Model 62J (a.k.a. Jubilee Edition)](https://www.amazon.com/Selmer-Paris-Jubilee-Saxophone-62J/dp/B0064S9JMC/ref=sr_1_1?tag=timothyrosenb-20) - $6,800
    - Hand-made in Paris, France
    - Colorful tone, easy expression
    - More difficult to control
    - Less consistent from one to another – all are a bit different
- [Yanagisawa WO20](https://www.wwbw.com/Yanagisawa-WO20-Series-Alto-Saxophone-J12181-J12181000002000.wwbw) - $5,100
    - Brand owned by Selmer
    - Factory made in Japan
    - Colorful tone; made from bronze
- [Buffet Crampon SENZO](https://www.amazon.com/Buffet-Crampon-Senzo-Brass-Saxophone/dp/B00H6DEYUG/ref=sr_1_2?tag=timothyrosenb-20) - $5,500
    - Made from "Red Brass" (higher copper content in the alloy)
    - Body made in France; key-work made by Keilwerth in Germany; assembled in China.
    - Very accurate intonation
    - Dark tone
    - Easy to control

The following list is a short, and by no means exhaustive set of saxophone-focused readings for students to study. The list is also represented in the [Saxophone Curriculum](/curriculum/).

# First Year

## Required Reading

- [Fall] [[Teaching Students How to Practice by Steven Mauk|Teaching Students How to Practice by Steven Mauk]]
- [Fall] [[8 Practice Hacks by Noa Kageyama|8 Practice Hacks by Noa Kageyama]]
- [Spring] A Jazz Guide to Practicing: How to Get the Most Out of Your Practice Sessions by Brent Vaartstra
    
    [Vaartstra - A Jazz Guide to Practicing.pdf](/media/reading/Vaartstra_-_A_Jazz_Guide_to_Practicing.pdf)
    
    - Not just for jazz students. The guide is great for practicing any kind of music.
- [Spring] Quality Practice by Susan Williams
    
    [Williams - Quality Practice A Musician's Guide.pdf](/media/reading/Williams_-_Quality_Practice_A_Musicians_Guide.pdf)
    

# Second Year

## Required Reading

- **[Fall]** *The Cambridge Companion to the Saxophone*, [Ch. 1: “Invention and Development”](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uVsRFN0yTdyckvEe5waRIEPD0eOizObt/view?usp=drivesdk) by Thomas Liley
    
    [Liley - The Cambridge Companion to the Saxophone Ch. 1.pdf](/media/reading/Liley-The-Cambridge-Companion-to-the-Saxophone-Ch.-1.pdf)
    
- **[Spring]** *The Cambridge Companion to the Saxophone*, [Ch. 2: “In the Twentieth Century”](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19FgBDUPgr_7k5cCH97jgSFMlaI_U8qQm/view?usp=drivesdk) by Don Ashton
    
    [Ashton - The Cambridge Companion to the Saxophone Ch. 2.pdf](/media/reading/Ashton-The-Cambridge-Companion-to-the-Saxophone-Ch.-2.pdf)

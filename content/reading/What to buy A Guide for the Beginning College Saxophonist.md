Sometimes, parents ask me what they should buy their new college saxophone students to help them be successful in college. It is such an expensive time!! I have compiled a little list that I hope will be helpful. Keep in mind--most of this stuff is "extra" and you can make do without it, so approach this as "stocking stuffers" that you (or grandparents) could get as special gifts, or even something to save up for. Academic Saxophone friends, what do you suggest? Saxophone students, what have you found helpful/useful that I left off the list?

# Clothing

1. A good black suit / pantsuit or a full-length black dress
2. Both white and black dress shirts with long sleeves
    - Price: $150—$500. You can sometimes find these used at thrift shops. (I’d keep it to the lower end—concert black is more of a prop than a fashion statement!)
    - A black suit can step in as a tuxedo with the addition of a bowtie, and can serve as attire for most concerts on campus, whether the requirement is “pit black,” which is solid black, or “concert black,” which is tuxedo and gown.
    - Those who identify as women often wear slacks onstage. In this case their concert attire for pit black and concert black are the same.
3. Black dress shoes 
4. Black socks or black stockings

# New Saxophones and Accessories

In the market for a new axe!? That is WONDERFUL! **My suggestion is to contact the saxophone professor at your university FIRST**. We often have connections that can potentially save you money, and can help guide you to the equipment that will be right for you. I have seen people purchase an instrument before college that turns out to have serious intonation issues, or a price tag that is way higher than it needs to be. Mouthpieces, too, are very personal, and it is best to have a specialist help you select the one that will help you get to the next level in your playing.

Unfortunately, the market is flooded with cheap saxophones and “knock-off” accessories. But, most saxophone players avoid these due to the poor quality of materials, craftsmanship, and quality control. ## Reeds

You will likely be using more reeds that you have in your entire life. In college, you are playing all the time, so you will go through them quickly. **Budget around $60 per month for reeds.** Some months you won’t hit that number—those are great months—but some you will exceed it, especially if you are playing multiple saxophones in the semester.

## A Saxophone Bag

I like a small messenger bag that I can put all my accessories into. In college, you often have to play multiple instruments for various ensembles. If you put all your folders, most of your reeds, pencils, tuner, metronome, speaker, and various other important items in one back, you can develop the habit of having it with you whenever you play. I lost things constantly as an 18-20-year-old; so, I would also suggest you embrace technology and put an AirTag or Tile in it in case it gets misplaced so you can find it quickly.

# A Notebook & Calendar

College-life *will* become over-whelming. That’s part of the deal. Your teachers will challenge you more than you have ever been challenged. Your personal-life will become more complex. Your days will get longer and more full. You need a way to keep track of it all, and relying on your brain is not going to work.

You can use any notebook you want, as long as you *use* it. It can be a paper book, digital notes, index cards, post-it notes, *whatever*. The point is that it’s something you will keep with you and use often.

You also need a calendar. Here, I recommend using a digital one because of how easy it is to enter and move things around as needed. The one that comes with your smart phone is fine. There’s *thousands* of apps if you need something different. Again, the most important thing is that you will use *daily*. My calendar is the only thing that separates my life from pure chaos. As I like to say, “If it’s not on my calendar, then I’m not doing it.” 

Adopt a practice of note-taking and planning your schedule *right away*. You will be glad you did.

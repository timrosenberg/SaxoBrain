The concerto has two accompaniment versions: **Fire**, for strings, piano and timpani, and **Flood**, for wind ensemble. The score links above are for **Fire**.

A solo tenor saxophone extract from the third movement is published separately as [[David Keeffe - Spring Wind|Spring Wind]].

### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0382 - Vittorio Monti - Czardas|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Vittorio Monti - Czardas]]

- Identity: Monti’s Czárdás has several distinct saxophone arrangements. The inspected Tierolff edition 3515SA is an E-flat alto-saxophone solo with piano or symphonic band, arranged by Alain Crépin; its sample is not interchangeable with the Lemoine/Muto, Carl Fischer/Roberts, Darok/Kovács, or Uetz/Terrett entries in the catalog.
- Lemoine 25210 is an E-flat saxophone/piano transcription by Ken-Ichiro Muto, 8-page score plus 3-page part; retailer labels elementary. [LMI retailer](https://www.lmi-partitions.com/produit/czardas-4/) (search_index_only).
- Carl Fischer W1711 is arranged by Charles J. Roberts, alto saxophone/piano, originally Monti's violin/piano work. [Ficks Music retailer](https://www.ficksmusic.com/en-ca/products/monti-csardas-czardas-arr-for-alto-sax-piano-fischer) (search_index_only).
- Darok edition is titled for alto saxophone and piano and edited by Béla Kovács. [Rózsavölgyi music shop](https://webaruhaz.rozsavolgyi.hu/hu/kotta/zongora-2-zongora-orgona-harmonium/czardas-for-alto-saxophone-piano-edited-by-bela-kovacs-1) (search_index_only).
- Tierolff lists a distinct alto/piano edition arranged by Alain Crépin and provides sample score images. [Tierolff publisher/retailer](https://tierolff.nl/en/csardas-3515sa.html) (search_index_only).
- Tierolff identifies its 3515SA edition as E-flat alto saxophone with symphonic band or piano, arranged by Alain Crépin; the publisher page provides a two-page score sample. [Tierolff publisher](https://tierolff.nl/en/csardas-3515sa.html) (opened).
- The inspected two-page sample is titled for E-flat alto saxophone and symphonic band or piano; the opening is marked Largo in 2/4, with the saxophone initially resting while piano chords sound, followed by sustained/slurred tones. The second page shows rapid scalar figuration and sextuplets in the saxophone part, with repeated tempo changes and rallentandos. [Tierolff score sample](https://tierolff.nl/media/unicode/unifile/attach/csardas-3515sa.pdf) (pp. 1-2; score_inspected).
- Score observation: In the inspected Tierolff/Crépin sample, the saxophone rests through the opening Largo chords before entering softly with a short slurred figure. Page 2 has rapid ascending and descending scalar runs, sextuplets, and high ledger-line notes; the piano part is chordal with frequent rests. [Score](https://tierolff.nl/media/unicode/unifile/attach/csardas-3515sa.pdf)
- Score link: [Tierolff/Alain Crépin two-page sample for the E-flat alto saxophone edition with piano or symphonic band.](https://tierolff.nl/media/unicode/unifile/attach/csardas-3515sa.pdf) (notation inspected).
- Collection/use: Arrangement of Monti's violin/piano work; multiple saxophone arrangements with distinct editors and editions.
- Review focus: Inference from this specific preview: review the transitions from the slow opening to quick scalar and sextuplet passages, plus coordination around repeated tempo changes and rallentandos.
- Gaps: Only the Tierolff/Crépin sample was inspected; no score pages were viewed for the Lemoine/Muto, Carl Fischer/Roberts, Darok/Kovács, or Uetz/Terrett variants.; No performance recording was verified or auditioned.

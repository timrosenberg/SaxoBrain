## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** [[Hitomi Kaneko]]: La Bulle

**Findings**

- Zen-On confirms La Bulle, composed 2001, alto saxophone and piano, commissioned for its 30 November 2001 Casals Hall Tokyo concert and premiered by Nobuya Sugawa and Minako Koyanagi. Score preface dedicates the work to Sugawa.
- Publisher work catalog gives 9:30, while score preface and digital shop say approximately 10 minutes. First notation page carries copyright 2002, distinct from composition/premiere 2001.
- Publisher number 560009, ISMN 9790223317979; the current digital edition is a 32-page PDF. EAM links exact performance by the premiere duo.
- Public preview includes an English notation legend and actual saxophone staves, permitting concrete observations.

**Notation evidence:** Notation legend defines timed groups played as fast as possible, variable rests after rapid groups, expanding/contracting beam spacing for acceleration and deceleration, pitched breath sound and highest possible tone. Printed pages 5–6 begin at quarter note 60 with repeated sustained four-note saxophone multiphonics, pp sempre, while piano contributes very soft high-register figures. Printed page 7 shows saxophone fast rising sextuplet figures, large register displacement, a later long low sustained tone, and dynamic growth from pp through mf and f. These are observations of the excerpt, not a full-work range audit.

**Notation inspected:** Yes, limited to the pages described.

### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0115 - Gino Samyn - Le Club des Notes|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Gino Samyn - Le Club des Notes]].

Gino Samyn, Le Club des Notes, for alto saxophone and piano, Lemoine according to the opened École de musique Vincent-d’Indy saxophone program. The separate L’Îlot distribution-catalog lead gives 27783HL, not verified in the opened school program.
[L’Îlot distribution catalogue](https://store-factory-medias.s3.eu-west-3.amazonaws.com/www.lilot-parts.com/media/catalogue_distribution_au_08_avril_2015_j-z.pdf) (search_index_only): Billaudot distributor catalogue lists Gino Samyn’s Le Club des Notes for alto saxophone and piano, Lemoine 27783HL.
[École de musique Vincent-d’Indy program](https://emvi.qc.ca/upload/files/20240929-Programme-de-formation-artistique-a-linstrument-SAXOPHONE.pdf) (opened, PDF p. 25 (printed p. 11)): The opened École de musique Vincent-d’Indy saxophone program lists Gino Samyn, Le club des notes, published as sheet music by Lemoine. The document states that the program is intended for teachers affiliated with the school and contributes to preparation for college music studies.

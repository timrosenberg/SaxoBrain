[[Review 0318 - René Duclos - Piece Breve|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[René Duclos - Piece Breve]]

- Identity: René Duclos's Pièce brève is an E-flat alto saxophone and piano work, Leduc AL20943 (1951). The Philharmonie guide describes three connected sections: melodic Allegretto in 6/8, Allegretto scherzando in 9/8, then a return to Allegretto; it lists low C to high E, a 3-minute duration, and fifth-year use in its conservatory sequence. The pitch convention for the range is unspecified.

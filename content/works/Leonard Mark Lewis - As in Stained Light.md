## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Leonard Mark Lewis: As in Stained Light

**Findings**

- Commissioned by Kenneth Tse and premiered by Tse with Lewis at the piano in March 2002 at the North American Saxophone Alliance conference in Denton.2003 in library records is publication copyright, distinct from the documented 2002 premiere.
- Dedicated to Kenneth Tse, for alto saxophone and piano. Composer sells the edition directly; the current linked PDF and existing older PDF are both one-page previews.
- Tse's 2009 masterclass article includes composer's discussion of ABACABA rondo form, hocket interplay, climactic D-flat cadence, and optional octave displacement of difficult late altissimo. It also lists two corrections: m 63 beat 3 second eighth=B-natural; m 161 accent moved to second eighth of beat 2.
- Recorded on CrystalCD657, An American Exhibition, by Kenneth Tse and Mami Nagai. A separate 2009 masterclass recording pairs Tse with Lewis.

**Notation evidence:** Opening page: Allegro quarter=126, common time, seven-sharp alto key signature against four-sharp piano; sax enters molto legato with upper-register sustained notes and a triplet figure. Piano carries continuous alternating chordal sixteenths marked marcato while saxophone shapes long lines with hairpins. This page supports tonal reading, upper-register control and ensemble pulse observations, not full range.

**Notation inspected:** Yes, limited to the pages described.

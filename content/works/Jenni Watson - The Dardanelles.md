## Research notes (private)

Checked 2026-10-27. [Composer’s page](https://www.jenniwatson.com/shop/the-dardanelles) supplies the edition and listening sources.

Difficulty estimate: **Third Year**, provisional. Samples from all four movements were inspected. Malta has sustained quiet phrasing; Search for Ephesus has flowing sixteenths at quarter note 142; Yalta combines lyrical lines and ornamental runs; Istanbul uses 14/8, triplets and chromatic figures. The complete suite requires control across three saxophones. Third Year is provisional, with moderate confidence. Samples carry a 2013 joint copyright, which is not asserted as a composition date.

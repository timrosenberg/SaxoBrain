## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** [[Kenji Sakai]]: In a Haze

**Findings**

- In a Haze 2018, for two alto saxophones,10 min, commissioned Naomi Shirai; premiered 21 December 2018, Rexxam Hall Kagawa, Naomi Shirai and Masanori Oishi.
- Composer describes stereo interaction that begins in coordination and grows opposed; latter half quotes dawn arpeggios from Ravel Daphnis et Chloé.
- Zen-On 509549, ISMN9790223332620, ISBN9784115095496; publisher catalog 15 May 2024 publication,16 score+8 x 2 part pages. Retailer 2025 date should not replace this publisher date.
- Publisher identifies recording OVCC-00160; label album page verifies Naomi Shirai and Masanori Oishi; no individual streaming track URL located.

**Notation evidence:** Inspected legend defines slap, key noise with a little breath, bisbigliando measured or fast, harmonics, non-vibrato, quarter tones and two multiphonic fingerings. Opening printedp 6,2/4 quarter 60, two alto parts closely aligned. Accented very short attacks alternate with pp slurred notes and hairpins; rhythmic figures expand into triplet, quintuplet and thirty-second-note subdivisions. Bisbigliando and non-vibrato instructions appear.

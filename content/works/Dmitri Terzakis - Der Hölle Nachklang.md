### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0077 - Dmitri Terzakis - Der Hölle Nachklang|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Dmitri Terzakis - Der Hölle Nachklang]]

- Identity: Dimitri Terzakis's *Der Hölle Nachklang I* is a 1992 alto saxophone and piano work published by Edition Gravis; the University of Macedonia repository dates the edition to 1994 and lists separate saxophone and piano PDFs. The repository denied file access, so the score notation was not inspected. The Hellenic Saxophone Library gives a 10-minute duration, a 1992 premiere by John-Edward Kelly and Bob Versteegh, and a detailed technical profile.
- University of Macedonia's repository record identifies the title, E-flat alto saxophone and piano, 1994 publication, Edition Gravis, two score files, and CC BY-NC 4.0; direct file request returned an authorization block. [University of Macedonia, Psepheda repository](https://dspace.lib.uom.gr/handle/2159/27370) (opened).
- Specialist library gives 10-minute duration, 1992 premiere by John-Edward Kelly and Bob Versteegh, graduate-level placement in its scheme, and educational aims including altissimo, microtones, dynamic range, rhythmic variety, legato, and vibrato. [Hellenic Saxophone Library](https://hellenicsax.gr/works/359_der_holle_nachklang_i) (search_index_only).
- Label page lists the 1992 work on the 1997 album *New Saxophone Chamber Music*, performed by Kelly and Versteegh, and lists track duration 9:55. [col legno label page](https://www.col-legno.com/en/shop/31891-kelly-versteegh-new-saxophone-chamber-music) (search_index_only).
- Score link: [Repository-listed alto saxophone PDF, but the repository returned Authorization Required](https://dspace.lib.uom.gr/bitstream/2159/27370/1/alto_saxophone.pdf) (notation not inspected).
- Score link: [Repository-listed piano PDF, not opened](https://dspace.lib.uom.gr/bitstream/2159/27370/2/piano.pdf) (notation not inspected).
- Recording: [Commercial album track, 9:55, John-Edward Kelly saxophone and Bob Versteegh piano](https://www.col-legno.com/en/shop/31891-kelly-versteegh-new-saxophone-chamber-music) track and performers identified; not auditioned
- Collection/use: The specialist library places it at graduate solo level in its own scheme and identifies a commission by the Eurythmie-Ensembles Amsterdam; this is use/provenance evidence, not a SaxoBrain level.
- Review focus: Inference from the specialist library's educational aims: inspect microtonal writing, altissimo demands, and control of the listed dynamic range if a score copy becomes available. The complete score remains inaccessible at the repository URL.
- Gaps: Repository metadata lists separate part PDFs, but its access block prevented score inspection; do not attempt to bypass it.; No recording playback or detailed college program was inspected; technical information comes from the specialist library's guide.

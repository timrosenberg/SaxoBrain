### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0191 - Jeroen d' Hoe - Prayer|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Jeroen d' Hoe - Prayer]].

There are two Donemus products. No. 20960 is the 2019 original for violin, alto saxophone and piano. No. 22176 is a December 2022 arrangement of that trio for violin, B-flat clarinet and piano. The current SaxoBrain research link points to 22176, while its instrument field names saxophone; use 20960 to support the saxophone scoring, and keep the edition mismatch visible.
[Donemus publisher catalog](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/20960/Prayer) (opened): Donemus identifies the trio instrumentation, 2019 composition/revision, 8:30 duration, Kugoni Trio commission and the meditative, alternating melody with steady accompaniment.
[Donemus publisher catalog](https://webshop.donemus.nl/action/front/sheetmusic/21328/Prayer) (search_index_only): Donemus also lists a 2021 revised version for violin, alto saxophone, piano and strings, originally commissioned for Kugoni Trio and Ataneres Ensemble.
[Donemus 20960 public score preview](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/20960/Prayer) (score_inspected, PDF pp. 1–7; notation observations from score pages): Donemus 20960 is the 2019 original trio for violin, alto saxophone and piano, commissioned by Kugoni Trio; its seven-page public PDF preview includes the cover/copyright and score pages, of which the opening and a later passage were visually inspected.
[Donemus 22176 public score preview](https://webshop.donemus.nl/action/front/sheetmusic/22176/Prayer) (score_inspected, PDF pp. 2–3): The current SaxoBrain link points to Donemus 22176, a December 2022 arrangement for violin, B-flat clarinet and piano of the 2019 saxophone/violin/piano trio; the four-page preview visibly shows those three instruments.

Score observations:
- The 20960 public preview opens in 6/4 at quarter=64 with violin and alto saxophone resting while the piano sustains a softly marked bell-like texture. At section C, bar 9, saxophone enters in a quiet sustained, slurred phrase marked quasi senza vibrato and dolcissimo espressivo. A later preview page continues with long tied tones and sparse entrances against repeated piano arpeggiated figures. These observations apply to the saxophone version 20960, not the clarinet arrangement 22176.

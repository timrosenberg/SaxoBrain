### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** [[Jun Nagao]]: Motus Animi

**Findings**

- Motus Animi 2023, for two saxophonists,17 min, Zen-On commission; premiere 21 December 2023 Tokyo Bunka Kaikan Small Hall, Shinichiro Hikosaka and Yuta Hikosaka.
- Three movements portray joy, anger and sorrow. Composer explicitly frames work as a bridge toward contemporary music for performers unfamiliar with it, emphasizing gesture and movement. This does not establish beginner difficulty.
- Actual score opening specifies 1 st Alto Saxophone and 2 nd Alto Saxophone, corroborating Schott instrumentation; Any Saxophone should be reviewed. Preface allows either player to play either part in each movement.
- Zen-On 509551, ISMN9790223332880, ISBN9784115095519; digital score 41 pages.

**Notation evidence:** Inspected openingpp 5–6:3/8, dotted-quarter 60, moving to 4/4 quarter 88 atbar 26. Frequent accidentals, triplet subdivisions, thirty-second-note figures, and large register changes appear in both parts. Slurred gestures alternate with accented/staccato figures, held notes and rests; dynamics range at least pp to ff with frequent hairpins. These observations cover movementI opening only.

**Notation inspected:** Yes, limited to the pages described.

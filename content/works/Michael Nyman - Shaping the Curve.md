### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0267 - Michael Nyman - Shaping the Curve|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Michael Nyman - Shaping the Curve]].

Michael Nyman’s Shaping the Curve (1990) is for soprano saxophone and piano, published by Chester Music. IRCAM records the work and scoring. Hal Leonard’s composer/product information says it was composed for John Harle and built on a four-chord sequence. The Royal Irish Academy of Music lists it in both saxophone Teacher and Performer diploma repertoires.
[IRCAM work record](https://ressources.ircam.fr/en/work/shaping-the-curve-1) (opened): IRCAM identifies the 1990 work, Chester Music, and soprano saxophone/piano instrumentation.
[Hal Leonard / Chester Music product record](https://www.halleonard.com/product/14019120/shaping-the-curve) (opened): Composer/publisher product description identifies John Harle as dedicatee/commissioning performer and describes the work as based on a four-chord sequence.

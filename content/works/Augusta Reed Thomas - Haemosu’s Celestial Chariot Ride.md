Duration: approximately 30 minutes 30 seconds.

A concerto based on six Korean sijo poems. The soloist doubles soprano and alto saxophones. The accompanying players are two flutists, oboe, bassoon, two percussionists, and piano, alongside string orchestra.

The six movements run without pauses:

1. Bright Jewels
2. I Will Write A Poem Too
3. Hold Back the Setting Sun
4. Temple Bells
5. A Crane Dances
6. Blue Sky

Steven Banks gave the premiere with the Sejong Soloists, conducted by Hannah von Wiehler, at Carnegie Hall’s Zankel Hall on May 17, 2024.

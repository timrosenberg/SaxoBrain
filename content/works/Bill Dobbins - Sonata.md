# **Description**

*Sonata* combines musical elements from both contemporary classical vocabulary and jazz traditions. It was written partially with the intention of stimulating the growing interest in crossing the boundaries between jazz and classical music, and encouraging creative musicians to become familiar with the vocabulary and performance practices of both. **For soprano or tenor saxophone and piano.**

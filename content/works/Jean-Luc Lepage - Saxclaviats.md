### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0180 - Jean-Luc Lepage - Saxclaviats|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Jean-Luc Lepage - Saxclaviats]].

The 2010 Saxophone Catalogue lists Lepage’s Saxclaviats for E-flat or B-flat saxophone and piano. A Luxembourg government examination repertoire list places it in a lower-division first-mention list and also specifies E-flat or B-flat. The current catalog’s soprano designation is not supported by these records.
[Johan van der Linden, Saxophone Catalogue 2010](https://johanvanderlinden.com/saxophone_catalogue_2010.pdf) (opened, printed p. 21): The catalogue PDF directly lists Lepage, Saxclaviats, for E-flat or B-flat saxophone and piano; it does not identify soprano specifically.
[Luxembourg Ministry of Education, Saxophone study program](https://portal.education.lu/Portals/82/Documents/prog-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p. 12 (printed p. 11)): The Luxembourg Commission nationale des programmes de l’enseignement musical lists Saxclaviats for E-flat and B-flat saxophone in the “répertoire-type pour l’examen de fin de cycle” under the lower-division first-mention section. This is a documented examination context, not a college placement.
[Johan van der Linden, Saxophone Catalogue 2010](https://johanvanderlinden.com/saxophone_catalogue_2010.pdf) (opened, printed p. 21): The opened 2010 Saxophone Catalogue lists E-flat or B-flat saxophone and piano.

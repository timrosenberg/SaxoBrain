**Identity checked:** [[Kae Hirakawa]]: A Sequence of Impulses

**Findings**

- A Sequence of Impulses, clarinet, saxophone(alto and soprano)and piano, is a three-player chamber work with saxophone doubling.
- Seven continuously connected sections portray situations and emotions around impulse, such as desire, conflict and patience. Duration 13:50.
- Premiere 18 May 2017,21st Century Music Society 14 thconcert, Tokyo Bunka Kaikan Small Hall:Rie Arima clarinet, Hiromi Hara saxophone, composer Kae Hirakawa piano.

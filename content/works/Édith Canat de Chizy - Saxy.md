### Difficulty review and approval, 2026-10-27

Approved rating: **First Year**. Tim approved this rating on 2026-10-27. Research confidence: **moderate**.

A short teaching piece that introduces modern articulation and color effects.

Evidence inspected: IRCAM calls Saxy a pedagogical work. The Philharmonie de Paris guide 10 ans avec le saxophone places it near the end of the seventh year of instrumental study and names grace notes, trills, and glissandos. Billaudot places it in the Panorama teaching collection.

Limitations: No notation inspected. The Philharmonie guide was read through its indexed passage; direct PDF retrieval failed. Seventh year of instrumental study is not seventh year of university. First Year is a provisional comparison with the catalog’s Bozza Aria and Rueff Chanson et Passepied placements.

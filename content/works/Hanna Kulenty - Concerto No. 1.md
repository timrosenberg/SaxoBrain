**Score observations.**
- On preview p. 2, the publisher states the notation is in C and the solo saxophone is amplified with delay, so the displayed pitches should not be treated as a transposed alto part. The opening solo line on p. 3 uses short note groups alternating with long tied notes, dynamic changes from very soft to very loud, and several changing meters including 3/4, 2/8, and 2/4. Preview p. 4 shows dense piano/keyboard chords against sustained ensemble tones, a more active solo saxophone figure, and a marked glissando. Only opening preview pages were inspected; no complete solo range claim is made.

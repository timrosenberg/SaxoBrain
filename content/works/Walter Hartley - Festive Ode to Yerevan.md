### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0387 - Walter Hartley - Festive Ode to Yerevan|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Walter Hartley - Festive Ode to Yerevan]]

- Identity: Hartley’s Festive Ode to Yerevan is a 1998 work for alto saxophone and piano. The Hartley estate saxophone catalog lists it as catalog no. 227, publisher Dorn, duration 2:45, and accompaniment ‘Piano Duet’; the dissertation bibliography independently lists alto saxophone and piano. The source does not clarify whether ‘Piano Duet’ means piano four-hands or another scoring.
- University of Maryland saxophone dissertation bibliography lists Festive Ode to Yerevan for alto saxophone and piano, 1998. [University of Maryland dissertation](https://api.drum.lib.umd.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/2153c64b-6b45-45aa-8f96-15586240afc5/content) (opened).
- Thematic catalog entry 248 (printed p. 156 / PDF p. 160) lists 1998 Festive Ode to Yerevan for alto saxophone, ‘piano duet.’ The catalog does not define that accompaniment label. [Hartley thematic catalog (hosted copy)](https://scispace.com/pdf/a-thematic-catalog-of-the-tuba-and-euphonium-literature-of-2dsw9vmjim.pdf) (printed 156 / PDF 160; opened).
- Official Hartley estate saxophone catalog lists Festive Ode to Yerevan as no. 227, 1998, alto saxophone, ‘Piano Duet,’ duration 2:45, publisher Dorn. [Walter S. Hartley estate, saxophone works catalog](https://www.walterhartley.com/Saxophone_2.htm) (opened).
- Dorn Publications catalog lists product DP1989 as alto saxophone and two pianos; relation of this abbreviation to the estate catalog’s ‘Piano Duet’ wording was not clarified. [Dorn Publications catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/images/DP%20Catalog%20April%202025.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Published saxophone work. The estate catalog calls the accompaniment ‘Piano Duet’ and the Dorn product catalog uses ‘a/2pno’; whether this is piano four-hands or two pianos is unresolved.
- Gaps: Clarify from a publisher score or record whether accompaniment is piano four-hands or two pianos; no notation inspected.; No saxophone recording verified.

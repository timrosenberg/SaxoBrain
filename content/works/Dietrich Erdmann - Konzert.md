### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Dietrich Erdmann, Konzert für Saxophon und Orchester (1989), with composer piano reduction.

**Findings**

- The concerto is dated 1989 and dedicated to Detlef Bensmann; scholarship identifies premiere 4 December 1989 Bremerhaven.
- Ries & Erler's orchestra catalogue specifies soprano and alto saxophone for one soloist; catalogued alto-only instrumentation is incomplete.
- Publisher orchestra catalogue gives 24 minutes; Bensmann's Thorofon recording runs 22:31.

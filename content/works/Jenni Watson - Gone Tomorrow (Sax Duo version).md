## Research notes (private)

Checked 2026-10-27. [Composer’s page](https://www.jenniwatson.com/shop/gone-tomorrow-sax-duo) supplies the edition and listening sources.

Difficulty estimate: **Advanced**, provisional. The saxophone-duo sample shows 7/16 and 5/16, rapid subdivisions, slap articulation, altissimo and timbral fingerings. The shop describes a challenging 12-minute work with many changes of mood and section. High confidence. The duo sample has a 2021 copyright; an explicit arrangement or composition date was not located. Both composer-linked recordings use alto saxophone and horn, rather than two saxophones.

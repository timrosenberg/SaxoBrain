Duration: approximately 6 minutes.

This character sketch draws on Caliban from Shakespeare’s *The Tempest*.
Rhythmic outer sections surround a more reflective central passage.
Richard Ingham commissioned the work for the 2014 bicentenary of Adolphe Sax’s birth, with funding from Creative Scotland.

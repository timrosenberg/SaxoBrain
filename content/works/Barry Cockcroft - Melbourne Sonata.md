A 12-minute sonata in three movements (Go, Slow, Blow), dedicated to Irish saxophonist Gerard McChrystal and premiered by Cockcroft and pianist Adam Pinto at the 2012 World Saxophone Congress in St Andrews. The saxophone part has few rests, so circular breathing is essential.

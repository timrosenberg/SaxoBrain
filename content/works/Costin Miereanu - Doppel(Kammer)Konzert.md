**Identity and findings.** Costin Miereanu’s Doppel(Kammer)Konzert (1985) is a concertante work for a saxophone soloist, percussion soloist and chamber orchestra, published by Salabert as EAS 18334. IRCAM and Salabert both give 16:40 and identify original performers at the 1985 Metz Festival. The saxophonist may use instruments from the sopranino through bass saxophone family, according to IRCAM’s work record.

**Sources checked.**
- [IRCAM Resources, institutional work record](https://ressources.ircam.fr/fr/work/doppel%28kammer%29konzert) (opened): IRCAM gives 1985 composition, 16:40 duration, Salabert EAS 18334, commissioning by C.E.R.M. Metz, saxophonist plus percussion soloist and chamber orchestra. Detailed saxophone choices include sopranino, soprano, alto, tenor and bass; premiere performers were Daniel Kientzy, Wim Koopman and Hilversum Chamber Orchestra conducted by David Porcelijn.
- [Éditions Durand Salabert Eschig composer brochure](https://www.durand-salabert-eschig.com/-/media/Images/DSE/PDFs/Brochures/M/miereanu_costin.ashx?hash=DB3940F1A0022C98EE93628A8FA342AAB67BA94A&la=en-GB) (search_index_only): Salabert’s composer brochure gives the same 16:40, instrumentation and premiere details.

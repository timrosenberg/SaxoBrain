### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0076 - Dietrich Erdmann - Concerto|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Dietrich Erdmann - Concerto]]

- Identity: Dietrich Erdmann's 1989 concerto for saxophone and orchestra was dedicated to Detlef Bensmann and premiered in Bremerhaven on 4 December 1989. Nico Schüler's article identifies a four-movement work using soprano and alto saxophones, with a cadenza and sound effects including pizzicato, laugh effects, and overblowing that can produce three-tone chords. The article also distinguishes the earlier 1988 Concert Piece for alto saxophone and small orchestra.
- The article distinguishes the 1988 Concert Piece for alto saxophone and small orchestra from the 1989 full concerto, which is dedicated to Detlef Bensmann and premiered in Bremerhaven. [Nico Schüler, Modernism in Color: Dietrich Erdmann’s Emphasis on Tone Color or on anti-avant-garde](https://zurnalai.lmta.lt/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/MKP_III_Nico-Schuler.pdf) (PDF p. 6, printed p. 102; opened).
- Article describes four movements, soprano and alto saxophone solo instruments, orchestral resources, solo cadenza and effects including pizzicato, laugh effects, and overblowing to create three-tone chords. [Nico Schüler, Modernism in Color](https://zurnalai.lmta.lt/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/MKP_III_Nico-Schuler.pdf) (PDF p. 6, printed p. 102; opened).

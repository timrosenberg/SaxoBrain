## Research notes (private)

**Earlier research superseded:** Use the movement names printed on the publisher cover: La Fontaine des Éléphants, Les Charmettes, Le Saint Suaire and La Sainte Chapelle. Retailer spellings such as La Fontina and Le Gaint Guaire are errors.

### Chat Batch 17 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

Dorn 858 is a five-piece set for soprano saxophone or oboe and organ, with separate solo parts. Groth lists 60 pages. The cover titles are Chambéry, La Fontaine des Éléphants, Les Charmettes, Le Saint Suaire and La Sainte Chapelle. Several retailer transcriptions of those names are incorrect. The inspected edition bears a 1981 copyright. That is not confirmed as the composition date or the date of the saxophone version.

[[Review 0407 - Yvonne Desportes - Chambery|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Yvonne Desportes - Chambery]].

Dorn catalog identifies Chambery by Yvonne Desportes as soprano saxophone and organ or oboe and organ (Dorn 858); BnF separately catalogs an oboe-organ version.
[Dorn Publications catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/images/DP%20Catalog%20Nov%202024.pdf) (opened): Opened Dorn 2024 catalog lists Chambery as soprano saxophone/organ or oboe/organ.
[Bibliothèque nationale de France catalog](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=14785267&typeNotice=p) (search_index_only): BnF result catalogues Chambery for organ and oboe, a separate matching scoring option.
[Bibliothèque nationale de France catalog](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do?index=AUT3&numNotice=14785267&typeNotice=p) (search_index_only): Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogs a facsimile of Chambery for oboe and organ (1976); this corroborates the oboe version but does not establish whether the saxophone option in Dorn is a separate arrangement or shares the same part.

Collection/transcription context: Chamber work with alternative wind instrument; oboe catalog record alone does not establish saxophone version's demands.

Review focus: Verify the soprano part in the Dorn score, including whether identical to oboe part, and inspect ensemble balance and movement sequence.

Open questions: No soprano-saxophone notation or matching performance was inspected. The opened Dorn listing and BnF record show soprano saxophone/organ and oboe/organ options, but whether the parts are identical is unresolved. Movement titles and reported 60-page extent were not independently verified.

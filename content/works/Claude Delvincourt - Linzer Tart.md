**Identity and findings.** Linzer Tart is one of six selections in Claude Delvincourt’s Croquembouches, arranged for alto saxophone and piano by Jean-Marie Londeix. Wind Music’s library catalog gives a total duration of 12:25 for the collection. A separate Spotify listing identifies a 1:56 track with Christopher Creviston and Hannah Gruber, but the recording’s version/instrument is not established. Scott D. Kallestad’s 2005 UNT dissertation includes Croquembouches in its survey of college saxophone faculty repertoire lists; one list labeled it sophomore, which is historical evidence rather than a current level recommendation.

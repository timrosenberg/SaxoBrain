### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Renato Goulart: Imagens do Brasil

**Findings**

- Correct title spelling is Imagens do Brasil. Saxofón Latinoamericano catalog dates the saxophone-and-piano work 1999.
- Composer-published retailer index offers a 19-page alto saxophone/piano score and part, Renato Goulart#1302419, A0.1741457.
- Duo Heimann-Braga recorded it for Miniaturas, Serestas e outras Imagens do Brasil during 2015 sessions. Performers are Erik Heimann Pais, saxophone, and Míriam Braga, piano.

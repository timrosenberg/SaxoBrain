## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Frederick Fox, Annexus (1980), alto saxophone and prepared piano, commissioned and dedicated to Thomas Liley.

**Findings**

- Gregory E.Miller's 2008 University of North Texas dissertation documents 1980 composition, Dorn 1988 publication, approximately 10 minutes, single movement.
- Prepared piano requires bow hairs threaded beneath selected strings before performance; other piano effects include muted strings, palm contact, fingertip glissandi and keyboard clusters.
- Saxophone techniques include minor-second and quarter-tone lip glissandi, two highest-possible altissimo gestures, growl or flutter tongue, and slow wide vibrato.
- Dissertation gives Thomas Liley and Robert Frascotti Roncorp 1983 recordings; Liley album title is inconsistent across dissertation sections, so retain as archival leads only.
- Dorn's indexed 2024 catalogue lists Annexus as 796, but URL returned 404, so catalogue number remains index-only.

**Notation evidence:** Authorized excerpts reproduced in dissertation printedp 29 show spatially proportioned durations, time in seconds, extended beams for sustained notes and vertical arrows coordinating saxophone/piano events. Printedp 32 excerpts show minor-second lip glissando and quarter-tone lip glissando with dynamic shapes. Excerpts are partial; full 19-page score was not inspected. Technique inventory elsewhere in record is credited to dissertation.

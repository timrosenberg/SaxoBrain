There is no stand-alone version for Koechlin's *Première Sonatine, Op. 194, No. 1*. Play the Oboe d'amore part on soprano saxophone. To perform or study the piece, look for catalog number **ME 8507** (for the solo and piano version) or **ME 8509** (for the full chamber score) from Éditions Max Eschig.

Catalog entry: [[Georges Enescu - Legend]].

George Enescu’s Légende (1906) was composed for trumpet and piano as a Paris Conservatoire competition work. The cataloged soprano-saxophone item appears to be a transcription, but its arranger, edition, and saxophone part were not verified.
[George Enescu National Museum](https://www.georgeenescu.ro/georgeenescu-ro_doc_2614_expozitia-fotodocumentara-george-enescu-in-franta_pg_0.htm) (opened): George Enescu National Museum states that Enescu composed Légende for trumpet and piano as a 1906 Paris Conservatoire competition piece and shows the Enoch score cover.

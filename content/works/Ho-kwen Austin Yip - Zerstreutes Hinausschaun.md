[[Review 0154 - Ho-kwen Austin Yip - Zerstreutes Hinausschaun|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Ho-kwen Austin Yip - Zerstreutes Hinausschaun]]

- Identity: Ho-kwen Austin Yip, Zerstreutes Hinausschaun (Absent-minded Window-gazing), 2017, 7:30, for soprano and alto saxophones with orchestra or piano. The assignment snapshot’s Donemus number 16931 is a different work, Die Lian Hua for flute; Donemus number 18125 identifies the correct saxophone work and lists a purchasable piano reduction.
- Donemus item 18125 identifies the correct work, orchestral instrumentation, 7:30 duration, 2017 composition, MYO commission, and a separately purchasable piano reduction. The incorrect Donemus item 16931 is Die Lian Hua for flute. [Donemus](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/18125) (search_index_only).
- The composer’s thesis explains that the piano reduction is used for the recording project and identifies the saxophone pair. [University of Iowa DMA thesis](https://s3.amazonaws.com/na-st01.ext.exlibrisgroup.com/01IOWA_INST/upload/1774817245164/Dennis%20Kwok%20DMA%20Recording%20Project%20Thesis%20Final%20Reviewed.pdf) (21; search_index_only).
- Composer post describes the Kafka story inspiration and January 2018 premiere by Timothy Sun, Hugo Loi, and Macau Youth Symphony Orchestra. [Austin Yip](https://austinyip.com/2018/04/22/zerstreutes-hinausschaun-%E3%80%8A%E7%AA%97%E5%89%8D%E6%81%8D%E6%83%9A%E3%80%8B-2017/) (search_index_only).
- Donemus public score preview (edition 18125), full orchestral score pp. 1 and 8 inspected. At the opening the soprano line enters after an initial rest with a sustained note and short tied/ornamented motion; on printed p. 8 the soprano line continues through tied notes and short figures while the alto line has a descending rapid beamed passage near the end. Exact range not established. [Donemus public score preview](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/18125) (PDF pp. 3 and 10; printed p. 8 for latter; score_inspected).
- Score observation: The opening orchestral page gives a delayed soprano entrance with a sustained note followed by short tied figures, marked expressive and moving from p toward mf. On printed p. 8, the soprano continues in longer tied notes with shorter figures, while the alto saxophone has a rapid descending beamed line at the page end. No exact range is inferred.
- Score link: [Donemus item 18125 public preview, orchestra score excerpt; piano reduction is separately purchasable.](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/18125) (notation inspected).
- Collection/use: Commissioned for Macau Youth Symphony Orchestra; published in both orchestral and piano-reduction forms.
- Review focus: Inference: the two soloists have differentiated writing in the inspected opening and printed p. 8, with the soprano entering over a sustained line and the alto taking a quick descending figure. Check coordination and line balance between the two saxophone parts.
- Gaps: Only a partial orchestral score preview was inspected; the piano reduction and remaining score are not available in this review. No direct recording page verified.
- Proposed correction: Replace the incorrect Donemus item 16931 reference with item 18125. Add piano to the edition/instrument notes if the catalog intends to represent the separately published reduction.

### Difficulty review and approval, 2026-10-27

Approved rating: **Advanced**. Tim approved this rating on 2026-10-27. Research confidence: **high**.

Quarter-tones, timbral trills, soft slap tongue, and very precise sopranino tone and pitch control.

Evidence inspected: Schott labels the sopranino edition difficult. Read Joseph Richards’s 2019 University of North Texas performance guide, printed pages 14-21. Visually inspected printed page 16, including the quarter-tone and tremolo fingering examples. The guide documents rhythmic density and very soft dynamics as additional challenges.

Limitations: The full standalone score was not inspected. Circular breathing is recommended in the guide for sustained pitches, but the guide explicitly says the work can be performed without it. It is not presented as mandatory.

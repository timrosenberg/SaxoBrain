## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Leonard Mark Lewis: Book of Dances and Other Diversions

**Findings**

- Alto saxophone and marimba. Composer's public score excerpt is headed Songs in Pieces, identified as movementIV by Ohio State's 2023 faculty program.
- Four movements: Simple Blue Toccata; Waltz - Saying Goodbye; Tango Triste; Songs in Pieces. Professional faculty performance by Michael Rene Torres, saxophone, and Susan Powell, marimba, on 13 February 2023 at Ohio State.
- RoseWind Duo recording on EquilibriumEQ161, Devils Garden/Angels Landing,2016: Clifford Leaman, saxophone, and Scott Herring, marimba. Four track timings 3:06,2:43,2:28,2:34 total 10:51; these are recording timings rather than a score duration.

**Notation evidence:** MovementIV opening: Rhythmic but Utterly Lyrical quarter=88; frequent 5/8,6/8 and 4/8 changes. Marimba repeats rapid chordal/low-note patterns with triplets and accents. Alto rests for the opening measures, then enters pp with tied/slurred sustained notes and swells, requiring quiet response and exact entrances over shifting pulse. Only movementIV opening inspected; do not generalize to other dances or infer four-mallet requirement from two staves alone.

**Notation inspected:** Yes, limited to the pages described.

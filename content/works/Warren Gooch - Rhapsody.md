### Chat Batch 17 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

Dorn 1362 for alto saxophone and piano. Composer-attributed College Music Society program note states composed 2003 for Randall Smith and Patrice Ewoldt, who premiered it at the World Saxophone Congress in Minneapolis. One continuous work in five sections: Marcato, dialogue-like cadenza, Cantabile, perpetual motion and energetic scherzo with opening return. Score copyright 2004 is publication data.

### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record]]

Catalog: [[Warren Gooch - Rhapsody]]

- Identity: Warren Gooch's Rhapsody is a single-movement work in five sections for alto saxophone and piano. College Music Society's 2006 Great Plains Chapter concert program gives a composer program note: Marcato opening, extended cadenza/dialogue, Cantabile, perpetual motion, and scherzo with a partial return. It states the work was written for Randall Smith and Patrice Ewoldt and premiered at the 2003 World Saxophone Congress.
- College Music Society Great Plains Chapter program gives the 2006 performance by Randall Smith and Marilyn Musick and reproduces a composer note with five-section outline, cadenza, dedication, and 2003 World Saxophone Congress premiere. [College Music Society Great Plains Chapter conference program](https://www.music.org/pdf/conf/reg/ProgramShell.pdf) (PDF pp. 10-11, printed 11-12; opened).
- 2013 New Horizons Music Festival program lists Rhapsody by Gooch performed by Lindsey Davis and Janet Coggins. [New Horizons Music Festival program](https://nsmf.truman.edu/files/2014/05/NHMF-Program-2013.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Commissioned for named performers and programmed at a saxophone festival; no score observed.
- Review focus: Inference: the extended cadenza and succession of contrasting sections focus review on pacing, formal continuity, and saxophone-piano dialogue.
- Gaps: No notation inspected and no recording playback auditioned.

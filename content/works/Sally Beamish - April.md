Written for Sound World’s scheme supporting performers during the coronavirus pandemic, *April* pays tribute to pianist Ellis Marsalis Jr.
It is a chaconne, a piece built on a repeating sequence of chords.
Piano, vibraphone, and saxophone take turns adding music with an improvisatory character.
These passages are fully notated in the original version.
Beamish also allows a longer performance with jazz improvisation.

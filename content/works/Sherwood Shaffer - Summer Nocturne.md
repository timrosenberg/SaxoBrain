> _Summer Nocturne_ was written in 1981 for Jonathan Helton and premiered by him that same year at the North Carolina School of the Arts, with later performances both in Europe and this country. Its movements are head by the following poetic quotations:
> 
> - "The night has a star of its own" from _Blizzard_ by Marie Graybeal
> - "Go and catch a falling star" from _Song_ by John Donne
> - "The night in silence under many a star" from _When Lilacs Last in the Dooryard Bloom'd_ by Walt Whitman
> 
> The sound and symbols of the poetry inspired the work as a musical experience rather than as a literal musical representation of their meaning. Therefore, the poetry only suggets the catalyst rather than specifically indicates what the music is about. The musical experience is the intent. The work is played without major pauses between the short movements.

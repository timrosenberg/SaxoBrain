### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0002 - Albert Tepper - Concertino|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Albert Tepper - Concertino]]

- Identity: Albert Tepper's *Concertino* is an eight-minute soprano saxophone-and-piano edition (Southern Music SU427 / HL3776341). The publisher says Tepper adapted it from his oboe-and-strings concertino, made the piano transcription, and published the saxophone version in 1952; it describes three short linked movements without pause and extended tonality. Trinity College London lists it on the Grade 7 soprano saxophone syllabus, and a University of South Alabama student recital program included it in 2022.
- Southern Music identifies the soprano saxophone edition, eight-minute duration, oboe-and-strings source, composer-made piano transcription published in 1952, three linked movements without pause, and extended tonality. [Southern Music Company catalog](https://keiserproductions.com/catalog/southern-music/?ic=&sc=&tc=&tid=31FAE9B6-28C5-4C96-AE1B-4558F1010F1B) (lines 324-337; opened).
- Trinity College London's syllabus lists Tepper's Concertino for Soprano Saxophone under Grade 7, Group A accompanied pieces. [Trinity College London saxophone syllabus](https://www.trinitycollege.com/qualifications/music/grade-exams/woodwind/saxophone-pieces) (Grade 7, item 45; opened).
- University of South Alabama Department of Music program lists the work on Zander Westphal's student saxophone recital with pianist Scott Schwab on February 16, 2022, and identifies the work as dating from 1946. [University of South Alabama Department of Music](https://www.southalabama.edu/colleges/music/archive/resources/202122/20220216westphalrecitalprogram.pdf) (1; opened).
- Collection/use: Saxophone version is an adaptation of the composer's oboe-and-strings work, with a composer-made piano transcription; Trinity lists the soprano edition at Grade 7, and a university student recital program documents performance use.

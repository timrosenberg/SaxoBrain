Commissioned by and dedicated to Joseph Lulloff, this four-movement sonata was composed in 2016. The publisher gives an approximate performance time of 23 minutes. Pann drew inspiration from [[William Albright]]'s saxophone music and Lulloff's combination of lyrical finesse and forceful virtuosity. [Theodore Presser Company, publisher's introduction](https://www.presser.com/114-41853-sonata-for-alto-saxophone-and-piano.html).

## Movements

1. **This Black Cat**
2. **Three Songs Without Words**
	1. Reverie
	2. Soaring
	3. Consolation
3. **Cuppa Joe**
4. **Epilogue: Lacrimosa in memory of Joel Hastings**

Movement II contains three songs within a single movement. “Cuppa Joe” alludes to Lulloff's love of coffee. The finale mourns pianist Joel Hastings and draws on the descending motif from John Dowland's *Flow My Tears*. [Album release notes from Nathan J. Silverman Co. PR, published by Musical America](https://www.musicalamerica.com/news/newsstory.cfm?archived=0&categoryid=5&storyid=52482).

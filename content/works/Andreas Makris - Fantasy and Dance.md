### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record]]

Catalog: [[Andreas Makris - Fantasy and Dance]]

- Identity: Andreas Makris's *Fantasy and Dance* is an alto saxophone work composed for Miller Sigmon for the 1974 Fourth World Saxophone Congress. The Greek Saxophone Library records an 11-minute, single-movement alto-and-piano edition and its premiere; a later publisher listing describes wind ensemble and string-orchestra/harp accompaniments, while a dealer notes those materials are rental items.
- Specialist saxophone library documents year, duration, alto/piano version, premiere, dedication, and lists specific review demands and college-level use; its difficulty designation is that site's assessment. [Hellenic Saxophone Library](https://hellenicsax.gr/works/110_fantasy_and_dance) (opened).
- Publisher description confirms the Sigmon commission and wind or string/harp accompaniment versions. [Sheet Music Plus, Lauren Keiser listing](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/fantasy-and-dance-22587255.html) (opened).
- IMSLP work list separates the 1974 piano, 1977 winds/harp, and 2000 strings/harp versions. [IMSLP](https://imslp.org/wiki/Template%3AConcertante_Works_%28Makris%2C_Andreas%29) (opened).
- The Hellenic Saxophone Library page embeds a first-measures score excerpt, labels it Alto Saxophone and Piano, and identifies a YouTube video as the work performance resource. [Hellenic Saxophone Library](https://hellenicsax.gr/works/110_fantasy_and_dance) (score_inspected).

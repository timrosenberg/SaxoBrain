## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** [[Isao Matsushita]]: Atoll II

**Findings**

- Composer's official list places Atoll II in 1982, for alto saxophone and piano, approximately 10 minutes, commissioned by Detlef Bensmann and published by Ongaku no Tomo Sha. It also lists a Schwann CD31007 H1 recording lead.
- EDA 29, Music for Saxophone from Berlin volume 3, contains Frank Lunte and Tatjana Blome's exact Atoll II recording. Qobuz identifies track duration 9:18.
- EDA's composer/performer program note describes five sections that develop from repeated notes and interacting chord waves. This is program-note description, not a notation inspection.
- Towson University includes Atoll II in undergraduate saxophone repertoire; Córdoba conservatory also lists it.

**Notation inspected:** No.

**Recordings and listening leads**

- [Atoll II, Frank Lunte and Tatjana Blome, EDA29, track 1](https://www.eda-records.com/177-0-CD-im-Detail.html?cd_id=42) Record label lists exact title/composer; album notes identify duo and Qobuz corroborates track metadata. No audio download or audition claim.

**Collection fit:** Professional contemporary recital work with direct university curriculum evidence.

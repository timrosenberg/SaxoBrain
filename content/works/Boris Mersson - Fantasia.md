### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0026 - Boris Mersson - Fantasia|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Boris Mersson - Fantasia]].

Boris Mersson, Fantasia op. 37, for alto saxophone and piano. The Antwerp conservatory holdings list identifies the 1981 Kunzelmann score and part, dedication to Iwan Roth, and 1979 composition date. A separate orchestral version also appears in catalog evidence.
[Philharmonie de Paris, 10 ans avec le saxophone](https://catalogue.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (search_index_only, 87): The Paris Philharmonie saxophone repertoire guide identifies Fantasia op. 37 for alto saxophone and piano and describes an adagio leading into a rhythmically driven allegro.
[Conservatoire Royal de Bruxelles/Antwerp library repertoire list](https://www.libraryconservatoryantwerp.be/lijsten/BSAX.pdf) (search_index_only): A Belgian conservatory library lists a 1981 Kunzelmann piano score and part, dedicated to Iwan Roth, and separately a 1982 30-page alto saxophone and orchestra score.
[Ficks Music](https://www.ficksmusic.com/en-ca/products/mersson-fantasia-for-alto-saxophone-and-piano-kunzelmann) (search_index_only): Current retail catalog identifies Kunzelmann edition for alto saxophone and piano, ISMN 9790206210983.
[Conservatoire Royal de Bruxelles / Antwerp saxophone holdings list](https://www.libraryconservatoryantwerp.be/lijsten/BSAX.pdf) (opened, 36): The Antwerp Conservatory library lists Fantasia per sassofono alto e pianoforte, op. 37, Zurich: Edition Albert J. Kunzelmann, 1981; one score and one part; dedicated to Iwan Roth; composed 1979.
[Philharmonie de Paris, 10 ans avec le saxophone](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, printed p. 87; PDF p. 82): On printed page 87 (PDF page 82), the guide identifies Mersson’s Fantasia, Op. 37, for alto saxophone and piano and characterizes its form as an Adagio flowing into a rhythmic Allegro.

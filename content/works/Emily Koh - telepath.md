## Program Information (Summary)

> Written for Ogni Suono’s tenth anniversary, the work reflects the duo’s collaboration across physical distance during the pandemic. Its title joins Greek ideas of distance and feeling. The performers begin near one another, playing in strict coordination. They gradually separate onstage and gain rhythmic independence while sustaining the responsiveness developed through their partnership.

Emily Koh, [program information](https://www.emilykoh.net/duos-trios/telepath).

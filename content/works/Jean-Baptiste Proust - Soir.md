### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0172 - Jean-Baptiste Proust - Soir|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Jean-Baptiste Proust - Soir]]

- Identity: Pascal Proust, Soir, in the catalog’s Billaudot GB5758 edition. Billaudot lists E-flat and B-flat saxophone/piano classifications and labels its edition facile (cycle 1); Proust’s own page describes an alto saxophone and piano version, five minutes, cycle 2.
- Billaudot identifies Pascal Proust, GB5758, E-flat and B-flat saxophone/piano classifications, and facile cycle 1. [Billaudot](https://www.billaudot.com/soir-6.html) (search_index_only).
- Composer site identifies alto saxophone and piano, five-minute duration, and cycle 2 (intermediate). [Pascal Proust](https://www.pascal-proust.fr/catalogue/bois/saxophone/saxophone-et-piano/soir/) (opened).
- Collection/use: Publisher and composer give differing level descriptors (cycle 1 facile versus cycle 2 intermediate); retain both as edition/source claims and resolve before use.
- Gaps: No score or recording inspected; range and musical demands remain unknown.
- Proposed correction: Correct composer from Jean-Baptiste Proust to Pascal Proust. Verify whether the record should represent alto saxophone only or the Billaudot classifications for both E-flat and B-flat saxophones.

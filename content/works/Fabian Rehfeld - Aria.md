### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0091 - Fabian Rehfeld - Aria|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Fabian Rehfeld - Aria]]

- Identity: The saxophone catalog entry is a Marty Lenard arrangement of Fabian Rehfeld’s *Aria*, Dorn Publications DORN2013, offered for B-flat or E-flat saxophone and piano. IMSLP’s record and public scan identify the original Rehfeld Op. 67 as a late-19th-century violin or cello work with piano or organ, so the saxophone item is a transcription of another instrumental piece.
- Current catalog lead identifies DORN2013 and the B-flat/E-flat saxophone and piano edition arranged by Marty Lenard. [Groth Music catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-141758.aspx) (search_index_only).
- IMSLP identifies Op. 67, first published in 1895, as for violin or cello with piano or organ; the public scan is a violin score. [IMSLP work record](https://imslp.org/wiki/Aria%2C_Op.67_(Rehfeld,_Fabian)) (opened).
- The public scan shows an Adagio molto sostenuto opening in the violin part. This observation is about the source violin score only, not the saxophone arrangement. [Public-domain Rehfeld source score, digitized from the 1918 Oliver Ditson edition](https://s9.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/e/e5/IMSLP146597-PMLP273045-Rehfeld_Aria_v_or_vc_p_psc_and_v_pt.pdf) (score_inspected).
- Score observation: Printed p. 32 opens Adagio molto sostenuto, with long tied notes in the violin/cello line over sustained piano chords that become more mobile with chromatic inflections; later on the same page the solo line briefly breaks into quicker notes before returning to a quiet dolcissimo statement. This is the original violin/cello score, not the saxophone arrangement. [Score](https://s9.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/e/e5/IMSLP146597-PMLP273045-Rehfeld_Aria_v_or_vc_p_psc_and_v_pt.pdf)
- Score link: [Original violin/cello and piano/organ score, not the saxophone arrangement.](https://s9.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/e/e5/IMSLP146597-PMLP273045-Rehfeld_Aria_v_or_vc_p_psc_and_v_pt.pdf) (notation inspected).
- Collection/use: Documented transcription of Rehfeld’s original violin/cello work; separate saxophone arrangement lists two saxophone transpositions. Original intended audience is not established by the sources found.

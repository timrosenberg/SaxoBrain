### Difficulty review and approval, 2026-10-27

Approved rating: **Advanced**. Tim approved this rating on 2026-10-27. Research confidence: **high**.

Irregular tuplets, frequent meter changes, exposed high-register writing, and detailed color and dynamic control.

Evidence inspected: Visually inspected the composer-hosted first page of the transposed alto score, edited August 2019. It shows 7/8, 5/8, 6/8, 3/8 and changing simple meters, ratios including 5:3 and 7:6, high-register phrases, and a three-timbre instruction. The composer describes a virtuosic nine-minute vocal-style fantasy.

Limitations: Only the first score page was inspected. The placement remains an editorial estimate, not a publisher grade.

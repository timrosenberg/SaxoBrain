### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0177 - Jean-Baptiste Singelée - Septieme Solo de Concert Op. 93|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Jean-Baptiste Singelée - Septieme Solo de Concert Op. 93]].

Jean-Baptiste Singelée, Septième Solo de Concert no. 7, Op. 93 (1863), originally for baritone saxophone and piano. The inspected public Musik Fabrik/Notendownload preview is a 2011 baritone edition revised by Paul Wehage, MFJBS011. Separate Roncorp and Dorn catalog leads also describe baritone editions. The current catalog’s alto/piano tag is not supported by these sources; the intended edition remains unresolved.
[CiNii Books, edition record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA84614610) (opened): CiNii identifies Roncorp c.1987, compiled/edited by Bruce Ronkin, baritone saxophone and piano, about 3:00, eight-page score and attached part. IMSLP independently lists Op. 93 for baritone saxophone and piano.
[IMSLP, Singelée works list](https://imslp.org/wiki/List_of_works_by_Jean_Baptiste_Singel%C3%A9e) (opened): IMSLP lists Op. 93 as the 1863 Seventh Solo for baritone saxophone and piano, consistent with the CiNii catalog record.
[Musik Fabrik Music Publishing listing via Notendownload](https://www.notendownload.com/solo-de-concert-no.-7-opus-93/mfjbs011) (opened): Public product page identifies MFJBS011, a baritone saxophone and piano edition of Op. 93, with 12 pages and an approximately 3:30 duration; seller’s “medium” label is not used as a SaxoBrain level.
[Notendownload score preview, opening](https://www.notendownload.com/media/4a/c9/96/1704668224/mfjbs011-1.png?1704668224=) (score_inspected, public preview page 2): Public score-preview images visibly identify the edition as baritone saxophone and piano, Jean-Baptiste Singelée Op. 93, revised by Paul Wehage and © 2011 Editions de la Fabrique Musique. I inspected the opening and a later page.

Score observations:
- The opening preview page marks “Introduction: Allegro risoluto”; the baritone saxophone begins with an ascending slurred figure and tied notes at forte, against a piano part with strongly accented chordal attacks. ([score source](https://www.notendownload.com/media/4a/c9/96/1704668224/mfjbs011-1.png?1704668224=))
- A later preview page shows a contrast between the saxophone’s slurred/held melodic phrases and repeated chord patterns in the piano; a subsequent saxophone entry follows a rest with a quick connected figure. The selected previews do not establish the full range or complete form. ([score source](https://www.notendownload.com/media/d2/4c/81/1713383660/mfjbs011-2.png?1713383660=))

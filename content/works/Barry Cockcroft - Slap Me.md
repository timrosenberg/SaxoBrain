A duet for two saxophones of the same pitch, written for Australian saxophonist Michael Duke, who asked for something like *Black & Blue* but longer and harder. Slap tonguing imitates bongo drums, and each player takes a solo over the other's accompaniment. About 5 minutes.

Full program note and score: [Reed Music](https://www.reedmusic.com/store/woodwind/saxophone/saxophone-duets/sax-same-pitch-duets/slap-me/)

## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Song Concerto by Libby Larsen. Tim confirmed the title in Score Batch 04 on October 20, 2026.

**Findings**

- The exact candidate is Song Concerto (2005),21 minutes, for a single soloist doubling alto and soprano with piano or chamber orchestra. UNT's 2024 DMA guide identifies this as Larsen's only saxophone concerto.
- Four movements: In Dance; Cri de Coeur; Mozart's Starling; Abandon. Commissioned by Barlow Endowment and Eugene Rousseau's students; premiered 15 February 2006 by Rousseau with University of Iowa Orchestra, William Jones.
- Libby Larsen Publishing provides the 76-page public orchestral perusal. The score carries 2006/2008 copyright, distinct from composition 2005.
- Tim confirmed the Song Concerto identity in Score Batch 04. The title, 2005 composition date and alto/soprano doubling are now reflected in the catalog.

**Notation evidence:** Candidate Song Concerto, first page: alto solo enters raucously at quarter 104 with chromatic sextuplet runs, accented notes and grace gestures; 4/4 changes briefly to 3/8. MovementII printed page 55 explicitly changes soloist to B-flat soprano. Slowly, extremely free, quarter≈63 uses parenthesized beat counts, push-ahead/pull-back timing and a broad dynamic swell to ff over drone textures. Full score appears in concert pitch. Do not infer transposed saxophone range from displayed pitches. Piano reduction was not inspected.

**Notation inspected:** Yes, limited to the pages described.

### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record]]

Catalog: [[Lawrence Moss - Six Short Pieces]]

- Identity: Lawrence Moss's Six Short Pieces is a 1993 work for E-flat alto saxophone and piano, published by Roncorp in 1995, dedicated to Reggie Jackson and Jessica Krasch. The UPV dissertation explicitly lists six separate pieces: Rounds, Blur, Broadway Boogie-Woogie, Chinese Lullaby, Introduction, and Jazzy. Its printed p.614 reproduces the opening score of Rounds.
- Dissertation states six movements, separately numbering Introduction as no.5 and Jazzy as no.6; it reproduces page 1 of Rounds for E-flat alto saxophone and piano. [UPV dissertation on jazz in the classical saxophone repertoire](https://riunet.upv.es/bitstream/handle/10251/4337/tesisUPV2925.pdf) (printed p. 614, score p. 1; score_inspected).
- In the reproduced Rounds opening, the score marks Leggiero, quarter=96, and p then pp; both alto saxophone and piano parts are shown. [UPV dissertation score excerpt](https://riunet.upv.es/bitstream/handle/10251/4337/tesisUPV2925.pdf) (printed p. 614; score_inspected).
- NYPL American Music Center collection finding aid lists the published score for E-flat alto saxophone and piano. [New York Public Library finding aid](https://nyplorg-data-archives.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/collection/generated_finding_aids/mus24605.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Retailer lists alto saxophone and piano, Northeastern Music Publications; its grade label is vendor metadata only. [Music8 retailer](https://www.music8.com/products/detail93576.php) (opened).
- Score observation: Printed p.614 reproduces Rounds p.1. The saxophone line begins Leggiero at quarter=96, with quick beamed figures and dynamics p to pp; piano has independent moving figures beneath.
- Collection/use: Published original saxophone/piano work; dissertation dates it 1993 and publication 1995.
- Review focus: Inference from the inspected excerpt: the opening Rounds combines light beamed saxophone figures with an independently moving piano line and soft dynamics. The excerpt does not establish demands across the other five pieces.
- Gaps: Only the opening of Rounds was inspected; no score details are available here for the other five pieces.; UPV site blocks automated page opening, but the locally available dissertation was read and its score figure inspected.

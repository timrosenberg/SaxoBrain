### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Germaine Tailleferre: Trois danses de La Nouvelle Cythère. Strong match to abbreviated catalog title Trois Dances.

**Findings**

- Musik Fabrik MFGT015 C is the alto saxophone and piano version of Trois danses de La Nouvelle Cythère. Distributor catalog names the three movements Pavane, Nocturne, and Galop.
- A separate B-flat soprano or tenor saxophone edition is indexed on MuseScore/ArrangeMe. These are alternative saxophone editions, not evidence for required alto/soprano doubling or a two-saxophone ensemble.
- Real Academia de Bellas Artes de San Fernando documents the suite in Elisa Urrestarazu and Cornelia Lenzin's professional saxophone/piano concert on 19 October 2021, with tempo indications assez lent, très lent, très vite.

### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record]]

Catalog: [Günter Raphael - Sonate Op. 74.md](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Günter Raphael - Sonate Op. 74.md). Full evidence log: [[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record|Archived evidence and recovery]].

**Identity and findings.** Tonger publishes Günter Raphael’s Sonata Op. 74a for alto saxophone and piano. EDA Records’ album page identifies its 1957 date and three movements; a recording by Frank Lunte and pianist Tatjana Blome is indexed with individual movement durations. This is distinct from Raphael’s Op. 74 Divertimento for alto saxophone and cello.

**Sources checked.**
- [EDA Records](https://www.eda-records.com/index.php?article_id=177&cd_id=68) (opened): EDA Records identifies Sonata Op.74a (1957) and lists Con moto, Vivace, and Allegro molto energico as its three movements.
- [Qobuz album metadata](https://www.qobuz.com/fi-en/album/music-for-saxophone-from-germany-1952-1969-frank-lunte-and-tatjana-blome/apkaus9vp2u4d) (opened): Qobuz credits alto saxophonist Frank Lunte and pianist Tatjana Blome on the EDA album; individual movement durations are 3:41, 4:25 and 4:23.
- [IMEP Mediatheque](https://biblio.imep.be/index.php?lvl=notice_display&id=5828) (opened): Institutional library record identifies the printed Tonger score for alto saxophone and piano, 23 pages plus saxophone part.
- [Stretta Music](https://www.stretta-music.be/raphael-sonate-op-74-nr-646604.html) (opened): Retailer lists the Tonger edition for alto saxophone and piano.

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** The Tale of Taliesin, concerto for alto saxophone and orchestra, piano reduction

**Findings**

- Billaudot's piano reduction GB8903 is 23 minutes, copyright 2010, ISMN 9790043089032, in Jean-Yves Fourmeau's collection.
- The composer work list dates the work 2007 and identifies commission by Ola Asdahl Rokkones and AOV Göttingen. The Lawo booklet instead says written 2008 and labels the recording 2009. Composition date remains conflicting.
- Eight connected narrative sections follow the Welsh Taliesin legend.
- The exact Lawo LWC1162 recording is Ola Asdahl Rokkones, St. Petersburg Northern Sinfonia and Fabio Mastrangelo, duration 24:57.

**Notation inspected:** No.

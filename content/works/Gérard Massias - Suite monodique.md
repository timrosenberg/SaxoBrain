**Identity and findings.** Billaudot MRB1068 is Gérard Massias’s Suite monodique for solo alto saxophone. Billaudot places it in its L’enseignement moderne du saxophone collection and assigns its publisher classification “Difficult (cycle 3).” This is publisher pedagogy metadata, not a SaxoBrain level.

**Sources checked.**
- [Éditions Billaudot](https://www.billaudot.com/mrb1068-6.html) (opened): Publisher identifies MRB1068 as solo alto saxophone in L’enseignement moderne du saxophone and labels it Difficult (cycle 3).
- [Confédération Musicale de France archive](https://cmf-archives.bibliossimo.net/files/original/c67cfef6ec420fd689a7a194cbfc7a2ae7bcb8ac.pdf) (opened): An archival 1967 French music federation periodical discusses Suite monodique as part of chamber music repertoire for saxophone and notes that its new series sought to expand beyond piano-only saxophone programming.
- [Conservatory library repertoire bibliography](https://www.libraryconservatoryantwerp.be/lijsten/BSAX.pdf) (opened, p.28): A published concert repertoire list indexes it as a 7-page solo work, composed in 1954, about 10:40, dedicated to Georges Gourdet.

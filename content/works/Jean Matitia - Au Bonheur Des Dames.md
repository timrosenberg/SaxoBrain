Catalog: [[Jean Matitia - Au Bonheur Des Dames]]

- Identity: Jean Matitia, Au bonheur des dames for alto saxophone and piano, Henry Lemoine 27975. The Lemoine 2024 saxophone catalog gives level 3 and 16 pages; the FFEA lists the work in its 2022 cycle III CEM collection.
- Catalog entry identifies the Lemoine edition and saxophone/piano scoring. [Groth Music](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-38087.aspx) (search_index_only).
- Henry Lemoine’s current saxophone catalog lists Au bonheur des dames for alto saxophone and piano, catalog 27975, level 3, 16 pages. [Henry Lemoine 2024 saxophone catalog](https://www.henry-lemoine.com/assets/pdf/CATASAX.pdf) (search_index_only).
- FFEA’s 2022 collection of alto saxophone works includes this title in its cycle III CEM section. [Fédération Française de l’Enseignement Artistique](https://www.federation-ffea.fr/medias/download/ffea_2022_saxophone-alto.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Adolphesax page identifies a live performance by Richard Ducros, alto saxophone, and Sophie Teboul, piano, at Gradignan. [Adolphesax performance listing](https://adolphesax.com/videos/richard-ducros-alto-saxophone-plays-au-bonheur-des-dames-by-jean-matitia/) (search_index_only).
- Recording: [Richard Ducros, alto saxophone, and Sophie Teboul, piano, live in Gradignan.](https://adolphesax.com/videos/richard-ducros-alto-saxophone-plays-au-bonheur-des-dames-by-jean-matitia/) Performance video listing identified; playback not auditioned.

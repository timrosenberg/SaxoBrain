Grew out of Cockcroft's improvising on Berio's *Sequenza VIIb* while studying in France: long melodic lines alternate with Berio-style multiphonics set to funky rhythms, which a friend said sounded like a chicken. After *Black & Blue* it is his most widely played piece. About 6 minutes.

Full program note and score: [Reed Music](https://www.reedmusic.com/store/woodwind/saxophone/saxophone-solo/sax-solo-soprano/ku-ku/)

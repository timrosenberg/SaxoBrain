### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0082 - Eduard Tubin - Sonata|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Eduard Tubin - Sonata]]

- Identity: Eduard Tubin completed his Sonata for alto saxophone and piano in Stockholm on 9 March 1951. The BIS CD 269 booklet documents its first performance in Uppsala on 23 September 1951 by Jules de Vries and Martin Keil, and the recorded performance by Pekka Savijoki and Roland Pöntinen. The three movements are timed 5:59, 4:41, and 3:24.
- Booklet gives completion date, first performance and performers, and identifies the three movements and track timings. [BIS CD 269 booklet](https://eclassical.textalk.se/shop/art28/BIS-CD-269_booklet_scan.pdf-cd34f8.pdf) (PDF pp. 1-3; opened).
- Program note describes a first movement with saxophone and piano themes, climax and virtuoso saxophone runs; the Adagio follows without a break and draws on a 13th-century troubadour song, while the finale is fast and concludes with a virtuoso rush. [BIS CD 269 booklet](https://eclassical.textalk.se/shop/art28/BIS-CD-269_booklet_scan.pdf-cd34f8.pdf) (PDF p. 3; opened).
- Recording: [BIS CD 269 performance by Pekka Savijoki, saxophone, and Roland Pöntinen, piano; movements listed as 5:59, 4:41, and 3:24.](https://eclassical.textalk.se/shop/art28/BIS-CD-269_booklet_scan.pdf-cd34f8.pdf) Booklet inspected; playback not auditioned.
- Collection/use: Original alto saxophone sonata with documented 1951 premiere and a commercial recording.
- Review focus: Inference from the booklet's program note: check the first movement's virtuoso saxophone runs, the attacca into the Adagio, and the fast finale's climactic rush. These details are program-note descriptions, not notation observations.

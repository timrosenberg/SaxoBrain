Originally for [solo piano](https://imslp.org/wiki/Croquembouches_(Delvincourt,_Claude)) (1926). Arranged and revised by Jean-Marie Londeix and officially published by Alphonse Leduc in 1992.
### Movement titles
1. *Plum pudding*. Allegro moderato
2. *Puits d'amour*. Lent sans excès
3. *Nègre en chemise*. Andane non troppo lento
4. *Linzer tart*. Allegro non tropo (mouvement de valse)
5. *Grenadine*. Allegro (mouvement de habanera)
6. *Rahat Loukhoum*. Très tranquillement

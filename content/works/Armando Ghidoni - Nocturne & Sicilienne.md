[[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record]]

Catalog: [[Armando Ghidoni - Nocturne & Sicilienne]]

- Identity: Armando Ghidoni's *Nocturne et Sicilienne* is published by Alphonse Leduc as AL29562 for alto, soprano, or tenor saxophone with piano or harp. The Royal Conservatory of Music's published saxophone syllabus includes it in its tenor-saxophone repertoire list; this establishes pedagogical use but not a college-level placement.
- Leduc catalog entry in publisher catalog identifies instrumentation and catalog number. [Alphonse Leduc catalog](https://casamusicaledelrio.it/casamusicale/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/CAT-18-107-Leduc-Catalogue-2018-ss-GS-v6-NO-CROPS-LO-RESv2.pdf) (opened).

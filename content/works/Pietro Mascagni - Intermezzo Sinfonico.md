### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0309 - Pietro Mascagni - Intermezzo Sinfonico|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Pietro Mascagni - Intermezzo Sinfonico]]

- Identity: The catalog identifies a Molenaar saxophone-and-piano arrangement of Mascagni’s Intermezzo Sinfonico from Cavalleria rusticana by Jack Ham, four pages. Separate arrangements exist for wind band, so the edition attached to this item must be kept distinct from the published band score by Van B. Ragsdale.
- Current catalog listing gives Molenaar 060656640 and credits Jack Ham for a saxophone/piano arrangement. [Groth Music](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-144004.aspx) (search_index_only).
- A public wind-band score is a separate arrangement by Van B. Ragsdale and lists instrumentation for full band. [Carl Fischer score](https://www.tpcfassets.com/resources/band/2015/pdf/SPS62_INTERMEZZO_SINFONICO.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Arrangement of an orchestral intermezzo; multiple saxophone and band arrangements circulate.
- Gaps: No source for the exact Molenaar Jack Ham saxophone/piano edition opened.; Do not transfer notation or demands from the distinct Ragsdale band arrangement.

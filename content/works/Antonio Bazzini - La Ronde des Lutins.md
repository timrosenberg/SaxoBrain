# “The Dance of the Goblins”

> **Publisher Desc.**  Here is a virtuoso concert piece that will challenge any alto saxophonist. "La Ronde des Lutins" (The Dance of the Goblins) originally composed for violin by Antonio Bazzini in 1852, is a masterful work that creates some intense technical gymnastics for any wind instrument. Many alternate fingerings need to be utilized. There is also slap tonguing, false fingerings, and optional double tonguing. It is graded "Advanced" due to the relentless technical facility needed and frequent use of the altissimo register. A true crowd pleaser, this solo is perfect for any recital or encore.

**Identity checked:** [[Jun Nagao]]: Trilithon

**Findings**

- Trilithon 2021, alto saxophone, baritone saxophone and piano,11 min; commissioned Nobuya Sugawa.
- Inspected composer preface says written April 2021; premiere 12 June 2021, Yamaha Hall Tokyo, Nobuya Sugawa alto, Yasuto Tanaka baritone, Minako Koyanagi piano. Zen-On English work catalog corroborates 12 June. Shop page gives 21 June, apparently transposed day digits.
- Zen-On 560031, SEO-031, ISMN9790223330282; digital edition 53 pages, copyright 2022 visible in preview.
- Composer preface connects title to three megaliths of Stonehenge and honors trio performers; opening melancholy piano theme is linked to pandemic-era expression.

**Notation evidence:** Inspected printed scorepp 4–5: Poco lento quarter 52,2/4, piano-only opening; baritone enters atbar 12 pp dolcissimo with high-register slurred line and sustained notes.

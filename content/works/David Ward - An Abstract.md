### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0070 - David Ward - An Abstract|Full review note]]

Catalog: [David Ward - An Abstract.md](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/David Ward - An Abstract.md). Full evidence log: [research record](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/research-review-2026-10-11/lane-3/records/0070.json).

**Identity and findings.** David Ward’s An Abstract is an alto saxophone and piano work published by Southern Music, copyright 1963. Hal Leonard identifies publisher level 3; WorldCat confirms score format and original publication. Fred Hemke recorded it; Paul Brodie also included it in a solo collection with practice recording. The Philharmonie guide and a 2005 UNT faculty-list survey use different historical pedagogical categories.

**Sources checked.**
- [Hal Leonard / Southern Music](https://www.halleonard.com/product/3773701/an-abstract) (opened): Publisher page identifies David Ward, alto sax, Southern Music SS101, eight pages, Level 3.
- [WorldCat](https://search.worldcat.org/title/An-abstract-%3A-for-alto-saxophone-and-piano/oclc/4026579) (opened): WorldCat lists the score as alto saxophone and piano, published 1963.
- [University of North Texas dissertation](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark%3A/67531/metadc330729/m2/1/high_res_d/1002715608-Lamar.pdf) (search_index_only): UNT dissertation table of recorded examples identifies Fred Hemke’s recording.
- [Music Minus One catalog listing](https://hsiaoya.com/en/products/advanced-alto-saxophone-solos-volume-3-01400747) (search_index_only): Brodie advanced solo collection listing includes this work and a Paul Brodie alto performance with piano accompaniment.
- [Claude Delangle et al., 10 ans avec le saxophone (Philharmonie de Paris digital scan)](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p.45; printed p.48): The guide calls the work atonal and places it in its fifth-year framework.
- [Scott D. Kallestad, University of North Texas DMA dissertation (2005)](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc4959/m2/1/high_res_d/dissertation.pdf) (opened, Appendix, printed p.89 / PDF p.97): The 2005 UNT dissertation’s appendix reports that An Abstract appeared on one selected college/university repertoire list, labeled freshman by that list. This is a historical survey result, not a current placement recommendation.

### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0080 - Doïna Rotaru - Legend 2|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Doïna Rotaru - Legend 2]]

- Identity: Doïna Rotaru's *Legend II* is cataloged in the IMEP library as a four-page printed work for oboe or soprano saxophone, published by Henry Lemoine. Rotaru's composer catalog lists *Legend II* as an oboe solo work, so the saxophone option is an alternate instrumental edition rather than proof of an original saxophone commission. No score or matching saxophone recording was inspected.
- IMEP institutional catalog record identifies the printed edition as *Legend II: pour hautbois ou saxophone soprano*, Paris: Henry Lemoine, four pages. [Institut royal supérieur de musique et de pédagogie (IMEP) library](https://biblio.imep.be/index.php?lvl=notice_display&id=18785) (search_index_only).
- Romanian composers' catalog lists *Legend II* (1996) for solo oboe, published by Henry Lemoine. [CIMEC Romanian composers catalog](https://muzicieni.cimec.ro/Doina-Rotaru.html) (search_index_only).
- CiNii bibliographic record independently lists *Legend II* for soprano saxophone or oboe, Lemoine, c.1998. [CiNii Books](https://ge.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA54532324) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Alternate saxophone edition of a work also cataloged for solo oboe; this is a transcription/alternate-instrument concern, not an automatic exclusion.
- Gaps: No score pages or saxophone performance inspected; technical demands and intended saxophone audience remain unknown.; Publication date differs between undated IMEP record and c.1998 CiNii record.

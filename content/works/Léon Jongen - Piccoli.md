### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0239 - Léon Jongen - Piccoli|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Léon Jongen - Piccoli]]

- Identity: Léon Jongen’s Piccoli is a two-movement alto saxophone and piano work; an institutional library catalog describes the score as for cello or alto saxophone and piano. A dissertation gives the movement labels “Pseudo vals” and “Pseudo rag” and dates it 1931.
- Royal Conservatoire Antwerp library catalog identifies Piccoli for cello or E-flat alto saxophone and piano, with dedication to Maurice Dambois. [Royal Conservatoire Antwerp library catalog](https://www.libraryconservatoryantwerp.be/lijsten/BSAX.pdf) (search_index_only).
- UPV dissertation describes the saxophone/piano version, 1931 date and two movement labels. [Universitat Politècnica de València dissertation](https://riunet.upv.es/bitstream/handle/10251/4337/tesisUPV2925.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Dorn/Andel catalog lead identifies publisher code ANDEL07 and 20 pages. [Groth Music](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-142040.aspx) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: The institutional catalog lists cello or E-flat alto saxophone with piano. This establishes an alternate-instrument edition, but not which instrument was original.
- Review focus: Inference: the dissertation’s ragtime description for the second movement supports checking its rhythmic articulation and style against the preceding pseudo-waltz.
- Gaps: No notation or recording inspected.; Exact publisher imprint and instrument provenance remain unverified in a primary catalog.
- Proposed correction: Qualify saxophone edition as an alternative to cello, according to institutional library catalog.

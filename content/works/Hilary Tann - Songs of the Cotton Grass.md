### Source and recording verification

The [composer’s instrumentation list](https://hilarytann.com/assets/pdf_files/Selected-Works-by-Instrument.pdf) confirms the soprano voice and soprano saxophone version. The linked recording contains three songs performed by mezzo-soprano Clara O’Brien and soprano saxophonist Susan Fancher.

[New York Women Composers](https://newyorkwomencomposers.org/catalog/songs-of-the-cotton-grass/) lists the published collection as four songs. The linked recording does not include a separately listed Slave’s Song.

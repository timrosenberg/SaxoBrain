Score observations:
- Miniature I starts Impetuoso at quarter note = 126 and uses high ledger-line saxophone writing, slurs alternating with accents/staccato, sudden ff-to-pp contrasts, and dense piano figures. II begins with eight saxophone bars of rest before high sustained notes; meter changes 4/4 to 9/8 and back. III starts at quarter note = 80 and uses syncopated, rest-separated sax entries, slurred sixteenth-note runs, and piano meter changes between 4/4 and 5/4. The preview is excerpt-only; range remains unverified; the cover labels the score transposed. ([score source](https://webshop.donemus.com/action/front/sheetmusic/16432), PDF pp. 3–7)

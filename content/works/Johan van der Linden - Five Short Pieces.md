- Movements verified by recording label: Introduction, Cristallica, Remain a Dreamer, Themism, Be a Realist.

**Notation evidence:** Publisher catalog printed p 28 shows first two alto movements and opening full score. Cristallica begins Largo misterioso quarter 52, long soft sustained notes and chromatic line; Allegro quarter 120 develops accented sixteenth-note figures. Several high ledger-line notes and fingering annotations are visible; no legend available to interpret the annotations.

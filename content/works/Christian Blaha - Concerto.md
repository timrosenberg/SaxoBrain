## Research notes (private)

### Research checked October 16, 2026

**Identity checked:** Saxophone Concerto for alto saxophone and string orchestra (2014), Christian Blaha

**Findings**

- Composer perusal score confirms July 28 to August 28 2014 composition for Femke Steketee, commissioned by Zeeuws Kamerorkest TY.
- Premiere 9 November 2014, St. Jacobskerk Vlissingen, Steketee with TY orchestra, conducted by Blaha.
- Three movements: Allegro vivace; Allegretto; Allegro vivace. Duration about 19 minutes.
- Scoring is alto saxophone plus violin I, violin II, viola, cello, double bass. Donemus D20150 edition copyright 2020 is distinct from 2014 composition.
- Composer links a YouTube performance and a full 36-page public perusal score.

**Notation evidence:** Visually inspected score pages 1 and 3: Allegro vivace quarter=132 in 4/4; saxophone rests during opening string exposition. Page 3 (bars 27 onward) has saxophone sixteenth-note scales, chromatic accidentals, ties and alternating slurred/tongued passages over active string counterpoint. Score is in concert pitch, so displayed saxophone notes must not be treated as written alto range.

**Notation inspected:** Yes, limited to the pages described.

## Research notes (private)

### Chat Batch 16 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

Aquatic (2011) is a roughly ten-minute duet for cor anglais or any saxophone and percussion, published by Faber. The publisher sells the full score and individual wind/percussion parts separately. The inspected score cover explicitly states the saxophone alternative. The Royal Northern College of Music programmed it as a saxophone/percussion duet in its 2016 Davies festival.

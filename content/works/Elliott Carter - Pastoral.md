## Research notes (private)

### Chat batch 08 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Carter, Pastoral: the [composer’s saxophone catalog](https://www.elliottcarter.com/compositions/pastoral-eb-alto-saxophone-and-piano/) confirms 1940 and ten minutes. Saxophonist [Neil McGovern](https://neilmcgovern.weebly.com/carter---pastoral.html) describes a gradually accelerating Allegretto, a quirky Giocoso interruption, a grander reprise, cadenza-like episodes and a lively coda. His 1945 date conflicts with the composer’s date. The [existing Sara Zazo/Eduardo Moreno video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wXuQJv7xIOc) is verified playable and explicitly alto saxophone/piano. The composer page’s English-horn audio is a separate version. Exact Presser 144-40005D has printed and digital options; notation and written range remain gaps.

[[Review 0087 - Elliott Carter - Pastoral|Full review note]]

Catalog: [[Elliott Carter - Pastoral]]

- Identity: Elliott Carter’s *Pastoral* was composed in 1940 and revised in 1982, with a listed duration of ten minutes. Carter’s official catalog lists an alternate E-flat alto saxophone and piano version as well as English-horn and piano, clarinet and piano, viola and piano, and an English-horn version with marimba and strings. The official page’s two recordings are English-horn performances; a separate saxophone recording is listed on Spotify.
- Composer’s official catalog gives date/revision, ten-minute duration, alternate versions including E-flat alto saxophone and piano, and English-horn recording listings. [Elliott Carter official catalog](https://www.elliottcarter.com/compositions/pastoral/) (opened).
- Digital music service lists a 9:20 saxophone recording of Pastoral by Kyle Horch and Pamela Lidiard on *Anglosax*. [Spotify track catalog](https://open.spotify.com/track/3eVr4D0jV7RiC5q0WSsHnQ) (search_index_only).

- Collection/use: Alternate saxophone version of a work that the composer’s site identifies as originally for English horn and piano; the sax version is listed at ten minutes.

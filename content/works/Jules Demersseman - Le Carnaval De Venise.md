Demersseman wrote *Introduction and Variations on 'Le carnaval de Venise'* for saxophonist # Louis Adolphe Mayeur, a student of Adolphe Sax. Mayeur went on to write his own version *[Grande fantaisie brillante sur 'Le carnaval de Venise'](http://localhost:1313/works/louis-adolphe-mayeur-grande-fantaisie-brillante-sur-le-carnaval-de-venise/)*. Both works are based on the folk song *O Mamma, Mamma Cara*, which was popularized in 1829 by Niccolò Paganini in his theme and twenty variations he called *Il Carnevale Di Venezia," Op. 10*.

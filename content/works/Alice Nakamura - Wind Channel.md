### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[SaxoBrain - Review and Repair Record]]

Catalog: [[Alice Nakamura - Wind Channel]]

- Identity: Alice Nakamura's *Wind Channel* (2016) is an original 14-minute work for alto saxophone and piano commissioned by Zen-On Music. Zen-On identifies the three sections and the 2016 Tokyo premiere by Nobuya Sugawa and Michiyo Haneishi; the public score preview and Sugawa's preface are now inspected.
- Official work page gives instrumentation, date, duration, commission, premiere, dedication, and three-section description. [Zen-On Music](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/en/publishing/cr/composers/works/detail/1336/) (opened).
- Publisher release notice is a secondary corroborating lead. [European American Music Distributors](https://www.eamdc.com/news/zen-on-releases-alice-nakamuras-wind-channel-for-alto-saxophone-and-piano/) (opened).
- Public publisher viewer spread printed pp. 6-7 (viewer p. 7) shows the opening solo saxophone notation. It uses changing meters, many accidentals, long sustained notes alternating with rapid beamed figures including septuplets, and marked pp/ppp, mf, and f contrasts. Technique labels include Ord., Sub tone, Slap tonguing, Breath tone, Let vib., and Let vib. gradually. Exact complete range is not established. [Zen-On ActiBook public score preview, spread printed pp. 6-7](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/trial/560034/?detailFlg=0&pNo=7) (printed 6-7; viewer p. 7; score_inspected).
- Publisher preview printed p. 4 (viewer p. 5) contains saxophonist Nobuya Sugawa’s preface, which describes full acoustic range, varied tonguing, and player-selected extended techniques. [Zen-On ActiBook public score preview, Sugawa preface](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/trial/560034/?detailFlg=0&pNo=5) (printed 4; viewer p. 5; opened).
- Composer program note printed p. 5 reiterates the three-section design and the 2016 premiere. [Zen-On ActiBook public score preview, composer program note](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/trial/560034/?detailFlg=0&pNo=6) (printed 5; viewer p. 6; opened).
- Score observation: Opening solo part (printed pp. 6-7): changing meters and frequent accidentals; sustained tones alternate with fast beamed figures, including septuplets. Dynamics range from ppp/pp through mf to f. Notated techniques include ordinary tone, subtone, slap tonguing, breath tone, and vibrato instructions. Complete range not established.

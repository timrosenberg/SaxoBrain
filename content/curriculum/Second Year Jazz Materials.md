# Jazz Scales (memorized)

- [The Blues Scale](https://www.notion.so/saxowiki/The-Scale-Series-f9f85d0fb4ad408abe282b12902b0db4#b40d82495ad84fdea61e4c5154c6b6e7)
- [Pentatonic Scales](https://www.notion.so/saxowiki/The-Scale-Series-f9f85d0fb4ad408abe282b12902b0db4#bdb50793dec14ee89a18623f8e35e7a7)
- [Whole Tone Scales](https://www.notion.so/saxowiki/The-Scale-Series-f9f85d0fb4ad408abe282b12902b0db4#aa97787454514a8fbb708aa81fae7f4b)
- [Diminished Scales](https://www.notion.so/saxowiki/The-Scale-Series-f9f85d0fb4ad408abe282b12902b0db4#6b497b11d7a14ad4947feaa85cca4aac)
- Melodic Minor Scales (ascending form, all keys)
    - formula: `1-2-♭3-4-5-6-7`
- Dorian Mode (all keys)
    - formula: `1-2-♭3-4-5-6-♭7`
- Lydian Mode (all keys)
    - formula: `1-2-3-♯4-5-6-7`
- Mixolydian Mode (all keys)
    - formula: `1-2-3-4-5-6-♭7`
    - a.k.a. “The Bebop Scale”
- Lydian Bebop Scales
    - formula: `1-2-3-♯4-5-6-♭7`
    - a combination of Lydian and Mixolydian modes
- Whole Step Triad Combination Scales (all keys)
    - two major triads, one whole step apart, formed into a scale (i.e. `C + D = C-D-E-F♯-G-A`
    - other ways to think of it:
        - formula: `1-2-3-♯4-5-6`
        - Pentatonic scale with a ♯4
        - Lydian mode without a 7

# Jazz Theory

- Spell all major, minor, augmented, and diminished seventh chords
- Identify every scale degree in all major and minor keys (i.e. What is the 6th scale degree of F minor?)
- Chord/Scale combinations (i.e. what scale(s) can be used on a G7 chord?)

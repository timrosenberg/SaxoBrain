**Identity and findings.** Sherwood Shaffer’s Summer Nocturne is an alto saxophone and piano work in Dorn DORN1405. Andrew Paul Hays’s 2007 DMA dissertation at the University of North Carolina at Greensboro identifies it as a five-movement continuous work written for Jonathan Helton during his undergraduate study at North Carolina School of the Arts.

**Sources checked.**
- [Groth Music](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-146118.aspx) (opened): Retailer page identifies DORN1405 and score/part details.
- [UNCG repository, Andrew Paul Hays DMA dissertation](https://libres.uncg.edu/ir/uncg/f/umi-uncg-1440.pdf) (search_index_only, indexed p. 44, Example 10b): The indexed page 44 passage discusses subtle meter changes tied to melodic phrasing and cites Summer Nocturne, mm. 159-162, Example 10b. The original University of North Carolina at Greensboro dissertation repository record is linked separately; its PDF link returned 403, so this text remains search-index evidence.
- [UNCG NC DOCKS dissertation record](https://libres.uncg.edu/IR/listing.aspx?id=1269) (opened): University repository record identifies Andrew Paul Hays’s 2007 dissertation and describes its performance-analysis scope.

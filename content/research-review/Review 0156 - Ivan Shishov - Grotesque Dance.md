## Research

Catalog: [[Ivan Shishov - Grotesque Dance]]

- Identity: Ivan Shishov, Grotesque Dance from The Master Barber, in an alto saxophone and piano edition, Edition Musicus EM157AS. A separate bassoon-and-piano arrangement by Felix Clyde exists; the sax edition’s arranger/provenance remains unresolved.
- A saxophone repertoire list identifies the E-flat saxophone and piano edition as Grotesque Dance from The Master Barber. [Adolphesax repertoire list](https://adolphesax.com/repertorio-saxofon-alto-ultima-actualizacion-2008/) (search_index_only).
- The Illinois Grade School Music Association lists the related title as a Class B alto saxophone solo, credited Shishov/Felix and Edition Musicus. [IGSMA solo list](https://www.igsmasouth.org/Downloads/IGSMA/Doc/Instrumental_Solo_Book.pdf) (search_index_only).
- IMSLP worklist result identifies a Felix Clyde bassoon-and-piano arrangement of the opera excerpt. [IMSLP worklist](https://imslp.org/wiki/List_of_works_by_Ivan_Shishov) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: An opera excerpt adapted for saxophone. A school solo-list appearance is documented, but does not establish simplification or college exclusion.
- Gaps: Saxophone edition score and its arranger attribution were not inspected; do not assume the bassoon arrangement is the saxophone edition.

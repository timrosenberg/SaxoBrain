## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Exact arrangement now identified: Stanton’s record gives alto saxophone, Lloyd Conley, Kendor 11434 and Grade 3. J.W. Pepper independently identifies alto solo with piano, supplier 11434 and Pepper 8603137. Publisher Vienna Wind Music explicitly dates the original Rondeau’s composition to 1729 and identifies it as the first movement of Première Suite de Symphonies. This is original-work history, not the date of Conley’s saxophone arrangement. Boosey & Hawkes’ edition description independently identifies the original as the first movement of First Suite in D, from Fanfares for Trumpets, Kettledrums, Violins and Oboes, published circa 1729. Original orchestral instrumentation does not establish Conley’s saxophone demands. Conley’s publisher-issued Grade 3 trumpet/piano version says it offers performance to student musicians. That statement concerns trumpet 11892; it cannot automatically be assigned to alto 11434. The exact alto edition’s retailer Grade 3 is the available teaching-market evidence. The 2023 IGSMA school contest list was opened and searched. Mouret/Conley entries were found for flute and orchestra bells, not alto saxophone. Those other-instrument rows are not used as evidence of this alto arrangement’s contest assignment. Prior Galliford Musicnotes observations concern a separate Easy Classical Themes Level 1 arrangement. They do not establish notation or intended use of Conley 11434. Searches found a San Francisco Saxophone Quartet Rondeau track and orchestral/trumpet versions. They were rejected as streaming candidates for this alto/piano arrangement.

Collection context: One movement from a Baroque orchestral suite in an exact Conley/Kendor alto/piano transcription. Retailer Grade 3 is documented; actual youth simplification and college/professional placement unknown.

Open questions: Conley 11434 saxophone notation remains uninspected. Full form, duration, written range, transposition, articulation and technical demands remain unknown. No exact alto/piano Conley recording verified. Current print availability unknown: retailer records differ and a catalog entry does not establish live stock. No exact alto-specific school syllabus or college/professional usage verified.

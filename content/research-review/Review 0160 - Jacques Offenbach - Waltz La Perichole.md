## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Kjos’s official exact-edition page identifies Offenbach, arranger Forrest L. Buchtel, edition S440, alto saxophone and piano accompaniment, publisher grade 1.5, and publication date March 2, 1959. This is the arrangement’s publication date, not an original-composition date. Groth’s public S440 cover visibly confirms E-flat alto saxophone solo with piano accompaniment and Buchtel’s arrangement. Live product text says piano score plus solo part and 4 pages. The publisher’s stated educational grade is evidence of intended teaching use. No opened source explicitly calls this a simplification for young players. No saxophone college/professional performance was verified. Clarinet, bassoon and trumpet editions of the same Buchtel title occur in catalogs and school lists. Their notation, instrument use and rankings must remain separate from alto edition S440.

Collection context: Operetta excerpt in a publisher-graded educational arrangement. Explicit simplification unknown. College/professional use unknown.

Open questions: Only cover inspected; no notation available from publisher page or exact Groth image endpoint. Written range, rhythm, tempo, duration and piano demands remain unknown. No exact alto saxophone recording verified after two YouTube metadata searches. Other instruments and original opera performances were excluded. Exact excerpt location and source-version date within La Périchole remain unknown. Do not use 1959 publication as composed year.

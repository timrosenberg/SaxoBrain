## Decision evidence

**Catalog update:** The approved attribution or identity correction is applied. The original catalog wording in the evidence below records the pre-correction state.

**Tim’s decision:** Advanced; composer corrected to Pascale Jakubowski. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Candidate-work notation. **Scoring:** Candidate Pascale Jakubowski Ibéji IV: soprano saxophone and piano.. **Duration:** Unknown..

- The inspected opening page combines long slurred lines, frequent accidentals and rapidly beamed descending and ascending figures.
- Dynamics include ppp, p, mf and f, with repeated hairpins and abrupt changes.
- Multiphonic chords appear with fingering diagrams and timed holds, including four, five and six seconds.
- The opening solo’s footnote directs the saxophonist to play into the open piano while its resonance pedal remains depressed. This makes instrument placement and resonance part of preparation.
- The page requests a lament-like, flexible character. Later pages, full range, duration, ensemble passages and the complete multiphonic vocabulary remain unverified.

**Candidate identity:** The score identifies the composer shown in the source note. Tim must confirm that identity before catalog attribution is changed.

**Inspected scope and limits:** One actual notation page inspected visually: first music page of the public Musik Fabrik soprano/piano sample on Sheet Music Plus. It bears copyright 1999 Éditions de la Fabrique Musique. Claims are limited to that opening solo page, not all 27 pages.

**Edition or identity note:** Catalog names Pedro Jakubowski. The composer website and sample title page name Pascale Jakubowski. An alto/accordion listing mislabels itself Ibéji IV; the composer calls that work Ibéji V, and its notation was excluded.

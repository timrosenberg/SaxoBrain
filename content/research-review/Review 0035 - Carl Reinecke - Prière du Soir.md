Catalog: removed at Tim’s request on 2026-10-13.

Carl Reinecke’s Prière du Soir appears to be Marty Lenard’s saxophone arrangement of Abendgebet, no. 10 from Mädchenlieder Op. 88. The original is catalogued for piano; the assigned Dorn listing is the only lead found for the saxophone/piano edition and could not be opened.
[J.W. Pepper](https://www.jwpepper.com/priere-du-soir-11602696/p) (search_index_only): Retailer product page identifies a separate euphonium/piano arrangement by Marty Lenard and calls the source tender and contemplative; this is not the saxophone edition and cannot establish its saxophone part.
[Tomplay](https://tomplay.com/fr/piano-sheet-music/reinecke/m-dchenlieder-op-88-no-10-abendgebet-evening-player-piano-score) (search_index_only): A product catalog identifies the source tune as Abendgebet, no. 10 from Reinecke’s Mädchenlieder Op. 88, for piano; the page does not show the saxophone transcription.

Collection/transcription context: Likely transcription/arrangement of Reinecke piano source; no evidence found about intended player age or simplification.

Review focus: Inference: treat the saxophone piece as an arrangement until its score is checked; the source piano work’s lyrical characterization does not establish the adaptation’s demands.

Open questions: Dorn saxophone edition DORN2010 and arranger credit remain search-lead-only. No readable saxophone notation, recording, or college/professional programming verified.

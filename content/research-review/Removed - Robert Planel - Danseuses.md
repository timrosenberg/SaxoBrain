### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0330 - Robert Planel - Danseuses|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Robert Planel - Danseuses]].

Robert Planel's Suite Romantique no. 2, Danseuses, is an alto saxophone and piano piece, Leduc AL20247.
[Hal Leonard / Leduc distributor listing](https://www.halleonard.com/product/48181098/suite-romantique-no-2-dancerssuite-romantique-no-2-dancers) (search_index_only): Publisher's distributor listing identifies the piece as no. 2 of six, for alto saxophone and piano; it gives a five-part form and describes the main theme returning at the end.
[CDMC / WindMusic library catalog](https://windmusic.org/index.php?lvl=notice_display&id=11435) (opened): An opened library catalog identifies the six-piece alto saxophone/piano suite, Leduc, 1944.
[Philharmonie de Paris, Dix ans avec le saxophone](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p. 44 (printed p. 47)): The Philharmonie de Paris ten-year saxophone guide describes no. 2, Danseuses, as light and animated in 2/4, quarter note = 96, with varied articulation and frequent tempo changes; listed duration 2:30. This is a guide description, not direct score inspection.

Collection/transcription context: A work from an original six-piece saxophone/piano suite. A distributor calls this piece intermediate; that rating is not a SaxoBrain level.

Review focus: The guide reports an animated 2/4 pulse at quarter note = 126. Inspect the score for articulation and rhythmic execution; no notation was inspected.

Open questions: No college performance or teaching placement verified. No score preview or performance was inspected; the guide’s description is secondary repertoire context, not notation evidence.

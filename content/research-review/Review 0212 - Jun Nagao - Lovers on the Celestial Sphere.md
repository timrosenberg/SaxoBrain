**Identity and findings.** Jun Nagao’s Lovers on the Celestial Sphere is Zen-On 560015 for soprano saxophone, tenor saxophone and piano, composed on commission from Nobuya Sugawa. Zen-On gives about 12 minutes, while an institutional catalog gives about 12.5 for the later band study score. Zen-On’s public four-page sample includes a preface and an opening score leaf.

**Sources checked.**
- [Zen-On publisher catalog](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/en/publishing/cr/composers/detail/67/works/?page=1) (opened): Lists scoring as soprano saxophone, tenor saxophone and piano, approximately 12 minutes.
- [National Diet Library of Japan](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000136-I1970023484963465498) (opened): Study score is the wind-band version; records original performance medium as soprano/tenor saxophone and piano, approximately 12.5 minutes.
- [Kenneth Tse performer discography](https://www.kenneth-tse.com/discography/stellar-saxes) (opened): Lists the piece for soprano and tenor saxophones and piano.
- [Crystal Records](https://www.crystalrecords.com/kennethtse.html) (opened): Album listing credits the work and pianist Kazuo Murakami.
- [Zen-On official product page](https://shop.zen-on.co.jp/item/detail/560015/) (opened): Zen-On product 560015 confirms the commissioned soprano/tenor/piano work, first performance in 2005 by Nobuya Sugawa, Sadahito Kuniuse and pianist Minako Koyanagi, and an approximate 12-minute duration.
- [Zen-On public score trial](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/trial/560015/?detailFlg=0&pNo=1) (score_inspected, opening score leaf in sample): Opened Zen-On’s public ActiBook sample (4 pages). The preface identifies the 2005 premiere and the opening printed score leaf was visually inspected.
- [NASA conference program search result](https://www.saxophonealliance.org/assets/2018/2018-NASA-Conference-Program-Book.pdf) (search_index_only): A NASA conference program search result lists the work in a collegiate solo competition event; the PDF URL returned 404 on opening, so the program context remains indexed-only.

**Score observations.**
- The opening sample page shows soprano and tenor parts over piano, marked Con espressione at a slow pulse, with legato saxophone lines and a flowing, chordal piano texture. The sample does not show enough of the work to infer complete range or later movement demands.

**Score/sample.**
- [Official four-page Zen-On ActiBook sample; opening score leaf visually inspected.](https://www.zen-on.co.jp/trial/560015/?detailFlg=0&pNo=1) (inspected).

**Collection and audience context.** Original saxophone duo with piano; separately available wind-band study score is not the original trio format.

**Review focus.** Inference from the inspected opening: assess blend between the two saxophone lines and the flowing piano texture, then review later handoffs and contrasts in the complete edition.

**Open questions.**
- Only an opening score leaf and preface were available in the public sample; the remainder of the duo score was not inspected.
- The later band study-score timing differs slightly from Zen-On’s original chamber-work estimate.

## Chat batch 10 follow-up, 2026-10-14

The composer estate’s live work catalog dates the alto saxophone and piano Sonate 1957 and names Tonger. It separately lists Divertimento Op. 74 for alto saxophone and cello (1952), so these are distinct works. Frank Lunte’s label-published notes identify Sonata Op. 74a as composed 1957 and dedicated to Emil Manz. They explain Raphael added in memoriam after Manz died during that year. EDA’s track list verifies three movements: Con moto, Vivace, Allegro molto energico. Exact public Naxos/EDA Topic recordings exist for each movement, 3:42, 4:25 and 4:23, total 12:30. Lunte’s interpretive notes characterize a determined Con moto, restless Vivace interrupted by reflective self-dialogue, and an uncompromising energetic finale. These are performer interpretations that suggest expressive contrast; they are not direct score observations. IMEP institutional library indexes Tonger’s Saxophonar edition as23 pages plus saxophone part. The record was search-index-only this turn because the detail endpoint failed.

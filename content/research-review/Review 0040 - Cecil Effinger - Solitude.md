## Chat batch 07 follow-up, 2026-10-13

The publisher’s current product page directly confirms E-flat alto saxophone and piano, score and parts,8 pages, edition114-40143. It offers print and paid digital/download formats and says the material is made available from the composer’s original edition. The University of Arkansas documents Solitude by Effinger in a 20 January 2004 chamber recital with Stan Morris, saxophone, and Linda Beckman, piano. Florida State University also catalogs it on a 7 July 1980 faculty-recital tape. University of Colorado’s Effinger archive lists Solitude, Op. 64No. 1,1960, and an English-horn-and-strings Solitude dated 1978. Without a score carrying the opus, these are historical leads rather than proof of the saxophone edition’s original date.

Score observations: No actual musical page was available in the opened Presser listing. The Groth image is the alto-and-piano cover only. Range, rhythmic demands, articulation, style and extended techniques remain unknown.

Collection context: A separately published saxophone/piano edition with verified university chamber and faculty-recital context. Original-versus-adaptation history remains unresolved; no simplified youth-transcription evidence.

Open questions: Composition date cannot safely be filled from the 1960 archival item until opus and version match are verified. No musical score or exact playable recording was verified. Faculty use supports historical performance context but does not establish difficulty. Do not attach same-title scores or recordings by Justin Wildridge, Duke Ellington or Andrew March. They are different works.

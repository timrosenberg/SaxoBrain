Catalog: [[Wijnand Van Klaveren - Sonata à la manière de Francis Poulenc]]

- Identity: Wijnand van Klaveren’s Sonate à la manière de Francis Poulenc (1998) is for alto saxophone and piano and is dedicated to Louis van Dijk. An Indiana University Doctor of Music dissertation analyzes all three movements and reproduces score excerpts. Its opening movement alternates Allegro and Paisiblement sections before returning to Allegro, with an ascending thirty-second-note saxophone pickup.
- Composer/publisher page gives instrumentation, date, competition use, and describes first-movement thematic construction. [Resolute Music composer/publisher](https://resolute-music.com/collections/all-saxophone/products/sonata-a-la-maniere-de-francis-poulenc) (opened).
- Indiana University Doctor of Music dissertation identifies the 1998 alto saxophone and piano sonata, its dedication to Louis van Dijk, and analyzes its three movements; printed pp. 58-60 discuss first-movement sections and score examples. [Indiana University Jacobs School of Music dissertation](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/iuswrrest/api/core/bitstreams/0c385609-cd57-4066-8b2e-a7374558f07d/content) (printed 58-60; opened).
- Dissertation describes the opening saxophone gesture as an ascending seven-note thirty-second-note pickup and reproduces the opening score excerpt; the same motif recurs through the movement, including six appearances in piano. [Indiana University Jacobs School of Music dissertation](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/iuswrrest/api/core/bitstreams/0c385609-cd57-4066-8b2e-a7374558f07d/content) (printed 59 / PDF 69; score_inspected).

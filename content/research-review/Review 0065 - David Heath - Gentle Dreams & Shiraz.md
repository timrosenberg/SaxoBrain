## Chat batch 08 follow-up, 2026-10-13

The publisher explicitly identifies Gerard McChrystal as arranger for soprano saxophone and piano. The pair had earlier been prepared for Kirsten Spratt’s flute recording. Just Flutes describes two contrasting jazz-rooted pieces, playable separately or as a pair. This is an accompanied two-piece collection rather than one continuous composition. The official ABRSM Woodwind syllabus from 2022 names the Camden soprano edition in its B-flat saxophone repertoire. Gentle Dreams appears in the Grade 5 list; Shiraz appears in Grade 7 with the upper line of the ossia specified. This is source-attributed teaching context, not a SaxoBrain level recommendation. This official evidence corrects the prior review’s suggestion that tenor exam use necessarily refers to a separate tenor adaptation. The exam uses the soprano-titled Camden edition. A Your Online Pianist practice item explicitly offers Gentle Dreams demonstrated on tenor, with piano-only practice beginning at figure A. Its previews and full practice materials differ in access; no full freely playable sax performance of both pieces was verified. O’Connor’s dissertation abstract dates the original Gentle Dreams for Julian Lloyd Webber to 1983. Shiraz’s original year and the paired sax edition date remain unverified, so one composition year cannot safely describe the collection. Trinity’s May 2026 update independently confirms CM115 in soprano/tenor lists and says to use the ossia at the end of Shiraz. This is an exam-specific variant instruction, with no evidence of a separately simplified publication.

Collection context: Two-piece arrangement collection with documented school/exam teaching context. No explicit simplification of original material established; Shiraz has an ossia choice documented by the exam instructions.

Open questions: No actual soprano/tenor score notation inspected. No full freely playable exact sax recording of the pair found; flute/cello/solo-piano versions were excluded. Original Shiraz year, sax arrangement year and full written range remain unknown.

Score observations: Opening page only, concert-pitch full score: Andante in common time, with a piano introduction before the solo enters. https://www.forsyths.co.uk/787862/ghidoni-armando-nocturne-et-sicilienne-alto-saxophone-piano.jpg The saxophone entry is marked mf with an expressive instruction, with long slurred phrases and sustained notes interrupted by short flowing figures. A review should consider phrase shape and breath planning. https://www.forsyths.co.uk/787862/ghidoni-armando-nocturne-et-sicilienne-alto-saxophone-piano.jpg The piano part moves through triplet and faster arpeggiated figures with chromatic accidentals while the saxophone sustains and phrases more slowly. This makes ensemble coordination and harmonic listening relevant at the opening. https://www.forsyths.co.uk/787862/ghidoni-armando-nocturne-et-sicilienne-alto-saxophone-piano.jpg The preview does not include the Sicilienne or establish the complete range.

**Evidence:** Notation. **Scoring:** Alto saxophone and piano. **Duration:** about 16 minutes 30 seconds.

- Long soft trills and tied tones at quarter = 54-56 require steadiness and smooth changes of fingering.
- Rapid ascending runs break out of the trills; the score explicitly requests very rapid playing.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Movement I, score pages 4-5, opening through the senza tempo passage at bar 19 and its continuation. Movements II-III and the full solo range were not inspected.

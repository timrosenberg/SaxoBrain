Catalog: [[Bertold Hummel - Sonata Brevis]]

- Identity: Bertold Hummel's *Sonata brevis*, Op. 95a (1991), is an 18-minute, three-movement work for E-flat alto saxophone and piano, written at saxophonist Normand DesChênes's suggestion and dedicated to him. Hummel's site describes a two-theme sonata opening, a hymn quotation in the arch-shaped slow movement, and a variable-metre rondo finale that ends in a virtuoso stretta. It lists the 1993 premiere and professional recordings.
- Composer foundation provides instrumentation, date, duration, movements, dedication, premiere, formal/technical descriptions, autograph references, and publisher number. [Bertold Hummel official site](https://bertoldhummel.de/en/work/op-95a) (opened).
- Composer discography identifies Lutz Koppetsch and Markus Bellheim 2007 recording. [Bertold Hummel official discography](https://bertoldhummel.de/en/discography/sonata-brevis-fuer-altsaxophon-und-klavier) (opened).

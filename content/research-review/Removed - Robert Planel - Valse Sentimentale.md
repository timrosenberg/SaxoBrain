### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0333 - Robert Planel - Valse Sentimentale|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Robert Planel - Valse Sentimentale]].

Planel's Valse Sentimentale is no. 4 of Suite Romantique, for alto saxophone and piano, Leduc AL20249.
[Finna / University of Oulu catalog](https://www.finna.fi/Record/oy.996786206252) (opened): University of Oulu's opened library record identifies no. 4, Valse sentimentale, Leduc AL20249, one score (5 pages) plus part.
[Philharmonie de Paris, Dix ans avec le saxophone](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p. 44 (printed p. 47)): The Philharmonie de Paris ten-year saxophone guide describes no. 4, Valse sentimentale, as a moderate waltz at quarter note = 160, with a slower middle section and return to the opening theme; listed duration three minutes. This is a guide description, not direct score inspection.

Collection/transcription context: Original piece in Planel’s six-work saxophone/piano suite. The guide uses a fifth-year label, which is retained as source terminology without conversion to a college year.

Review focus: The guide reports an animated 2/4 pulse at quarter note = 126. Inspect the score for articulation and rhythmic execution; no notation was inspected.

Open questions: No score pages or performance inspected.

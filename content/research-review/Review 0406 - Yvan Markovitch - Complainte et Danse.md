Yvan Markovitch’s Complainte et Danse is an alto saxophone and piano work, Leduc AL23637. A 2024 University of Music Münster festival program identifies saxophone and piano; NYPL’s older catalog record gives the composer variant “Ivan Markovitch” and a c.1966 Leduc imprint.

Alphonse Leduc AL23637 is the alto saxophone and piano edition. The cover was visually inspected, but it contains no notation. The primary teaching guide 10 ans avec le saxophone lists a duration of about 5 minutes 35 seconds and a slow complaint followed by a fast dance. It recommends the end of seventh-year study. Apple’s catalog identifies a complete recording by Kyle Horch and pianist Yshani Perinpanayagam. Metadata was verified; audio was not auditioned. The existing 1966 value is retained. The guide prints 1966, but it does not explicitly distinguish composition from publication; the older copyright citation alone is insufficient to resolve that distinction.

**Score observations:** No actual notation inspected. Published teaching-guide description identifies Lent at quarter note 40, a moderate transition at 80 and Vif at 144. Technical observations are attributed to that guide rather than claimed as a score reading.

**Collection context:** Original saxophone concert work with professional recordings. Its approachable level does not make it a lyrical transcription.

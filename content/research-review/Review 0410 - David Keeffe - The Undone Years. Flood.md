## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Australian Music Centre gives 25 minutes and four movements: The undone years; The old lie; Spring wind; Legs as quick as lilac-shoots. AMC describes the solo part as virtuosic, requiring altissimo and multiphonics, with moderate band parts. Its difficulty label is Advanced. Commissioned by Jason Xanthoudakis; first performed by Xanthoudakis with Grainger Wind Symphony and Roland Yeung on 26 February 2011 at St Stephen’s Church, Richmond, Victoria. The composer’s supplied AMC overview says Fire and Flood largely share material, with very different orchestration. Flood emphasizes heavy, dark, overwhelming effects. AMC explicitly has no score or recording products for this version and directs enquiries to the composer or agent.

Collection context: Original concerto for professional soloist and wind ensemble; no young-player simplification documented. Fire’s chamber materials must not be treated as Flood’s score.

Open questions: No public Flood notation or exact playable Flood recording verified. No Flood-specific publisher or purchase/download link verified.

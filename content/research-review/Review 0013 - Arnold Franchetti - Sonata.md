- Identity: A saxophone sonata attributed to Arnold Franchetti is listed in the Regni Sax Solo-Duet Collection index. A 2013 scholarly article identifies a *Saxophone Sonata* from 1970 in its discussion of Franchetti's works, and a dissertation excerpt connects Franchetti's saxophone writing to Donald Sinta. These sources support the work's existence, but saxophone type, instrumentation, edition, publisher, and score remain unverified. The 1970 and 1971 dates conflict across sources and remain unresolved.
- Saxophone collection index lists Sonata for saxophone and piano under Franchetti. [Living Jazz Archives, Regni Sax Solo-Duet Collection](https://livingjazzarchives.org/cms/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Regni-Sax-Solo-Duet-Collection.pdf) (opened).
- VIAF lead lists saxophone/piano sonatas among works, but does not identify the saxophone type or edition. [VIAF](https://viaf.org/viaf/29955865) (search_index_only).
- 2013 scholarly article identifies a Saxophone Sonata dated 1970 among Franchetti's works and describes its writing as imaginative and improvisatory. [Mondo Ladino 37 (2013), scholarly article](https://www.istladin.net/web/pdf/MondoLadino/ml2013.pdf) (115; opened).
- A doctoral dissertation excerpt identifies Arnold Franchetti among composers commissioned by Donald Sinta to write for saxophone; its extracted work list identifies a Franchetti Sonata dated 1971. [Library and Archives Canada dissertation repository](https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol2/BVAU/TC-BVAU-42002.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Scholarly source confirms a saxophone sonata and a Donald Sinta commission context; exact intended audience and edition are unknown.
- Review focus: Inference from the scholarly description of imaginative, improvisatory writing: inspect the score for notation or timing that realizes this character; no score pages were found.
- Gaps: Sources report both 1970 and 1971; reconcile the date and establish saxophone type, accompaniment, publisher, and exact edition.; No readable score, verified saxophone recording, or specific college program located.

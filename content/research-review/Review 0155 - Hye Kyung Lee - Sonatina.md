## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

The composer’s works page dates the soprano saxophone and piano Sonatina to 1993. It names Drivingly, Freely and Intensely, with timing examples 4:00, 2:40 and 7:20. This supersedes the previous note’s unsupported inference that a 2001 recital date was the composition year. The composer separately lists a 2003 Sonatina for soprano saxophone and wind ensemble. Its scoring and version date should stay separate from the piano edition. Musik Fabrik’s live composer page confirms a soprano-saxophone-and-piano score and part publication. It reports composition awards for the Sonatina in 1996 and 1998, further establishing that the work predates the 2001 recital. The composer’s discography identifies Todd Yukumoto and HyeKyung Lee’s complete Sonatina recording on Blue, Equilibrium EQ64, 2004. The three exact public YouTube Topic tracks are labeled Allegro, Largo and Allegretto, with combined duration 15:33. These recorded titles differ from the current composer work-list labels. Kutztown University’s Suggested Saxophone Repertoire lists Lee’s Sonatina in its all-levels soprano repertoire. This documents college teaching use without fixing a SaxoBrain difficulty level. No young-player simplification was verified.

Collection context: Original saxophone/piano composition in three movements. College teaching repertoire and commercial professional recording verified; no documented young-player simplification.

Open questions: No readable notation inspected. Exact written range, rhythmic detail, articulation and piano coordination remain unknown.

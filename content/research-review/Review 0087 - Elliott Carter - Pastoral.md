## Chat batch 08 follow-up, 2026-10-13

The composer’s specific saxophone page dates the work 1940, lists ten minutes, and identifies it as a version of the English-horn work. Groth’s exact saxophone item identifies Presser 14440005D. Ficks confirms the printed and digital-download options for 144-40005D, 20 pages, ISBN 9781491108741. Saxophonist Neil McGovern’s program note describes an initial Allegretto that gains speed, an interrupting Giocoso, a more expansive return of opening material, brief cadenza-like passages and a lively concluding coda. His 1945 date conflicts with the composer’s composition date and is not adopted. The University of Iowa’s Center for New Music program notes describe the work’s restrained jazz influence and linear counterpoint as indebted to Walter Piston. This note accompanies a clarinet recital, so it supplies musical context rather than saxophone-specific technique claims. The existing YouTube URL was verified as Sara Zazo and Eduardo Moreno performing alto saxophone and piano at Fundación Juan March, Madrid, February 2, 2020. It is the saxophone version. The original composer page’s audio and listed Holliger/Hove recordings are English horn. They must not be labeled saxophone performances.

Collection context: Authorized saxophone version of an original English-horn work. No youth transcription or simplified school edition was verified.

Open questions: No readable saxophone notation sample was exposed on the inspected Groth, Ficks or official composition pages. Written range, exact meters and articulations remain unverified from notation. Ten-minute official duration differs from the twelve-minute retail estimate in the separately indexed Sheet Music Plus entry. The verified Zazo video is 9:34 including its presentation. McGovern’s 1945 date should be treated as conflicting program-note dating. Original composition 1940 and edition/publication dates must remain separate.

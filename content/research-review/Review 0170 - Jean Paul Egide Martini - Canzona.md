## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Groth confirms piano score, alto part, an alternative B-flat clarinet/soprano/tenor saxophone/tenor trombone part, and a tuba part in the same item. The public score cover explicitly reads J.P.E. Martini, Canzona, Arr. Jos de Klerk, with alto saxophone and piano and optional clarinet, soprano or tenor saxophone, tenor horn or tuba. The Royal Conservatoire Antwerp library independently attributes the arrangement to Jos de Klerk and records Molenaar 1952, four pages. This is publication evidence, not the composition year of Martini’s original. The Dutch KNMO 2010 wind solo repertoire list indexes Martini’s Canzona for B-flat saxophone in 4e Divisie. The indexed source gives no edition number and no age bracket, so it provides bounded local repertoire context rather than a SaxoBrain level.

Collection context: Multi-instrument wind arrangement of earlier Martini music. Local Dutch wind solo repertoire use is documented; youth simplification is not established.

Open questions: Only edition cover inspected, with no notes. Technical demands and exact solo ranges remain unknown. The original Martini source work and composition date were not located. No exact public saxophone recording verified. KNMO PDF open failed and its catalog-number match is unresolved.

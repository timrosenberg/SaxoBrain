## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Opened CiNii exact work record BA90401771 gives F. Combelle, Paris, Andrieu Frères, c1938, one score of two pages and one part on one sheet. It takes the title from the score caption. The 1938 date is a publication/copyright catalog date, not a verified original composition year. The recording label Clarinet & Saxophone Classics identifies Marcel Mule’s historical Variations track on CC0013 as Genin/Combelle. Its newer catalog credits François Combelle alone for Variations sur Malborough. MusicBrainz’s opened Le Saxophone français compilation record identifies disc 3 track 18, 2:58, as François Combelle, Marcel Mule on alto saxophone and Marcel Gaveau on piano. These are secondary metadata, not auditioned sound. Important identity uncertainty: the label’s Genin/Combelle credit suggests a source-composer/arranger relationship. No score or booklet comparison verified the relationship between that recorded track and the two-page Andrieu Malbrough edition.

Collection context: Historical alto and piano variation work. An apparent Génin/Combelle arrangement relationship is flagged for comparison, not settled. No explicit young-player simplification documented.

Open questions: No readable exact Andrieu score found; variation count, written range, rhythmic demands and articulation unknown. No exact playable recording verified. Historical recording identities are documented, but their relation to this printed edition remains uncertain. Original composition year unknown; 1938 should not be promoted from copyright/publication to composed.

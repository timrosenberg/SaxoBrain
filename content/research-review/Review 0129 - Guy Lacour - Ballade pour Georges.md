## Chat batch 10 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Live publisher HTML verifies the piano reduction GB6014, 48 pages, Daniel Gremelle collection, publisher classification Difficult (cycle 3), and 1996 copyright. Copyright is not used as composition year. The Confédération Musicale de France woodwind annals place Ballade pour Georges, with a specified cut from 8 to 20, in the 1997 end-of-third-cycle alto saxophone repertoire history. This is direct institutional teaching context; it does not establish a SaxoBrain level or certify the uncut work’s demands. The publisher separately identifies orchestral/string-quintet version GB6013 O, approximately 15 minutes, and a septet product GB6013. These editions are distinguished from the piano reduction. A user-uploaded catalog scan indexes 1994, dedication to Daniel Gremelle in homage to Georges Gourdet, 1995 premiere, and 14:50 duration. Those details remain provisional because the original catalog provenance was not authenticated.

Collection context: Publisher and CMF explicitly document advanced French teaching/examination circulation; no young-player simplification established for the full work. The CMF entry is a cut version.

Open questions: No public readable notation found; publisher HTML has no PDF preview and Groth sample area is empty. No exact public saxophone-and-piano recording verified in composer/title/performer searches. 1994 composition date remains unconfirmed in authenticated primary evidence.

Sources checked:

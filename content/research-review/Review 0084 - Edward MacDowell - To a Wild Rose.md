## Research

Catalog: [[Edward MacDowell - To a Wild Rose]]

- Identity: The cataloged alto saxophone and piano version is a Richard Hall arrangement published by Dorn Publications as DORN1345. This is distinct from the Willis Coggins arrangement in Belwin’s *Student Instrumental Course, Level Two*, which is cataloged separately and derives from MacDowell’s original solo-piano miniature in *Woodland Sketches*, Op. 51. The target DORN1345 score was not available for inspection.
- Catalog lead identifies Dorn Publications DORN1345 and Richard Hall as arranger for the saxophone and piano item. [Groth Music catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-145581.aspx) (search_index_only).
- CiNii library record describes Coggins/Girlamo version for alto saxophone and piano as Level Two, first published by Belwin Mills circa 1970, and notes the source was originally solo piano. [CiNii Books, National Institute of Informatics](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA89778568) (search_index_only).
- Belwin listing identifies the separate Willis Coggins arrangement as Grade 2 and its school/community usage label. [Sheet Music Plus distributor listing](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/to-a-wild-rose-1414100.html) (search_index_only).
- Faber Music identifies MacDowell’s original piano work as *To a Wild Rose*, Op. 51 No. 1. [Faber Music](https://www.fabermusic.com/shop/to-a-wild-rose-op51-no1-d24853) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: A transcription of MacDowell’s solo-piano piece. One separately cataloged Coggins saxophone edition carries a Level Two school-course designation; that descriptor does not establish the target Dorn edition’s difficulty.
- Gaps: The arranger/version mismatch between the catalog’s Richard Hall DORN1345 item and the Coggins edition is unresolved; locate the Dorn publisher record or score.; No score or saxophone performance inspected for either edition.

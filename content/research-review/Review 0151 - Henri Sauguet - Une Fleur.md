## Chat batch 10 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Fresh publisher browser inspection confirms GB6425 for saxophone with piano or organ, eight pages and copyright 1998. Groth lists six pages; retain this page-count discrepancy privately rather than treating either count as duration. Billaudot's Panorama saxophone Volume 2 includes Une fleur and explicitly labels the anthology préparatoire. This is documented educational collection context. It does not show that the piece is a simplified transcription. Arno Bornkamp's Genuin booklet identifies Une Fleur as an original 1985 alto-saxophone/piano work and labels its 3:11 track a world-premiere recording. His work-specific note connects the music to Sauguet's floral imagination. The Philharmonie saxophone teaching guide, printed page 39, describes a singing character, about four minutes, and a low A to adapt up an octave within the phrase. The saved public guide PDF was visually checked in this review. This is pedagogical commentary about the Panorama anthology, not direct notation inspection of GB6425. Exact Apple Music track metadata identifies Sauguet, Bornkamp and Leo van Doeselaar. Public preview playback was started and the player showed an elapsed timer and ninety-second excerpt length. Playback accessibility was verified, but no audio audition analysis is claimed.

Collection context: Billaudot explicitly includes the piece in a préparatoire anthology. Flag this teaching context for Tim's college/professional collection decision. Bornkamp's professional recording is also verified; anthology placement does not establish that it is a simplification.

## Chat batch 12 follow-up, 2026-10-14

1974 is printed at the top of the actual score, separately from copyright 1983. Antwerp library explicitly identifies 1974 as year of composition, and 1983 as publication year. Lemoine’s opened exact-edition page confirms E-flat alto saxophone and piano, 24663, Claude Delangle collection, and its own Advanced label. It documents a premiere by René Decouais and several performances by the Delangle Duo. Tom Bergeron’s performer-authored saxophone multiphonics study lists Sève among repertoire with multiphonics. This corroborates that the opening page alone cannot stand for the whole technique profile. The publisher sells printed and digital versions. The digital product is paid sheet music, so it is not a free-download link.

Score observations: Exact Lemoine 24663 alto part printed page 2, opening only: treble clef and quarter-note 144, presque rauque. The opening alternates abrupt fff attacks with pp notes, rests and chromatic low-register figures. No meter signature is shown on this inspected page. Actual opening notation includes flutter-tongue markings Flatt., accented separated notes, sustained tones, tied chromatic notes, sans vibrer and a marked timbral oscillation. These observations refer to the written E-flat alto part; sounding pitch is not inferred. The page instructs the player to preserve the fingering of low C while producing another notated pitch. This is a concrete harmonic and fingering demand, separate from conventional fingered-note changes. Preparation inference from this page: quick low-register attacks and dynamic changes, flutter tongue, color control and the special retained-fingering effect need work. Remaining score is needed to identify all multiphonics and the complete range.

Collection context: Original saxophone/piano work with documented premiere and professional duo performances. No young-player simplification verified.

Open questions: Only first alto-part page inspected. Full-work written range and later ensemble coordination remain unverified. No matching YouTube performance located.

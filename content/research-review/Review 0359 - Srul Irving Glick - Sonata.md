## Chat Batch 16 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

**Proposed decision:** Retain, difficulty unknown. **Confidence:** high. **Approval:** Applied 2026-10-15.

**Reason:** Retain the serious three-movement original. Label notes describe cantillation, a quasi-improvisatory slow movement and klezmer/fugue finale. These stylistic descriptions do not establish range, speed or technical density for a specific college year.

Estate catalog lists Sonata for Alto Saxophone and Piano (Adio), 1992, 20:42, Dorn. Ravello booklet independently identifies Adio 1992 and its three movements. Brodie/Tryon Apple recording gives20:40 with the same three movement characters. Existing 1972 conflicts with both estate and recording-label documents.

**Collection context:** Complete Adio sonata; distinct from the soprano sonata on same recording.

**Remaining gaps:** No public notation preview or graded exact-work evidence found. CMC source used in old research could not be reopened; verify provenance of old 1972 before applying correction.

**Sources:** [Srul Irving Glick official estate](https://www.srulirvingglick.com/chamber) · [Ravello Records, Metropolis liner notes](https://eclassical.textalk.se/shop/17115/art74/4858474-583a94-713757701211.pdf) · [Apple Music Classical](https://classical.music.apple.com/us/album/302622375) · [Musikforum Demond](https://www.demond.de/srul-irving-glick-sonata-for-alto-saxophone-and-piano-55801) · [Apple public catalog API](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=302622375&entity=song)

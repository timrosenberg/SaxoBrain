**Tim’s decision:** Difficulty unknown; retain unassigned. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Source description / gaps. **Scoring:** Publisher: saxophone or clarinet and piano. Library and festival program specify soprano saxophone.. **Duration:** Unresolved. Retailer lists 4 minutes; Corelia lists 8 minutes. No exact recording timed..

**No notation inspected for this card.** Source descriptions and recording identification are not direct score analysis. Use Hold if the evidence does not support a decision.

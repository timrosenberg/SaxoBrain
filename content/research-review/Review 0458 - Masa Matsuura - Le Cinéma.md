## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Second Year. Approved and applied October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Notation. **Scoring:** Alto saxophone, tenor saxophone and piano, three players. **Duration:** about 9 minutes.

- After nine measures of piano introduction, the alto enters softly with a slurred triplet melody.
- Tenor answers the alto phrase; sustained endings and handoffs need matching tone, intonation and pacing.
- The piano introduction mixes quintuplets, triplets, chromatic figures and wide chord spacings.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Score pages 4-5, bars 1-15; both saxophone entries and the piano introduction inspected. Publisher prefaces also read. Later irregular-meter passages are described in Sugawa’s preface but were not inspected in notation.

**Edition or source note:** Two separate saxophonists are required. This is a trio, with no doubling between the alto and tenor roles.

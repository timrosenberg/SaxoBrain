## Chat batch 07 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Opened publisher page labels it Medium, 12:15, and dedicates it to Lynn Klock of UMass Amherst. Program notes describe jazz-rock fusion energy, a warm evening and a starry sky. The actual opening score carries 2006 copyright. This does not independently establish the original composition year. The composer’s Fusion Suite shop listing specifically sells a bassoon adaptation. Its fourteen-minute duration belongs to that adaptation. Albany Records identifies Klock and the composer as the Fusion Suite performers on Connections, TROY1182 (2010), an album of world-premiere recordings. Composer catalog indexing describes the bari version as advanced and eleven minutes, differing from the publisher’s Medium and 12:15. Its table was not opened, so this remains a source-comparison lead.

Inference from the visible pages: rhythmic precision through ties, fluent connected figures, and shaping long lines against the piano are concrete preparation demands.

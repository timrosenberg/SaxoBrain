## 2. Fabian Rehfeld - Aria

Removed from catalog · [[Review 0091 - Fabian Rehfeld - Aria|Full review]]

Lenard’s exact saxophone preview is now inspected. It opens Adagio molto sostenuto with held, tied and slurred notes, soft dynamics and a brief eighth-note run over sustained chromatic piano harmony. Dorn DP2013 supports soprano, alto, tenor and baritone alternatives; no exact saxophone recording was verified.

**Score observations:** Inspected the exact Lenard/Dorn saxophone-and-piano first-page image. It says transcribed and edited by Marty Lenard and opens Adagio molto sostenuto in 4/4. The saxophone line has no key signature in this sample. The first twelve bars emphasize held half notes, ties and slurred stepwise movement, with a grace-note figure and a descending/returning eighth-note run in bar 8. Dynamics include mf, f, pp and hairpins. The keyboard initially sustains chords before moving through inner chromatic tones. Inference from the sample: phrase continuity, sustained tone and soft dynamics matter more than continuous fast passagework in this opening. This does not establish the complete arrangement’s range or demands. The public source score, printed pp. 32–33, was also inspected. It supplies violin and cello alternatives with piano or organ, sustained writing, later chromatic grace figures and ornament signs. It is not the saxophone edition. The saxophone sample labels its stave only Saxophone. The public image does not identify which B-flat/E-flat part is shown, so sounding transposition and edition-specific range remain unverified.

**Collection context:** Documented transcription. No explicit young-player simplification found. Its original violin score appears in Graded Pieces, but that source collection does not establish the saxophone arrangement’s intended audience.

**Remaining gaps:** No exact accessible saxophone recording identified in targeted searches. Only the first page of the saxophone arrangement was inspected; complete range, later ornaments and B-flat/E-flat transposition remain unknown. Original composition year unknown; 1895 is first publication and 2017 is transcription publication.

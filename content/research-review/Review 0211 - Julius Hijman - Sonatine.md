## Chat batch 12 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Forbidden Music Regained and Donemus identify the original 1934 alto-and-piano work and three movements: Allegretto, Andantino and Allegro con brio. Original manuscript archive reference is 049/H2-04b. The Donemus edition is a 30-page score with a 9-page separate part, sold in printed and paid digital form. A thirteen-page public preview includes front matter and printed score pages 1–9, still within movement I. Its 2021 copyright is edition metadata. Editor Lourens Stuifbergen describes three largely independent contrapuntal lines, a more lyrical middle movement and an energetic finale. His note identifies corrections of apparent manuscript slips in the second movement piano part. This is editor analysis, separate from direct preview observations. The Leo Smit Foundation’s current public video identifies Chi-Chun Chen, saxophone, and Vicky Juo, piano, at the Forbidden Music Regained festival in the Conservatorium of Amsterdam on 30 November 2024. Video duration 14:10 includes an introduction; performance begins 2:34.

Score observations: Visually inspected the 1934 facsimile opening and modern Donemus printed score pages 1,4,7 and9 within the first-movement preview. The Donemus preface explicitly says Score in C, so no written alto range is inferred. The transposed individual saxophone part was not inspected. Opening I. Allegretto is quarter note 92 in 2/4, beginning p. Chromatic accidentals, slurred short-note groups, rests within gestures, ties and crescendos alternate sustained solo tones with quicker figures. The two piano lines move largely independently in persistent sixteenth-note patterns. Registers and clefs change, so coordination and balancing three independent lines are concrete ensemble demands. Printed pages 6–7 introduce Vivo, poco più mosso and changes among 2/4, 3/4 and 3/8. The solo mixes held notes and slurred chromatic figures above piano movement. The preview does not reach the end of movement I or show II and III.

Collection context: Original historical saxophone chamber work, with modern institutional revival and a conservatory festival recording. No young-player simplification or graded teaching intent found.

Open questions: Preview remains in movement I; complete written alto range and later-movement notation remain uninspected.

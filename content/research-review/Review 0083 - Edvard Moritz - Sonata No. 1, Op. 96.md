## Research

Catalog: [[Edvard Moritz - Sonata No. 1, Op. 96]]

- Identity: Edvard Moritz’s *Sonata No. 1, Op. 96* is a 1938 work for E-flat alto saxophone and piano published by Southern Music and dedicated to Cecil Leeson. Historic discography and reviews document Leeson’s recording with pianist Josef Wagner; a contemporaneous review characterized it as melodic and not written to exploit the saxophone’s spectacular effects.
- Southern Music identifies Op. 96 for alto saxophone and piano, four movements, a 27-minute duration, and an advancing-player descriptor. [Southern Music Company catalog](https://keiserproductions.com/catalog/southern-music/?ic=&sc=&tc=&tid=88C526BC-7F74-4338-AF3B-E3397778AED5) (opened).
- Music history event page identifies the 1939 sonata for E-flat alto saxophone and piano among works written for Cecil Leeson. [musica reanimata program/review](https://musica-reanimata.de/en/0050.review.html) (opened).
- Contemporary review identifies Decca album 187 and a Leeson-Wagner performance, and describes the work as melodic while noting it does not exploit the saxophone spectacularly. [The New Yorker, May 3, 1941](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1941/05/03/concert-records) (opened).
- Purdue digital collection result lists the 1938 work in a saxophone sonata discography and cites a recording of Leeson. [Purdue digital collection, saxophone discography](https://cdm16850.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/MusCnrtEvnt/id/4079/download) (search_index_only).

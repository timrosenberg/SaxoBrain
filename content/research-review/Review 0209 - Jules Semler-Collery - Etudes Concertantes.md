## Chat batch 12 follow-up, 2026-10-14

The exact ARTS² library record identifies ten studies for E-flat alto saxophone and piano ad libitum, Max Eschig ME7464, two parts of 23 and 43 pages, and copyright 1964. It credits Marcel Mule with a prefatory role. The composer remains Semler-Collery. Groth provides all ten headings: Andantino; Allegretto ben staccato; Adagio con expressione; Allegro comodo; Mouvement d’Habanera; Mouvement de Saltarelle; Alla Polacca; Rigaudon Risoluto; Andantino con expressivo; Allegro brillante. The named styles and tempo contrasts are verified, although the notation is unavailable. The inspected cover places the collection in the repertoire of the Paris Conservatoire saxophone class and specifies optional piano. This establishes institutional teaching identity. It does not establish beginner or youth-specific purpose. The Karol Lipiński Academy of Music in Wrocław includes Études Concertantes among études for its two-year second-cycle saxophone study program. The exact curriculum page was visually inspected. This is documented graduate-study use, rather than an assumed placement from the title. Sibley Music Library’s Karel Husa archive lists a commercial Buffet-Crampon LP, B.C.B. 102, with François Daneels, saxophone, and Claudine Capelle, piano, performing Études Concertantes on side 1. The archive does not specify which studies are recorded. The old review’s Paris Conservatoire 1931 contest designation belongs to the immediately preceding Busser Asturias library item. The isolated record for Études concertantes contains no such designation. The 2:40 time in the Antwerp bibliography is also not adopted as a whole-collection duration.

Collection context: Collection of ten separately named studies. Piano accompaniment is optional.

Open questions: No notation inspected. Hal Leonard Closer Look returned no available preview, and Groth supplies a cover only. Study-specific range, rhythm and articulation therefore remain unknown. The 10 ans avec le saxophone PDF returned a blocked-page response, so its technical description is retained as a search-index lead only. No exact playable recording located after two bounded YouTube searches. The library’s LP entry does not establish whether all ten studies were recorded. Original composition year remains unknown. Library copyright 1964 and distributor-era publication 2001 must not be entered as composition years. No youth-specific simplification evidence was located. Optional piano describes performance format rather than a simplified version.

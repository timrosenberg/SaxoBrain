## Research

Catalog: [[Manolis Kalomiris - Chant Grec]]

- Identity: Manolis Kalomiris’s Chant Grec is a 1929 vocal-and-piano work later arranged for saxophone and piano. The Leduc AL29479 edition is for alto or soprano saxophone and piano; the Greek Saxophone Library describes a single four-minute movement and lists early college years among its educational stages.
- Greek Saxophone Library gives the 1929 date, four-minute duration, single movement, alto/soprano and piano instrumentation, Leduc edition data and pedagogical descriptors. [Greek Saxophone Library](https://hellenicsax.gr/works/632_chant_grec) (search_index_only).
- Leduc catalog lists AL29479 as alto or soprano saxophone and piano. [Alphonse Leduc catalog](https://casamusicaledelrio.it/casamusicale/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/CAT-18-107-Leduc-Catalogue-2018-ss-GS-v6-NO-CROPS-LO-RESv2.pdf) (search_index_only).
- A Nakas Conservatory recital archive lists the work in a soprano saxophone and piano performance. [Nakas Conservatory](https://www.nakas.edu.gr/en/events-archive/concerts-archive/educational-year-2025-2026/echoes-of-romance-and-myth-duo-duende-dimitrios-melas-soprano-sax-marios-kazas-piano/) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Adaptation from voice and piano; Leduc edition supports both alto and soprano saxophone.

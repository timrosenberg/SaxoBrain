## Chat batch 07 follow-up, 2026-10-13

The exact Groth listing confirms Clair Leonard, alto saxophone and piano, Bourne 108319. The edition cover places the work in the Sigurd Rascher Series of Instrumental Solos and labels it a fantasy dedicated to Rascher. The Vassar College finding aid lists a Recitative and Abracadabra folder dated 1941 with Leonard–Rascher correspondence. The guide supplies archival context, not musical pages. A University of North Texas history describes Rascher performing the work with the Texas Tech band in February 1955. Florida State University catalogs the work in a saxophone/piano faculty recital of 7 July 1980.

Score observations: Only the edition cover was visually inspected. No actual musical notation was located. The word fantasy on the cover is edition metadata; specific recitative or fast-section demands cannot be inferred from the title.

Collection context: Historical concert saxophone repertoire with a Rascher dedication and documented university performance. The Bourne piano edition is substantive recital repertoire; no simplified youth-arrangement claim is supported.

Open questions: The 1941 archival folder date is not definitive proof of composition year. A 2026 festival program assigns 1947, so the blank composition field should remain until a dated score or reliable work history resolves this. Band performance is documented but orchestration authorship and relation to the piano edition remain unknown. No musical score, exact saxophone range, duration, technical profile or public playable recording was verified.

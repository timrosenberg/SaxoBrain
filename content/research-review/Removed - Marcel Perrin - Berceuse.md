### Chat Batch 14 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

Leduc AL20803 is alto saxophone and piano. Québec includes Berceuse at general level 3 among simple lyrical works for tone study, and again at 4/5. MSHSAA catalogs it as Class C. The Philharmonie guide gives 1950, Andantino 2/4 with a faster section, simple binary rhythms and some triplets. An Adolphesax performance page credits saxophonist Efrem Roca and Leduc score.

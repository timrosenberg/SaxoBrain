### Research Review, October 11, 2026

[[Review 0329 - Robert Planel - Conte de Noel|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Robert Planel - Conte de Noel]].

Robert Planel's Suite Romantique no. 5, Conte de Noël, is an alto saxophone and piano piece, Leduc AL20250.
[CDMC / WindMusic library catalog](https://windmusic.org/index.php?lvl=notice_display&id=11435) (opened): An opened library catalog identifies the six-part Suite romantique for alto saxophone and piano, published by Leduc in 1944.
[Sheet Music Plus listing](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/category/artists/r/robert-planel/?prefn1=instruments&prefv1=Alto+Saxophone) (search_index_only): The retailer search result identifies no. 5 as Conte de Noel for alto saxophone and piano.
[Philharmonie de Paris, Dix ans avec le saxophone](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p. 44 (printed p. 47)): The Philharmonie de Paris ten-year saxophone guide lists no. 5, Conte de Noël, as moderate and calm in 6/8, with three sharps; listed duration 3:20. This is a guide description, not direct score inspection.

Collection/transcription context: One of Planel's original six saxophone/piano pieces; intended level is unresolved.

Review focus: The guide reports an animated 2/4 pulse at quarter note = 126. Inspect the score for articulation and rhythmic execution; no notation was inspected.

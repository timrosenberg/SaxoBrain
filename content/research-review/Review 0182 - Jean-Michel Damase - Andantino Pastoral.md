## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

The actual Leduc cover names Jean-Michel Damase, specifies alto saxophone and piano, gives AL 29594 and explicitly prints 1er cycle. This is direct evidence of a teaching-oriented edition. The publisher’s 2018 catalogue separately lists Andantino AL 29507 and Andantino Pastoral AL 29594. Plain Andantino recordings or previews must not be treated as this work. The Centre de Documentation Musicale d’Alsace exact record 5926 verifies AL 29594, alto/piano, three-minute duration, four score pages plus separate parts and 2004 publication. Its local repertoire label is Année 4, with no equivalence established to college study. Broekmans & Van Poppel’s Prima Vista 2007 catalogue lists product 167532 as Andantino pastoral for E-flat alto with accompaniment, 1er cycle, alongside separate product 167531 Andantino. The Tarragona conservatory’s 2025-2026 GP 1 saxophone teaching plan lists Andantino Pastoral by J. M. Damase in its saxophone-and-piano repertoire bibliography. This independently documents conservatory teaching use. No source inspected names an arranger or establishes a prior work from which this edition was simplified. No professional or college performance use was verified. Publication 2004 was not used as a composition year.

Collection context: Standalone teaching edition explicitly marked 1er cycle, independently listed in conservatory teaching repertoire. No documented transcription or simplification of an earlier work.

Open questions: No notation was accessible: three observed LMI images are cover variants, Hal Leonard Closer Look is unavailable and the Sheet Music Plus browser gallery has only the cover. Actual tempo, meter, articulation, saxophone range, accompaniment texture and technique remain unknown. Exact composer/title YouTube search returned unrelated material. No exact saxophone recording was verified, so streaming remains blank. Composition year remains unverified. 2004 is explicitly a publication year in the exact library record. Retailer feeds report 96 pages and sometimes formatCD, while the exact library record describes four pages plus parts and the actual cover describes sheet music.

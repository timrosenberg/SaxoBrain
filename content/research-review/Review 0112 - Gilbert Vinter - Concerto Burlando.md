The opened CDMC/Wind Music record identifies a composer-made piano reduction, 31 pages plus a separate part, published by Studio Music in 2005, ISMN M-050-07822-7. Its original-work keyword supports concerto identity. Emerson’s opened exact listing confirms alto saxophone/piano and publisher reference M050078227, matching the institutional ISMN. Emerson displays a conflicting separate ISMN field, M050011750. Existing Groth lead gives M050078579. These numbers are unresolved edition/catalogue conflicts and should not be silently unified. No SaxoBrain level is assigned. Search-index National Band Council of Australia results list the work as the 2009 test piece performed by alto saxophone winner Tom Fox. The underlying official PDF was blocked, so this use remains an indexed lead.

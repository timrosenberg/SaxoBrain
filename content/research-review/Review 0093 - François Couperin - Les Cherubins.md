- Identity: François Couperin’s *Les Chérubins ou l’aimable Lazure* is a harpsichord piece from the twentieth ordre in Book IV of *Pièces de clavecin*. The saxophone publication is a Marcel Perrin arrangement in Simrock EE2987; that 14-page combined volume also contains Fritz Kreisler’s *La Précieuse*, which is a separate work.
- Presto listing identifies EE2987, saxophone and piano/organ scoring, editor Marcel Perrin, and the combined Couperin/Kreisler contents. [Presto Music sheet-music catalog](https://www.prestomusic.com/sheet-music/products/7250948--couperin-f-les-cherubins-and-la-precieuse) (opened).
- Piano Encyclopedia catalog identifies the source as Couperin’s harpsichord work in Book IV, Ordre 20 in G major. [Piano Encyclopedia](https://enc.piano.or.jp/en/musics/24360) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Transcription of Couperin’s harpsichord music; the current combined edition pairs it with Kreisler’s separate *La Précieuse*.
- Review focus: Inference: consult the Perrin arrangement to establish its key, ornament realization, and whether keyboard reduction preserves the source texture; no saxophone score was inspected.

## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Opened Boosey/Schott 2007–08 publisher catalog confirms EE2987, Perrin, alto saxophone with piano/organ and ISMN 9790221109057. Opened Presto exact EE2987 listing identifies Simrock, Marcel Perrin, 14 pages and the two distinct contents: Couperin’s Book IV Ordre 20 Les Chérubins and Kreisler’s La Précieuse. The inspected edition cover says alto saxophone and piano/organ, Marcel Perrin, Elite Edition 2987 and D. Rahter, Hamburg/London. This historical imprint is preserved as cover evidence; current catalog publisher remains Simrock. IMSLP’s opened original-work record identifies Book IV as published 1730 and Les Chérubins as Ordre XX No. 3. The existing private research claim that it comes from Book II 1717 conflicts with these exact-work catalog records. 1730 is publication, not a verified composition year. The performers Hans-Dieter Karras and Holger Lustermann list the exact Perrin arrangement for alto saxophone and organ in their current program III. This establishes professional performance use and the organ option. Their source-book/date line repeats Book II 1717 and is contradicted by the original-work record; it is not used for chronology.

Collection context: Documented harpsichord transcription, with professional alto/organ performance use. No explicit young-player simplification found. Combined edition’s Kreisler companion must not be attributed to François Couperin.

Open questions: No readable exact Perrin saxophone notation found. The available exact-edition images were covers, so written range, ornament realization, articulation and phrase length remain unknown. No matching accessible playable exact saxophone recording verified. Performer repertoire listing does not supply a work-specific player. Original composition date unknown; Book IV 1730 is publication. Existing Book II 1717 research should be flagged as a provenance conflict.

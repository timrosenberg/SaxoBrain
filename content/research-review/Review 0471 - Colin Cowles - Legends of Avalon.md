## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Remove from the catalog. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Source description. **Scoring:** Alto or tenor saxophone and piano; edition alternatives. **Duration:** Whole-suite duration unverified.

- Presto describes five movements depicting Arthurian scenes, with the composer’s foreword and performance notes.
- London College of Music lists movements 1, 2 and 5 as a selection, so that listing does not establish requirements for the complete suite.
- National College of Music lists King Arthur and Sir Lancelot separately. No movement’s register, rhythms or articulation have been inspected.

**No notation inspected for this card.** Source descriptions and recording identification are not a direct score analysis. Choose Hold if this does not support a level decision.

**Inspected scope and limits:** No notation inspected. Five-movement identity is confirmed. Movement-specific decisions require the actual parts. The complete movement order and duration remain unverified.

**Edition or source note:** Edition says alto or tenor. LCM also permits soprano in its examination context; this is not evidence of saxophone doubling.

## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Publisher directly confirms Harry Gee, SS501, alto saxophone, twelve pages and its Level 4 label. Groth’s exact linked record confirms piano accompaniment. The Royal Conservatory’s 2013 saxophone syllabus places Harry Gee’s version in Grade 7 alto List A, Mainly Transcriptions, and describes it as from a Sonata in D Major. This is direct evidence of a transcription and graded teaching use. Young Musicians Festival’s own saxophone guideline lists the exact Harry Gee alto/piano arrangement in its Level 5 repertoire. Its levels belong to that festival’s scale, with no equivalence to publisher Level 4 or RCM Grade 7 assumed. The catalog version is a transcription, as classified by RCM, rather than an original saxophone composition. Precise source-sonata number and original key are unresolved: RCM says D Major, while a licensed baritone edition’s description says third movement of a D Minor sonata. The baritone description cannot settle the alto arrangement’s provenance. Timucua’s public performance video, 3:43 including presentation, names Scott Devlin and Christine Griffin in a teacher/student classical saxophone recital. A frame at about 0:37 visually confirms alto saxophone and piano. The Harry Gee edition is not named. Phoenix Music Academy’s upload names Lawrence Qian performing the work in Vancouver Kiwanis Music Festival’s 2021 Grade 7 repertoire class. Its title/description verifies student festival context, but the arrangement and instrument were not independently confirmed from that upload.

Collection context: Saxophone transcription with exact Gee edition documented in two educational syllabi. No verified simplified version or edition-specific college curriculum placement found.

Open questions: No actual SS501 notation inspected; written range, rhythms, articulation and piano texture remain unknown. Source-sonata number, source-key conflict and composition year unresolved. Recording’s arranger/edition is unidentified. No exact alto/Gee college or professional curriculum placement verified. Baritone PMEA audition use is a different edition and not transferred.

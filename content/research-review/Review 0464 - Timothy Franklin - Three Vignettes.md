# Review 0464: Timothy Franklin - Three Vignettes

[[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 02|Score batch 02]] · Catalog work (removed)

**Batch decision number: 21.** Decision applied October 20, 2026.

## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Remove from the catalog. Approved and applied October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Notation. **Scoring:** Tenor saxophone and piano. **Duration:** About 8:20 (publisher).

- Vignette 1 opens in 4/4, Wistfully quarter = 68; the tenor enters p legato after four bars of rest with mostly sustained notes and short slurred figures.
- Vignette 2 opens in 3/4, Pensively quarter = 54; the five-flat tenor key signature and long tied/slurred phrases require pitch security and sustained phrasing over repeated piano chords.
- Vignette 3 opens in 3/4, Boating song quarter = 72; the tenor line is more active, with long slurs, dotted rhythms, sixteenths and grace notes above sustained piano harmony.

**Inspected scope and limits:** All three pages of the publisher sample visually inspected: printed p.1 Vignette 1 opening, p.4 Vignette 2 opening and p.8 Vignette 3 opening. This broadens the previous review beyond Vignette 2. These are opening excerpts only; endings, whole-piece range and later technical material remain unverified.

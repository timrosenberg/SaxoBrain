## Chat batch 06 follow-up, 2026-10-12

Philharmonie de Paris’s repertoire guide identifies Choudens AC20883 and four contrasting movements: improvisatory, freely incantatory, lunar, and static/free. Its nearby 15-minute marking belongs to Music for Stonehenge, not Espaces irradiés. https://catalogue.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf Saxophonist-researcher Bernardo Zagalaz Lijarcio’s 2015 University of Córdoba thesis dates the composition 1978 and gives approximately 24:30. His score analysis identifies unmetered/free passages alongside changing meters, quarter tones without supplied fingerings, flutter tonguing, small glissandi, multiphonics with fingerings, key sounds, tremolos, and graphic rhythm. These are the researcher’s observations, not an independently inspected score. https://helvia.uco.es/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10396/12998/2015000001186.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1 The thesis explains proportional duration lines: the performer interprets approximate note lengths according to the drawn line lengths. This is a concrete rhythmic-reading demand beyond conventional meter. https://helvia.uco.es/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10396/12998/2015000001186.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1 Antwerp Conservatory’s score-holding catalog identifies the 1980 Choudens publication, score and part, handwritten facsimile, and dedication to Serge Bichon.

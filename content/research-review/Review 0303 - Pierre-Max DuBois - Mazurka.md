Catalog: [[Pierre-Max DuBois - Mazurka]]

- Identity: Pierre-Max Dubois's Mazurka, Hommage à Chopin, is an alto saxophone and piano work published by Leduc as AL23010. The Philharmonie de Paris's 10 ans avec le saxophone guide gives 3:10, dates it 1961, describes a Lento followed by Allegro vivo, and lists it as intended for seventh year in that guide's conservatory sequence.

Catalog entry: [[Jean-Michel Damase - Vacances]].

Jean-Michel Damase, Vacances, Billaudot GB4949 (1990), for E-flat alto saxophone and piano, approximately three minutes. The publisher lists “Facile (cycle 1)” and adults as audience; the French Wind Music Archive describes a preparatory level indicated on the score. A University of Panama recital project reproduces selected excerpts attributed to this edition and discusses it in an undergraduate recital context.
[Éditions Billaudot](https://www.billaudot.com/vacances-8.html) (opened): Publisher product page identifies GB4949 as E-flat saxophone and piano, c. 3 minutes, copyright 1990, “Facile (cycle 1),” and adult audience.
[French Wind Music Archive catalog](https://windmusic.org/index.php?id=7452&lvl=notice_display) (opened): French Wind Music Archive catalog identifies the 1990 Billaudot edition for alto saxophone in E-flat and piano, 3+1 pages, with “niveau préparatoire indiqué sur la partition”; this is the archive’s bibliographic description and does not establish the complete part’s demands.
[University of Panama recital project with score excerpts](https://up-rid.up.ac.pa/6333/1/karen_santiago.pdf) (score_inspected, printed pp. 7, 9–10; PDF pp. 11, 13–14): A University of Panama undergraduate recital-project PDF reproduces score excerpts attributed to the 1990 Gérard Billaudot edition, and I visually inspected its opening, syncopation, high-register, and closing excerpts (printed pp. 7, 9–10). These are excerpts, not the complete score.

Score observations:
- The university project’s reproduced opening excerpt shows 12/8 and Moderato with dotted quarter = 76; the saxophone rests through several opening beats before a slurred melodic figure. Visually inspected later excerpts show syncopation, a high-register phrase with leaps and a rapid connected descent, and an active closing figure with short rests. These are selected excerpts copied from the 1990 Billaudot edition, not a full score; exact written range was not verified.

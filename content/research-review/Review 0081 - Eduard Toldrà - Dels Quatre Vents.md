## Research

Catalog: [[Eduard Toldrà - Dels Quatre Vents]]

- Identity: The saxophone publication *Dels Quatre Vents* (UME21470) is an alto saxophone and piano adaptation of the fifth movement in Eduard Toldrà’s *Sis Sonets*, originally written for violin and piano in 1921. A library catalog search result attributes a transcription under this title to José Amaz; exact relation of this edition to other transcriptions needs confirmation.
- UME21470 is listed for alto saxophone and piano. [Presto Music sheet-music catalog](https://www.prestomusic.com/sheet-music/products/7007577--dels-quatre-vents) (search_index_only).
- Toldrà specialist catalog identifies the source cycle as six violin-and-piano sonnets and says there are adaptations for saxophone among other instruments. [Eduard Toldrà specialist catalog](https://sites.google.com/site/eduardtoldra/eduard-toldr%C3%A0-i-soler/complete-catalog/sis-sonets) (search_index_only).
- Spanish performing-arts library record attributes a transcription of Dels quatre vents to José Amaz and describes the source as a printed violin-and-piano sonnet. It lists a different UME plate number, so its relationship to UME21470 is unresolved. [CDAEM performing arts library](https://bibliotecacdt.mcu.es/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=61204) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Documented transcription of an original violin-and-piano movement. The exact arranger for UME21470 remains uncertain.
- Review focus: Inference: confirm whether the saxophone part preserves violin-specific phrasing and range in the published sax transcription; no saxophone score was inspected.
- Gaps: No readable saxophone score or saxophone recording found; musical details and exact adaptation cannot be assessed.; UME21470 and the institutional catalog’s UME21466 record appear to be different publication records; verify edition relationship.

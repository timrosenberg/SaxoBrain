## Chat Batch 17 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

**Proposed decision:** First Year. **Confidence:** high. **Approval:** Recorded 2026-10-15.

**Reason:** Inference is supported unusually directly: Wei-Lun Chien’s analytical dissertation describes these duos as suitable for intermediate students or college freshmen. Inspected excerpts show quadruplet subdivisions within compound meter and unbarred two-part suspensions. These are valuable college musicianship demands without requiring a virtuoso level.

Twenty-four original duos, composed 1942, also conceived as lessons for soprano/contralto voices or soprano/alto saxophones. Billaudot GB 3715/GB 3716 offer same-key or mixed B-flat/E-flat saxophone combinations. The modern edition is associated with Georges Gourdet. Publisher lists difficult, cycle 3; copyright 1984 is publication evidence. Chien reports optional lower-octave alternatives for some high notes in the two-alto version.

**Score observations:** Two reproduced excerpts inspected, rather than full collection. Duo 1, bars 7-9: 12/8 with four-note subdivisions against compound pulse and chromatic voice-leading. Duo 9: no barlines or meter signature; sustained suspensions require steady shared pulse, balance and intonation.

**Collection context:** Original substantial duet collection, expressly retained by Tim. Keep complete collection in one record.

**Remaining gaps:** Complete score not inspected. Label dates duos 1946, conflicting with 1942 in analytical dissertation and existing catalog; retain 1942 pending manuscript confirmation.

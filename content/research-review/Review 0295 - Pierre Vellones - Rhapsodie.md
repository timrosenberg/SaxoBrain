**Identity and findings.** Pierre Vellones’s Rapsodie Op.92 was originally scored for alto saxophone, harp, celesta and percussion. Marcel Mule made a reduction for alto saxophone and piano, published by Henry Lemoine in 1947. The BnF catalog indexes an ensemble recording at 5:02, but the performance version and exact relation to piano reduction require checking.

**Sources checked.**
- [CiNii Books](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA89757841) (opened): Identifies original ensemble scoring and Marcel Mule alto sax/piano reduction, Lemoine 1947, 8-page score and part.
- [Bibliothèque nationale de France audio catalog](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb384232910) (opened): Indexes Rapsodie Op.92 recording at 5:02 and original sax/harp/celesta/percussion instrumentation.

**Tim’s decision:** Advanced. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Recording + sources. **Scoring:** Unaccompanied saxophone; alto in Philharmonie/Córdoba sources, soprano in catalog and conflicting scholarly listings. **Duration:** Approximately 15 minutes, Philharmonie guide p.101.

- Philharmonie guide: circular breathing; playing while singing or growling; double and triple articulation.
- Tom Bergeron’s specialist multiphonics bibliography identifies numerous specified multiphonic fingerings.

**No notation inspected for this card.** Source descriptions and recording identification are not a direct score analysis. Choose Hold if this does not support a level decision.

**Inspected scope and limits:** No original notation inspected. Attributed technique evidence is concrete, but full register, pacing, fingering practicality and exact scoring need the original part. Leduc AL27003 is verified in the Philharmonie guide; no current publisher product page or authorized score preview was located.

**Edition or source note:** Catalog says soprano. Philharmonie guide p.101 and Córdoba conservatory curriculum say alto. A University of Córdoba notation-study table p.147 instead says soprano. No title page was located to settle this conflict. Do not automatically change saxophone type. The bibliography distinguishes 1982 composition from 1984 publication.

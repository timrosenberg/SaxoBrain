# Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 03

[[Remaining Repertoire - Decision Queue|Decision queue]] · [[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 02|Completed batch 02]]

Completed October 20, 2026: 15 levels assigned, eight works removed and two retained with difficulty unknown. All 25 decisions are recorded. Original batch numbers and evidence remain below.

**Evidence coverage:** Actual notation was inspected for 4 works. Recording, album or discography reference links are provided for 12. These groups overlap. Cards without inspected notation explicitly say so. No audio audition is claimed. Duration, sample scope and edition conflicts are stated where verified; an excerpt does not establish whole-work difficulty.

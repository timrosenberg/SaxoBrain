Groth confirms Combre C05990, alto saxophone and piano, and dedication to Jean Ledieu. Royal Conservatoire Antwerp’s score catalog describes a 1999 Combre score and part in Arc en ciel. The Confédération Musicale de France’s annals list Partita C05990 as a saxophone E-flat selection in 2000 under end-of-second-cycle examinations. This is an institutional use record, not Tim’s level.

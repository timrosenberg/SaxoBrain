## Chat batch 08 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Publisher composition year is 1992. Its ca. 15-minute duration differs from the 9:57 public Kelly recording and Hellenic Library’s 10-minute description. Hellenic Library lists altissimo, microtones, dynamic contrasts, mixed rhythmic values, legato and vibrato; this is attributed technical guidance beyond the short preview. Publisher program note relates the work to Terzakis’s childhood memories of the 1941–1944 German occupation of Greece.

Score observations: The incipit explicitly labels the staff Altsaxophon (Es), so it is a transposed alto saxophone staff. It shows 4/4, quarter note = 60, two bars of saxophone rest, then a slurred entry with a triplet and ornament over a repeated quiet piano bass. Only the opening system was inspected. It cannot establish the complete written range or the later extended techniques.

Collection context: Professional original alto and piano work dedicated to Kelly. Specialist educational description documents extended-technique study; no simplification identified.

Open questions: Catalog title omits I; publisher and opening score show Der Hölle Nachklang I. Flag title correction for Tim. Complete repository part remains blocked. Opening sample cannot establish complete written range. Publisher ca 15 duration and recording 9:55 differ; preserve both with attribution. Hellenic header 1991 differs from publisher/score 1992.

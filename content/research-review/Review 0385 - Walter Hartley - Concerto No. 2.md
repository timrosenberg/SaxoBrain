Catalog: [[Walter Hartley - Concerto No. 2]]

- Identity: Walter S. Hartley’s Concerto No. 2 is a 1989 alto saxophone concerto. The Hartley estate catalog gives a small orchestra or piano option, 10:30 duration, and Dorn publication, catalog no. 173. Dorn’s 2025 catalog separately lists Concerto #2 for alto saxophone and small orchestra as DP431, score and parts. A 1989 Saxophone Journal excerpt is bibliographically listed by an UPV dissertation.
- Hartley estate catalog lists Concerto No. 2 (1989), alto saxophone, small orchestra or piano, 10:30, Dorn, no. 173. [Walter S. Hartley estate, saxophone works catalog](https://www.walterhartley.com/Saxophone_2.htm) (opened).
- Dorn Publications 2025 catalog lists Concerto No. 2 as alto saxophone and small orchestra, score and parts, item DP431 (printed p. 24). Its listing does not specify a piano part. [Dorn Publications catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/images/DP%20Catalog%20April%202025.pdf) (printed 24; search_index_only).
- The UPV dissertation bibliography cites a Saxophone Journal excerpt from Concerto No. 2 for Alto Saxophone by Walter Hartley, vol. 14, no. 2, September/October 1989 (printed p. 783). The excerpt itself was not accessed. [Universitat Politècnica de València dissertation bibliography](https://riunet.upv.es/bitstream/handle/10251/4337/tesisUPV2925.pdf) (printed 783; opened).

## Chat batch 06 follow-up, 2026-10-12

The composer’s own work commentary documents a 26 January 1979 premiere by Iwan Roth and Zürcher Camerata under Räto Tschupp. The original ensemble combines divided strings, two percussion groups and a predominantly rhythmic piano part. https://www.arminschibler.ch/multimedia/DasWerk1986.pdf Schibler describes a classically played saxophone line against jazz-derived ternary rhythms in piano and percussion. An introduction leads into a fast section, a three-part slow middle with a furious Agitato, and a fast finale over piano ostinatos. Material develops from the opening motive. These are composer statements, not inspected-score observations. https://www.arminschibler.ch/multimedia/DasWerk1986.pdf Antwerp’s institutional score record explicitly identifies composition as 1977–78 and duration 16:30. The composer catalogue gives 19 minutes, so duration is source-dependent. https://www.libraryconservatoryantwerp.be/lijsten/BSAX.pdf The live dealer record identifies Kunzelmann GM902 for alto saxophone and piano. This clarifies that the catalog’s piano scoring is a concerto reduction. https://www.bodensee-musikversand.de/product_info.php?products_id=234921

Collection context: Original saxophone concerto written for Iwan Roth and professionally premiered. No youth transcription concern documented.

Open questions: No readable score or solo-part preview located. No playable exact-work saxophone recording verified; composer archive lists ELSC06 and Jecklin568, and SRG neo composer page did not expose this work. Duration conflicts: composer catalogue 19 minutes; Antwerp score record 16:30. Composition spans 1977–78; scalar year metadata requires a convention rather than pretending a single date is established.

Catalog: [Clare Loveday - Wind-Play.md](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Clare Loveday - Wind; Play.md). Full evidence log: [research record](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/research-review-2026-10-11/lane-3/records/0053.json).

**Identity and findings.** The composer’s current compositions page identifies Wind-Play (2000) for soprano saxophone and piano, 3:30, premiered 22 August 2001; the composer also rewrote it for alto saxophone. SAMRO’s work archive dates its entry 2001 and describes a five-pulse piano opening motif and a saxophone line derived from suspended piano harmonies; the score upload is still being processed. An independent researcher’s catalogue gives a different duration (2:40), left unresolved.

**Sources checked.**
- [SAMRO Scores work archive](https://www.samroscores.org.za/work/wind-play/) (opened): SAMRO archive dates Wind-Play 2001; identifies it as written for a young player and describes a five-pulse piano opening motif, with saxophone melody derived from suspended piano harmonies. The score is marked as still being processed, so notation is unavailable.
- [Karlien Gerber, An Annotated Bibliography of Selected Classical Saxophone Works by South African Composers](https://repository.up.ac.za/server/api/core/bitstreams/c85ea27d-b97a-4b47-bfa6-266368d61904/content) (search_index_only, p. 84): University of Pretoria bibliography also indexes soprano and alto scores and gives 3:30; the underlying scan is unavailable, so its own level III notation is not interpreted.
- [Noa Even, repertoire page](https://www.noaevenmusic.com/saxandpiano) (opened): Noa Even’s performer-maintained repertoire page lists Wind-Play (2000), 3:30, soprano or alto saxophone and piano, with an attached video link.
- [South African Saxophone Catalogue](https://southafricansaxophonecatalogue.com/loveday-clare) (opened): Independent South African Saxophone Catalogue lists Wind-play (2000), soprano saxophone and piano, 2:40, premiered 22 August 2001 by Adriaan Esterhuizen and Clare Loveday. Duration differs from other listings and remains unresolved.
- [Clare Loveday, compositions page](https://clareloveday.co.za/compositions.html) (opened): Current composer page identifies Wind-Play (2000) for soprano saxophone and piano, an alto rewrite, 3:30, and the 2001 premiere.

**Identity and findings.** Pierre-Max Dubois’s À l’espagnole is No.1 of Pièces caractéristiques en forme de suite, Op.77, for alto saxophone and piano. A 2000 Hungaroton album credits saxophonist Rigó Sándor and pianist Christina Leeb-Grill; its track listing gives 3:55, close to the catalog’s approximate 3:30. This is an edition/performance timing comparison, not a correction.

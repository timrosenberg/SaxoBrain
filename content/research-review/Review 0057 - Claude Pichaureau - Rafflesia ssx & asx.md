**Identity and findings.** The exact Billaudot GB5479 title is Rafflesia, du prélude à l’épilogue (2e/3e mouvements), associated in catalog metadata with B-flat and E-flat saxophone and piano. This does not match the catalog note’s soprano/alto duet description. A Philharmonie reference guide clarifies the related three-movement saxophone/orchestra work, but does not identify the saxophone/piano GB5479 edition.

**Sources checked.**
- [Éditions Billaudot catalog](https://www.billaudot.com/gb5479-6.html) (search_index_only): Official Billaudot catalog lists GB5479 as Rafflesia, du prélude à l’épilogue (2nd/3rd movements), under saxophone B-flat, and shows B-flat saxophone/piano and E-flat saxophone/piano classifications.
- [Hartman Music retailer](https://www.hartman.si/pichaureaurafflesia-du-prelude-a-lepilogue-saxop) (search_index_only): A retailer describes the GB5479 issue as soprano and alto saxophone with piano, but does not provide a readable score. This conflicts with a duet interpretation and needs edition confirmation.
- [Éditions Billaudot catalog](https://www.billaudot.com/gb4860-6.html) (search_index_only): Billaudot lists Prélude à rafflesia, GB4860, as a separate 1990 solo alto saxophone work of four pages. Keep it distinct from GB5479.
- [University of Córdoba repertoire guide](https://helvia.uco.es/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10396/12998/2015000001186.pdf?isAllowed=y) (search_index_only, p. 128): A university repository’s repertoire guide indexes Prélude à Rafflesia (1990) by Claude Pichaureau as solo alto saxophone, distinct from the cataloged GB5479 title.
- [Claude Delangle et al., 10 ans avec le saxophone (Philharmonie de Paris digital scan)](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p.86; printed p.91): The guide distinguishes solo alto Prélude, a soprano/orchestra second movement, and alto/orchestra Epilogue within the 15-minute work.

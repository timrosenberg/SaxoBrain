## Chat batch 08 follow-up, 2026-10-13

The Swedish Music and Theatre Library’s opened title notes distinguish composition 1985 from viola arrangement 1987. The edition reproduces the composer’s manuscript, with 22-page score and two parts. Groth confirms DORN0819, piano score and separate alto saxophone part, duration 12–13 minutes, and a separately offered viola edition. Mark Records’ own catalog gives three movements: Allegro moderato, Andante sostenuto and Molto vivace. The recording is Ronald L. Caravan and Sar-Shalom Strong in Single Reed Expressions, volume 4. The label-supplied first-movement YouTube track is verified playable and lasts 4:12. The full score was not inspected, so the fast/slow/fast movement sequence is catalog evidence rather than inferred notation. The previous Sacramento recital source concerns Monolog No. 4, as its fresh program extraction confirms. It does not document performance of this sonata and should not be retained as sonata evidence.

Collection context: Original saxophone and piano sonata with a documented alternative viola version. No simplified youth-edition context verified.

Open questions: Groth’s image is a cover dated 1985, not notation. No full score or saxophone part preview was inspected. Written range and specific technical demands remain unknown. Only the first movement of the recording was playback verified. The label confirms all three recorded movements, but no single full-work video or playlist was verified. No work-specific analytical performer note or thesis was located. The three-movement outline is the current useful musical evidence.

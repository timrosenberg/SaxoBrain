Catalog: [[Warren Gooch - Rhapsody]]

- Identity: Warren Gooch's Rhapsody is a single-movement work in five sections for alto saxophone and piano. College Music Society's 2006 Great Plains Chapter concert program gives a composer program note: Marcato opening, extended cadenza/dialogue, Cantabile, perpetual motion, and scherzo with a partial return. It states the work was written for Randall Smith and Patrice Ewoldt and premiered at the 2003 World Saxophone Congress.
- College Music Society Great Plains Chapter program gives the 2006 performance by Randall Smith and Marilyn Musick and reproduces a composer note with five-section outline, cadenza, dedication, and 2003 World Saxophone Congress premiere. Compare Makris Fantasy and Dance rather than grading solely from the World Congress premiere.

Dorn 1362 for alto saxophone and piano. Composer-attributed College Music Society program note states composed 2003 for Randall Smith and Patrice Ewoldt, who premiered it at the World Saxophone Congress in Minneapolis. One continuous work in five sections: Marcato, dialogue-like cadenza, Cantabile, perpetual motion and energetic scherzo with opening return. Score copyright 2004 is publication data.

**Score observations:** Marcato, quarter note = 120; opening shifts from 6/8 to 5/4, common time and other meters. Accented sustained notes alternate with fast grace-like chromatic figures and descending sextuplet passagework. Piano chord attacks and held textures require precise coordination with ornamented solo line.

**Collection context:** Original contemporary recital work with substantial internal contrasts.

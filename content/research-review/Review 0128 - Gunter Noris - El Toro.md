Its excerpt score labels the solo line Solo in C, so the visible notation is concert pitch rather than the transposed alto part. The excerpt confirms optional castanets and drum set. These are scored accompaniment options, not separate versions. The visible music is a pasodoble in 2/4 at quarter note = 120. Other editions include a distinct tenor edition EMR2306F and multiple non-saxophone and ensemble versions. Their audio and range should not be transferred to this alto edition.

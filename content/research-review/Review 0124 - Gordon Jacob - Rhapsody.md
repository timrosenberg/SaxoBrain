## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Jacob’s official catalog identifies cor anglais or E-flat saxophone with strings, dates the work 1948 and credits John Addison’s piano reduction. Its stated October completion follows its stated June premiere, so the exact chronology is internally inconsistent. The current Galaxy 1.5094 publisher record explicitly permits alto saxophone. Trevco specifies an alternate E-flat alto part on the reverse of the English-horn part. This resolves the prior claim that the catalog purchase might lack a saxophone part. Paul Brodie’s performance guide in Music Minus One describes expressive playing, strong accents tied across bar lines in Allegro vivace, clear sixteenth-note triplets beginning at measure 107, warm tone in Molto espressivo, and a floating pianissimo close. These are performer-authored observations read through a public text mirror, not our notation inspection. Hal Leonard confirms Music Minus One MMO4115 includes an annotated solo part and professional alto/piano recordings by Paul Brodie and Antonin Kubalek. Its public preview offers Benson and Whitney audio, not Jacob. Full audio access requires the purchased book/code.

Collection context: Published concert work with an authorized saxophone alternative and professional saxophone teaching recording. Brodie calls it a transcription of an English-horn solo; no youth simplification documented.

Open questions: Actual Jacob saxophone notation and full written range remain unverified. No public exact saxophone recording found; YouTube results were English horn and excluded. Official catalog’s June premiere precedes its October completion; exact chronology unresolved. Publisher gives nine minutes; the older MMO text reports 6:19 for its recording.

## Research notes (private)

### Chat Batch 17 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

Alphonse Leduc AL23637 is the alto saxophone and piano edition. The cover was visually inspected, but it contains no notation. The primary teaching guide 10 ans avec le saxophone lists a duration of about 5 minutes 35 seconds and a slow complaint followed by a fast dance. It recommends the end of seventh-year study. Apple’s catalog identifies a complete recording by Kyle Horch and pianist Yshani Perinpanayagam. Metadata was verified; audio was not auditioned. The existing 1966 value is retained. The guide prints 1966, but it does not explicitly distinguish composition from publication; the older copyright citation alone is insufficient to resolve that distinction.

[[Review 0406 - Yvan Markovitch - Complainte et Danse|Full review note]]

Catalog entry: [[Yvan Markovitch - Complainte et Danse]].

Yvan Markovitch’s Complainte et Danse is an alto saxophone and piano work, Leduc AL23637. A 2024 University of Music Münster festival program identifies saxophone and piano; NYPL’s older catalog record gives the composer variant “Ivan Markovitch” and a c.1966 Leduc imprint.
[Presto Music listing](https://www.prestomusic.com/sheet-music/products/7378136--ivan-markovitch-complainte-et-danse) (search_index_only): Presto listing identifies Yvan Markovitch, alto saxophone and piano, Leduc AL23637, six pages.
[University of Music Münster program](https://www.uni-muenster.de/imperia/md/content/musikhochschule/menschmusikfestival/_mmf2024_programmheft_web.pdf) (search_index_only): A 2024 university festival program lists the work as performed by saxophone and piano, spelling the composer Yvan.
[New York Public Library catalog](https://www.nypl.org/research/research-catalog/bib/b10233418) (search_index_only): NYPL catalog notes Paris: A. Leduc, c.1966 and gives composer as Ivan Markovitch.

Collection/transcription context: Recital-programmed work, performed in a university festival; no grade or difficulty level documented.

Open questions: No score pages were inspected. No matching saxophone recording was verified or auditioned. Composer-name spelling varies between Yvan and Ivan across the indexed program and library catalog sources; exact relationship of the older imprint to the current Leduc edition is unresolved.

## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Groth verifies the 27975 piano score and alto solo part, 22 pages. The physical cover names Jean Matitia, alto saxophone and piano, and Collection Claude Delangle. The publisher’s indexed 2024 catalogue describes 16 score pages plus separate part and niveau 3. Its 2003 copyright is not a verified composition year. The Fédération Française de l’Enseignement Artistique includes this exact Lemoine 27975 work in its 2022 Cycle III CEM pedagogical list. Musicalta’s 2011 professional program also lists Richard Ducros and Jonas Vitaud in the piece, documenting both educational and professional use. The exact Ducros/Teboul video description calls the work a suite after music composed for Julien Duvivier’s 1929 silent film. VCIS describes a saxophone/piano arrangement created for Arno Bornkamp, in Rudy Wiedoeft style, and reports altissimo G-sharp. VCIS incorrectly names Billaudot as publisher here, so its provenance and range claims require score or composer corroboration. The full-work-titled video lasts 10 minutes and names Richard Ducros, alto saxophone, Sophie Teboul, piano, live at Théâtre des Quatre Saisons in Gradignan. It is not labelled an excerpt.

Collection context: Film-music suite in saxophone/piano form, documented in French teaching repertoire and professional concert use. No simplified youth edition established.

Open questions: Only cover inspected; VCIS’s altissimo statement is a retailer report, not a verified notation observation. Publisher PDF blocked direct access; indexed catalogue evidence is explicitly qualified. Original composition date and detailed arrangement provenance remain unverified. Do not turn copyright 2003 into composed year.

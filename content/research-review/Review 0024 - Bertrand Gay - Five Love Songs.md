## Chat batch 06 follow-up, 2026-10-12

The Swiss National Library distinguishes EMR13406 alto/piano from EMR13407 tenor/piano. Both have a 31-page score and separate part and are catalogued with publication date2011. This resolves the earlier review’s uncertainty about EMR13406; no composition year is established. · https://ead.nb.admin.ch/web/sb-pdf/2012/sb201216.pdf The publisher preview supplies alto part and piano-score excerpts. The alto/piano edition is secure; EMR22994 is a separately listed play-along product in retailer indexes and should not replace EMR13406 automatically. · https://www.abel.at/shop/prme/177951.pdf

Score observations: Preview spread1, transposed alto part: Monday’s Song starts in9/8, dotted-quarter=63; accidentals, sixteenth-note figures and contrasting dynamics occur. It changes to4/4 at quarter=60 and then a Più mosso3/4 passage. Printed score7–10 shows sustained tones, short fast figures, expressive hairpins and substantial piano writing. Printed score15 has an ad-lib struck-percussion passage on the piano’s lower staff while saxophone rests. Only these excerpts were inspected. · https://www.abel.at/shop/prme/177951.pdf

Collection context: Multi-movement cycle issued for multiple wind instruments. Original instrumental origin and intended audience remain unresolved. The inspected saxophone notation has meter changes and active figurations, so no evidence supports describing it as a simple young-player melody transcription.

Open questions: Only partial EMR13406 preview inspected; complete range and all five songs remain unverified. No matching saxophone listening player verified; play-along audio is advertised for EMR22994, whose contents/performer require direct publisher check. Composition year not found.

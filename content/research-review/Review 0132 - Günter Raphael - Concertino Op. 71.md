## Chat batch 10 follow-up, 2026-10-14

The composer estate’s live work catalog identifies Concertino Op. 71 for alto saxophone and orchestra 1951, published by Breitkopf & Härtel. This directly establishes original composition year. The live Groth page for EB5986 explicitly says it includes a piano score reducing the orchestra and a saxophone part. This independently verifies the catalog’s accompaniment edition. Frank Lunte’s EDA label notes call the 1951 Concertino highly virtuosic and a milestone of solo-concerto literature. This is a named professional performer’s assessment, not a SaxoBrain level. The same notes say Emil Manz recorded the saxophone concerto for WDR with Wolfgang Sawallisch; this performance helped motivate Raphael’s later Sonata dedication. Exact public performer upload by Katarzyna Piotrowska runs approximately 15:23. Its description names her on alto saxophone and Marek Werpulewski on fortepian, the Polish term for piano. The video’s visible title card names all three movements: Allegro, Andante and Presto. The accompaniment is verified from performer metadata; a static title card was visible during inspection.

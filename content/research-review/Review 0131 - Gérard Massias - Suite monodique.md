Billaudot confirms MRB1068 as solo saxophone in Georges Gourdet’s L’enseignement moderne du saxophone collection, classified Difficult (cycle 3). The Royal Conservatory of Music syllabus places the complete Suite monodique in ARCT saxophone performance unaccompanied repertoire for alto saxophone. This supplies substantial diploma-level institutional repertoire context without assigning a SaxoBrain level. A complete public performer upload by Éric Maillet is verified. The video identifies a home practice recording and indexes five movements: Modéré, souple et libre; Vif, âpre et cursif; Lent, rêveur et chantant; Allègre, ironique et dansant; Animé, capricieux et rythmé. Duration is approximately 9:22. The performer’s movement starts are 0:00, 1:15, 2:00, 6:00, 7:44.

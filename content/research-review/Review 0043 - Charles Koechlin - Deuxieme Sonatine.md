## Research

Catalog entry: [[Charles Koechlin - Sonatine No. 2]].

Charles Koechlin, Deuxième Sonatine, Op. 194 no. 2 (composed 1942–43). IMSLP identifies a soprano saxophone-or-oboe d’amore solo with piano, and a separate ensemble version. The opened score scan is the ensemble version. A publisher catalogue lead describes an alto-or-tenor edition, so the assigned soprano instrument tag needs edition verification.
[Schott Music](https://www.schott-music.com/en/deuxieme-sonatine-no70838.html) (search_index_only): Schott/Max Eschig identifies the edition as op. 194/2 for oboe d’amore or soprano saxophone and piano, with intermediate label and 18 pages.
[Forsyth Music](https://www.forsyths.co.uk/sheet-music/instruments/woodwind/saxophone-sheet-music/228561-koechlin-charles-deuxieme-sonatine-opus-194-n-2-9790045037710.html) (search_index_only): Retailer identifies four movements, 34 pages for the broader score listing, and full original ensemble scoring.
[Conservatorio Superior de Música de Navarra syllabus](https://csmn.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/16-Guia-docente_Instrumento-aplicado-a-la-musicologia-II.-Saxofon-2024-2025.pdf) (search_index_only): A conservatory syllabus lists the soprano saxophone version among its repertoire options.
[Studio 52 album listing](https://www.studio52.gr/info_gr.asp?infoID=00000qyq) (search_index_only): Arno Bornkamp recording listing gives the four movement names and durations.
[IMSLP, 2 Sonatines, Op. 194](https://imslp.org/wiki/2_Sonatines,_Op.194_(Koechlin,_Charles)) (opened): IMSLP record identifies Op. 194, composed 1942–43, with a two-player oboe d’amore (or soprano saxophone) and piano version, and a distinct ensemble version. The accessed score PDF is the ensemble scoring, not the piano version.
[United Music Publishers Saxophone Catalogue 2010](https://johanvanderlinden.com/saxophone_catalogue_2010.pdf) (search_index_only): A separate saxophone-catalogue search result lists Sonatine No. 2, Op. 194 for alto or tenor saxophone and piano, conflicting with the soprano version in the score’s title page.

Score observations:
- The opened 28-page ensemble edition title page labels the solo instrument oboe d’amore or soprano saxophone. Opening line begins Andante, très calme, presque adagio at pp=, with a slurred chromatic line, very soft dynamic, and changing meters; p. 2 continues with chromatic motion and triplets over sustained ensemble parts. This is not the piano-reduction edition. ([score source](https://imslp.eu/files/imglnks/euimg/a/ab/IMSLP835740-PMLP1315266-Koechlin_2me_Sonatine_EnsFassg.pdf))

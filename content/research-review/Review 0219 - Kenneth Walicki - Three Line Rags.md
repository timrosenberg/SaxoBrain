## Chat batch 13 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Composer works list dates Three Line Rags 1984, 10 minutes, alto saxophone and piano. Composer downloadable edition’s performance notes explicitly say composed January 1983 in East Lansing, Michigan, written for Griffen Campbell. The discrepancy remains unresolved. Composer-hosted edition contains three rags, Loxodromus Rag, Rhumb Line Rag and Longitudinal Madness Rag. Its movement titles differ from the Dorn preview, whose first movement is Kredit Kard Krash Rag. NYPL institutional finding aid confirms a published Three line rags score by Ken Walicki in box 2111, reproduction of holograph manuscript.

Score observations: Groth’s sole product image is the first score page, labeled C Score. Saxophone pitches are concert pitch, not transposed alto notation. The first movement is headed Kredit Kard Krash Rag, with tempo 92 and 2/4 meter. Visible saxophone writing includes syncopated and tied figures, rests, accidentals and ledger-line notes. The piano part has chromatic, offbeat figures and repeated bass-to-chord motion. Only movement 1’s opening page was inspected. Composer PDF performance notes explicitly identify transposed alto saxophone notation and a slide-into-note symbol. This is a separate edition from the Dorn C-score preview. Composer PDF pages 7, 17 and 24 inspect each movement’s opening. Loxodromus Rag: 2/4, quarter note 92, syncopated chromatic figures, grace notes and piano octave motion. Rhumb Line Rag: 2/4, eighth note 76, dense slurred sixteenth figures and accidentals. Longitudinal Madness Rag: 4/4, half note 168, running eighths, long slurs and piano bass/chord alternation. Composer PDF page 33 shows the third-movement double-bar ending; page 34 is blank. Front matter, all movement openings and ending confirm a complete revised score; every interior page was not visually inspected.

Collection context: Multi-piece title; movement names and number of independent pieces remain unverified. No youth simplification or transcription documented.

Open questions: Composition year conflicts: composer works list 1984 versus explicit score note January 1983. No composed proposal. Dorn and composer PDF movement naming/edition relationship remains unexplained. No exact performance recording located; composer scores-and-recordings page provides this work a Score link only. Full written range and every interior page not inspected.

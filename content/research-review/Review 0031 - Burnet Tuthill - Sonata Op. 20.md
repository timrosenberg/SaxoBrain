## Research

Catalog entry: [[Burnet Tuthill - Sonata Op. 20]].

Burnet Tuthill, Sonata for alto saxophone and piano, Op. 20, Southern Music edition SS497. ABRSM’s current ARSM diploma list includes the complete sonata. An archival Leeson manuscript description identifies 1939 and three movements, but that scan was not inspectable here; the catalog’s 1941 date therefore remains unresolved.
[Stanton’s Sheet Music](https://www.stantons.com/sheet-music/title/sonata-op-20/03774129/) (search_index_only): Southern Music listing identifies the alto saxophone edition, publisher and catalog identifier; retailer describes lyrical and technical writing.
[ABRSM diploma syllabus copy](https://sites.create-cdn.net/sitefiles/37/2/9/372980/ABRSM_Diploma_Syllabus.pdf) (search_index_only): ABRSM ARSM diploma list includes the complete Op. 20 sonata as a recital work.
[10 ans avec le saxophone scan copy](https://pdfcoffee.com/10-ans-avec-le-saxophonepdf-pdf-free.html) (search_index_only): A Paris saxophone repertoire guide lists Op. 20, but the extracted table gives no study year for this entry.
[ABRSM, ARSM Repertoire List: Saxophone from 2026](https://www.abrsm.com.tw/diploma/from2024/music%20performance/ARSM/ABRSM%20Saxophone%20Performance%20ARSM%20Repertoire%20from%202026.pdf) (opened, 1): ABRSM’s ARSM saxophone list valid from January 2026 includes Tuthill’s complete Sonata for alto saxophone, Op. 20, published by Southern Music.
[Southern Music catalog via Keiser Productions](https://keiserproductions.com/catalog/southern-music/?ic=&sc=&tc=&tid=CDFE9FD5-9C3D-402C-9C76-A26D218F504F) (opened): Southern Music/Keiser identifies a separate concerto piano reduction as intended for advanced players; this listing is included only to distinguish the adjacent catalogue result and does not describe Op. 20.
[Philharmonie de Paris, 10 ans avec le saxophone](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, printed p. 71; PDF p. 66): On printed page 71 (PDF page 66), the guide identifies Tuthill’s Sonata, Op. 20, for alto saxophone and piano and lists Allegro giocoso, Andante un poco adagio, and Presto molto vivace. It gives no study-year placement.

Collection/transcription context: Original alto saxophone sonata with current advanced diploma repertoire placement.

Review focus: Inference: the ARSM inclusion establishes advanced diploma repertoire use, not an undergraduate year. The archival description suggests three contrasting movements, but score-specific demands remain unverified.

Open questions: The exact publisher edition score was not accessible. Ball State archival PDF could not be inspected after browser timeout; its catalogue description is not notation evidence.

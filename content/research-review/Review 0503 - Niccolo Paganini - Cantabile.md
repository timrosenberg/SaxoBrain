## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Remove from the catalog. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Alternate-edition notation. **Scoring:** Alto saxophone and piano; David Camwell, Dorn DP1501. **Duration:** Status unknown for the catalog edition..

- Alternate edition only: the inspected notation is Bertrand Moren's EMR101017, not Camwell DP1501. Its pitches and technical details must not be transferred to DP1501.
- Moren solo preview: Andante con rubato in common time, sustained slurred melody, detailed crescendos and diminuendos, and ritardando/a tempo coordination.
- Moren solo preview: triplet runs alternate with sixteenth-note flourishes, chromatic inflections, a quintuplet and a sextuplet before the displayed page ends.
- Moren score preview: solo line above a repeated triplet-chord piano accompaniment requires clear rhythmic independence and shared rubato.

**Alternate edition:** This notation does not establish the demands of the edition currently cataloged.

**Inspected scope and limits:** A NEW authorized alternate alto edition supplies concrete lyrical and ornamental evidence. Camwell DP1501 remains uninspected. Do not infer that a lyrical title makes the Camwell edition school repertoire.

**Edition or identity note:** DP1501 is Camwell's Cantabile in D Major. EMR101017 is a distinct Moren alto arrangement of Op.17. Cantabile and Waltz Op.19/MS45 is a different work.

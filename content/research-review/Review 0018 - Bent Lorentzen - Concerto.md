## Chat batch 06 follow-up, 2026-10-12

Wise Music’s full work page verifies solo alto, the specified wind/brass ensemble with piano and percussion, 23 minutes and rental availability. https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/22267/Saxofonkoncert--Bent-Lorentzen/ Dedicatee David Pituch’s publisher-hosted note describes a through-composed single movement. Lorentzen combines classical repertoire influence with avant-garde saxophone practice and Latin music. Ensemble block harmonies alternate short and sustained textures; the solo line can deliberately be covered by the ensemble. Some passages place different simultaneous tempo marks on saxophone and winds to loosen strict meter. These are documented program-note claims, not score observations. https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/22267/Saxofonkoncert--Bent-Lorentzen/ The worklist records completion on 10 June 1986 and premiere on 6 March 1987 with Pituch, Danish Radio Symphony Orchestra and David Epstein. https://www.danishmusicologyonline.dk/downloads/dmo_04/Bent_Lorentzen_Vaerkfortegnelse_090512.pdf POLMIC’s Warsaw Autumn archive documents Pituch with the Danish Radio Symphony Orchestra under Leif Segerstam on 23 September 1989. https://archiwumcyfrowe.polmic.pl/pub/material?id=3216

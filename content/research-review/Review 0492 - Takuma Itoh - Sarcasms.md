# Review 0492: Takuma Itoh - Sarcasms

[[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 03|Score batch 03]] · [[Takuma Itoh - Sarcasms|Catalog work]]

## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Advanced. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Notation. **Scoring:** Alto saxophone and piano. **Duration:** 8 minutes, composer webpage;7:00 total in preview contents table.

- First movement opening alternates 2/4 and 3/4 with accented low triplets at fff and a leap into a high growled note.
- Second-movement sample at quarter = 120 contains chromatic triplet lines, slurred leaps and an isolated sfz note marked honk.
- Both inspected pages demand close coordination with active piano writing and abrupt dynamic or tempo changes.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Authorized composer excerpt has 12 PDF pages. Visually inspected PDF pages 3 and 5, the opening notation of movements I and II. Retained complete page 3 with copyright. Later movements were not visually analyzed.

**Edition or source note:** Composer identifies five short movements,2008,commissioned by Donald Sinta. Webpage and score table differ on duration. Do not transfer circular-breathing claims from Itoh Palpitations to this work.

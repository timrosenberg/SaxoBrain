**Evidence:** Recording + sources. **Scoring:** Soprano and alto saxophone, one player; orchestra or composer’s piano reduction. **Duration:** Publisher: ca.24 minutes; Bensmann recording listing: 22:31.

- Ries & Erler explicitly lists soprano/alto for one soloist, establishing a doubling requirement.
- Nico Schüler describes four movements: Sostenuto/Vivace, Allegretto scherzando, Adagio molto espressivo, Allegro assai.
- Schüler reports special sound effects in solo cadenzas, including three-note chords at the close of the first-movement cadenza.
- Schüler identifies changing short tonal centers, contrasting expressive and aggressive episodes, and irregular rhythms. This remains attributed analysis.

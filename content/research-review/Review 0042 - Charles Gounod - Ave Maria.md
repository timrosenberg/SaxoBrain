## Research

Catalog entry: [[Charles Gounod - Ave Maria]].

Charles Gounod, Ave Maria (Méditation sur le premier prélude de J.S. Bach), arranged for saxophone and piano; the assigned edition credits Susan Fancher and supplies E-flat and B-flat solo parts.
[Hal Leonard](https://www.halleonard.com/product/50490314/ave-maria) (search_index_only): Hal Leonard catalog lists Nobuya Sugawa’s arrangement for soprano, alto, tenor or baritone saxophone and piano.
[JW Pepper](https://www.jwpepper.com/ave-maria-bach-gounod-11589520-2191490/p) (search_index_only): JW Pepper describes another saxophone-and-piano arrangement as an expressive legato melody over Bach’s C-major prelude.
[YouTube recording metadata](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j1FL5gndDRQ) (search_index_only): A published saxophone performance recording is identified as Kyle Mechmet, alto saxophone, and Casey Rafn, piano.
[Profs Edition public score excerpt](https://www.profs-edition.com/uploads/extrait_pdf/Ave-Maria-Gounod-sax-extrait_1590479015-extrait.pdf) (score_inspected, 3): A four-page public preview names David Louis as arranger, alto saxophone and piano, Andante semplice, and presents the instrument part. The score preface identifies the source as Gounod’s melody over Bach’s Prelude in C BWV 846 and says this alto arrangement is transposed a minor third above the original.

Score observations:
- Preview p. 3 opens with the alto saxophone resting while the piano plays repeating broken-chord figures. The saxophone enters later with a broad sustained phrase, followed by shorter moving notes; long ties, crescendi, soft dynamics and brief runs are visible. Preview p. 4 continues the lyrical part with repeated high held notes, slurred turns and changing crescendi/diminuendi. ([score source](https://www.profs-edition.com/uploads/extrait_pdf/Ave-Maria-Gounod-sax-extrait_1590479015-extrait.pdf))

Listening:

Collection/transcription context: Transcription of Gounod’s melody over Bach’s Prelude in C. The inspected preview is a different arranger/edition from the catalog’s Susan Fancher/Combre lead, so do not transfer its notes to that edition.

Review focus: Inference from the inspected David Louis preview: this arrangement centers on breath-supported sustained tone, smooth slurs, expressive dynamic tapering, and coordination with regular piano arpeggiation. These observations do not establish the Susan Fancher edition’s exact demands.

Open questions: Catalog references Susan Fancher and Combre, while the inspected score is arranged by David Louis. Edition match unresolved. Only opening and early part pages of the David Louis preview were inspected; complete range and later sections unknown.

## Chat batch 12 follow-up, 2026-10-14

CiNii’s opened score record identifies mezzo-soprano, violin or saxophone and piano, German/French text, and credits the saxophone transcription to Jean-Marie Londeix. Its 1937 date is a copyright/publication date. Fryderyk Chopin University’s 2018 official concert program explicitly dates the work 1933. The Anet institutional acquisitions catalog separately gives 1933 in the work notes while listing Eschig 1937 publication and plateME4522. This supports 1933 as the original work date. The exact 1937 edition is a chamber song with an instrumental substitution. The optional violin and alto saxophone occupy the same role; they are not an expanded simultaneous ensemble. Warner Classics’ official YouTube Topic release identifies Nin and credits Danielle Millet, mezzo-soprano, Jean-Marie Londeix, saxophone, and Henriette Puig-Roget, piano. It is the complete named track, duration 5:26. Metadata matches the cataloged saxophone version. Chopin University’s Erasmus student concert demonstrates university performance of the work, but uses the performers’ own arrangement with mezzo-soprano, soprano and alto saxophones, and piano. That scoring is not evidence to add soprano to the ME4522 Londeix edition. CiNii’s linked author heading says Nin-Culmell, Joaquín despite the score transcription naming Joaquin Nin. Independent university and label sources identify Joaquín Nin 1879–1949, so that catalog authority-link conflict is retained privately.

Collection context: A vocal chamber transcription for alto saxophone with professional recorded use. University use is documented in a different student ensemble arrangement. No youthful teaching simplification verified.

Open questions: No actual notation inspected. Written alto range, saxophone transposition in the piano score, rhythmic demands and text/ensemble coordination remain unknown. Original 1933 date corroborated by institutional work/program notes. A performer upload instead says 1937, apparently using the publication date; that claim was excluded. Existing Voice and Piano fields are preserved. Optional violin is contextual evidence, not an automatic simultaneous ensemble addition. No complete free score located.

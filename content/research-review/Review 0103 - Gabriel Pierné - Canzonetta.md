## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Publisher-distributor confirms Mule alto saxophone and piano transcription, AL19414, eight pages, from the clarinet and piano work of 1888. The publisher describes one movement in contrasting linked sections: dotted siciliano-like rhythms, a scherzando passage, a Più Vivo section without the opening dotted rhythm, and a return of the opening theme. These are publisher descriptions, not independently inspected notation. Trinity College London's saxophone repertoire list, May 2026 update, places the exact Mule/Leduc AL19414 edition in Grade 7. This is an exam-board placement. An exact named saxophone recording by Marcel Mule and pianist Marcel Gaveau is publicly playable on YouTube. The uploader identifies Pierné, Op. 19 and Mule's arrangement. The player duration is 3:38. Richard Scholfield's public recital also contains Canzonetta at 34:30. The visible video identifies arr. R. Scholfield, so it is a separate revised arrangement.

Collection context: Mule transcription is explicitly described by the publisher for advanced players and appears in Trinity's Grade 7 list. No simplification designation verified.

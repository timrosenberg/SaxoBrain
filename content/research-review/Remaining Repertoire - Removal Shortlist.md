# Remaining Repertoire - Removal Shortlist

[[Remaining Repertoire - Decision Queue|Decision queue]]

All three removal decisions were approved by Tim and applied October 20, 2026. The recommendations and sources below remain as review history. Continue with [[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 01|the 25-work score batch]].

## R1. Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart - Adagio & Romance

Catalog entry removed October 20, 2026.

**Recommendation:** Recommend scope removal under the approved exclusion of educational lyrical transcriptions. Exact Hautvast soprano/piano identity is corroborated by the distributor. Tierolff lists the same title and arranger for soprano as an alternative solo instrument and labels it grade 2-3. This is a scope judgment. It is not a score-based finding about its complete musical demands.

**Confidence:** high edition identification; medium scope-removal recommendation.

- Adagio & Romance by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, arranged Willy Hautvast, item P46, soprano saxophone among the solo alternatives, publisher grade 2-3. [Tierolff primary publisher; live search-index verification, direct fetch unavailable](https://tierolff.nl/en/adagio-romance-p46.html).
- Printed page 500 identifies Mozart Adagio & Romance, Willy Hautvast, soprano saxophone and piano, TierolffSS3075. [Elkin Music distributor catalog; live indexed exact edition](https://www.elkinmusic.com/public/catalog.pdf).

**Decision:** Remove. Approved and applied October 20, 2026.

## R2. Felix Mendelssohn - Six Romances sans parole

**Future edition:** Tim intends to add this repertoire back someday using a different edition. The Gérald Venturi / Billaudot GB6917 entry was removed. No replacement edition has been selected.

Catalog entry removed October 20, 2026.

**Recommendation:** Recommend scope removal only if the approved exclusion covers complete collections of lyrical Romantic piano transcriptions. Billaudot identifies this exact Venturi collection as cycle 2. A newly found mirror of a 2015 MTNA Young Artist program includes three selections, so do not say the edition has no college-age use. Its removal would be a deliberate catalog-scope choice.

**Confidence:** high edition identification; medium scope-removal recommendation.

- Six romances sans paroles opus 19, 30 et 53, Gerald Venturi arrangement, E-flat saxophone and piano, GB6917, 32 pages, copyright 2000, moderately difficult (cycle 2). [Billaudot primary publisher; live search-index verification, direct fetch unavailable](https://www.billaudot.com/gb6917-6.html).
- Same GB6917 edition and identity with cycle-2 classification. [Billaudot French primary catalog; live index](https://www.billaudot.com/six-romances-sans-paroles-opus-19-30-et-53-3.html).
- Young Artist woodwinds section of January 18, 2015 program lists Six Romances Sans Paroles transcribed by Gerald Venturi, selecting Op.19 No.4, Op.19 No.6 and Op.53 No.4. The program defines Young Artist as ages 19-26. This is counterevidence to a blanket claim of no college-age use; mirror provenance limits confidence. [Third-party mirror of MTNA South Central program; opened live, original host not located](https://expydoc.com/doc/4967429/student-performance-competitions-january-18--2015).

**Decision:** Remove. Approved and applied October 20, 2026.

## R3. Armando Ghidoni - Douce Chansonnette

Catalog entry removed October 20, 2026.

**Recommendation:** Recommend removal as educational repertoire outside the collection’s college/professional focus. This is an original work, rather than a transcription. Lille Conservatoire places it in cycle I, fourth year; that system is not a SaxoBrain college-year designation. The list misattributes the publisher, so the exact Leduc edition should be checked if retained.

**Confidence:** medium, educational-use evidence; no complete score reviewed.

- Cycle I, fourth-year admission repertoire lists Douce chansonnette by A. Ghidoni; the list’s Billaudot imprint conflicts with the established Leduc AL27575 edition. [Lille Conservatoire admission repertoire, live search-index verification](https://conservatoire.lille.fr/download/file/2471?key=I3Mr1V05Oj1bGR6cr4%2F3vQ%3D%3D).

**Decision:** Remove. Approved and applied October 20, 2026.

## Transcriptions retained for review

The two Paganini Sonata Appassionata entries represent different alto and soprano editions, DP1443 and DP1442. They were not treated as duplicate records. [Dorn publisher catalog, soprano and alto sections](https://www.grothmusic.com/images/DP%20Catalog%20April%202025.pdf).

Mozart’s complete oboe concerto, Telemann’s concerto, Joplin’s rag and the Korngold suite remain for edition or score review. A transcription label alone does not establish whether a work belongs in the collection.

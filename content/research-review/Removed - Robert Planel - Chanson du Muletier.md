## Research notes (private)

**Earlier research superseded:** This exact record is Bonneau Suite AL 20303, not Planel. Remove its attribution from Planel research; publisher-authorized score sample verifies AL 20251 instead.

### Chat Batch 16 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

Chanson du Muletier is movement VI of Suite romantique for alto saxophone and piano, Leduc AL 20251. Authorized score sample bears copyright 1944, which does not prove the composition year. All six individual movements exist in this catalog; no complete-suite target record is established. Previously cited WindMusic id 11435 actually describes Paul Bonneau’s Suite AL 20303, not Planel’s Suite romantique.

This is a guide description, not direct score inspection.

Collection/transcription context: One of six original alto saxophone/piano pieces, from 1944; no simplification is established.

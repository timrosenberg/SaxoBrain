Live Groth HTML confirms the exact AL29793 alto saxophone and piano publication. The Fédération Française de l’Enseignement Artistique’s 2015 saxophone syllabus lists Sur la Place by J. Delacroix, Leduc, in its Cycle I third-year E-flat repertoire. This is a documented French music-school use, separate from any SaxoBrain placement. Forsyth’s edition description labels AL29793 cycle 1, with 9 pages and alto saxophone/piano in the title. Michel Musique describes it as the end of the first cycle. These edition-specific teaching descriptions are more concrete than Stanton’s general promotional language. No transcription or source work is identified in the opened exact-edition records. The evidence supports a standalone saxophone-and-piano publication; original-composition provenance and composition date remain unknown. The actual AL29793 cover explicitly prints Fin de 1er cycle, confirming the intended French teaching-cycle context directly from the publication. This cover contains no notation.

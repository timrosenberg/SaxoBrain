**Identity and findings.** Camden CM061 Mood Music is a six-piece alto saxophone and piano collection credited in retailer catalogues to Philip or Phillip Gates; composer spelling remains unresolved across those listings. The National College of Music, London’s alto/baritone syllabus includes Wonderland, one title from the collection, on its Grade 5 solo list.

**Sources checked.**
- [Groth Music Camden listing](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-147384.aspx) (search_index_only): Catalog lead lists CM061, six movements and classical/jazz bridge description; composer spelling Philip vs Phillip needs confirmation.
- [National College of Music, London saxophone syllabus](https://ncm-london.co.uk/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/syllabus-saxophone-alto-baritone.pdf) (opened, p. 8, Grade 5, List B): The National College of Music, London Grade Examinations in E-flat Alto/Baritone Saxophone lists Gates’s Wonderland from Mood Music under Grade 5 (Students’ Division). This supports that one item only; it does not establish a grade for all six pieces.

Sonata The Life of Christ, Op.34, for soprano saxophone AND piano. Current instrumentation omits piano. Opening score dates the work 1991, revised 1992. Dorn DORN0151 includes four movements, piano score and solo part; retailer lists 54 pages. Retailer reports premiere March 7, 1992 at Oberlin with composer and his sister.

**Score observations:** Opening score page and both performance-note pages inspected. Opening: 4/4, quarter=120, chromatic melodic fragments, wide registral shifts and abrupt dynamic changes. Player must stand in front of the piano and direct sound into it at indicated points; piano-string plucking is defined. Circular breathing is optional.

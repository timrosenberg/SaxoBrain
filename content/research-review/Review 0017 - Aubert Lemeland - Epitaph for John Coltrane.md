Catalog: [[Aubert Lemeland - Epitaph for John Coltrane]]

- Identity: Aubert Lemeland's *Epitaph to John Coltrane*, Billaudot GB3218, is a B-flat saxophone-and-piano work. Billaudot places it in *L'enseignement moderne du saxophone* and labels it moderately difficult (cycle 2). The publisher's B-flat designation is compatible with soprano saxophone, but the exact instrument is not specified on the record.

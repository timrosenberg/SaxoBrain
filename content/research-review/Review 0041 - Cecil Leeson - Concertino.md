Catalog entry: [[Cecil Leeson - Concertino]].

Cecil Leeson, Concertino for E-flat alto saxophone and wind symphonette (or chamber orchestra), with piano reduction. Aaron Eric Butler’s 1981 Ball State thesis catalog describes multiple archival states: an early reduction volume containing movements I and II, a separate volume for movement III, a later complete reduction, an E-flat alto part, and an engraver-corrected publisher copy of the full three-movement piano reduction. The two-movement description applies to one early volume, not the whole work. The PDF is a catalog of archival materials, not the musical notation itself.
[Aaron Eric Butler, Music Manuscripts and Photographs in Cecil Leeson Archival Saxophone Collection, Ball State University, printed pp. 24–29 (PDF pp. 29–34)](https://cardinalscholar.bsu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/cb5bf379-2361-4cb6-8f58-05af0aa35c89/content) (opened, printed pp. 24–29; PDF pp. 29–34): Opened Ball State University thesis catalog of the Cecil Leeson Archival Saxophone Collection. Printed pp. 24–29 (PDF pp. 29–34) describe several manuscript states: an early 64-page piano reduction volume with movements I–II; a companion volume for movement III; a later complete reduction; a separate E-flat alto saxophone part for all three movements; and an engraver-corrected publisher copy of the full work. The first two movement timings are listed as about 7 and 5 minutes. These are archival descriptions, not notation.
[Ball State University repository](https://cardinalscholar.bsu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/cb5bf379-2361-4cb6-8f58-05af0aa35c89/content) (opened): Search result for the Ball State archive records originally listed the work for saxophone and wind symphonette, with piano reduction; this was followed to the full PDF thesis.

- Identity: Jaromír Weinberger, Concerto for E-flat alto saxophone and orchestra, commissioned by Cecil Leeson. Piano score was completed in 1940 and its premiere was delayed until 1946; Keiser’s current performance edition incorporates post-premiere revisions by Tristan Willems, with orchestra and piano-reduction formats.
- Keiser describes commission, completion/premiere timeline, revisions, performance edition, and available piano reduction. [Keiser Productions](https://keiserproductions.com/jaromir-weinbergers-alto-saxophone-concerto-reemerges/) (opened).

[Ball State University archive](https://cardinalscholar.bsu.edu/bitstreams/cb5bf379-2361-4cb6-8f58-05af0aa35c89/download) (search_index_only).

# John C. Worley: Five Winter Landscapes Research

[[Review 0200 - John C. Worley - Windswept Snowscapes|Original movement review]] · [[Chat Batch 12|Batch 12 decisions]]

The complete work’s existence is confirmed by printed score headings. It is not added to the public catalog because the full five-movement contents and a complete edition remain unverified. Tim requested removal of the standalone Windswept Snowscapes entry.

## Confirmed Components

| Movement | Title | Instrumentation | Edition |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | White Morning Stillness | Tenor saxophone and piano | Dorn 0212 |
| II | Windswept Snowscapes | Alto saxophone and piano | Dorn 0208 |
| Number unknown | Ski Trail Through the Birches | Soprano saxophone and piano | Dorn 0153 |

[White Morning Stillness score](https://grothmusic.com/images/product/large/ae00-127241.jpg) explicitly names the cycle, movement I and tenor. Its manuscript heading dates this movement 1990. Only the opening page was inspected. It begins Largo at quarter note 48–52 in 4/4, with a quiet solo entrance over independent piano voices. [Edition listing](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-146371.aspx).

[Windswept Snowscapes score](https://grothmusic.com/images/product/large/ae00-127013.jpg) explicitly names the cycle, movement II and alto. Its opening notation was inspected in batch 12. [Edition listing](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-146147.aspx).

[Ski Trail cover](https://grothmusic.com/images/product/large/ae00-121714.jpg) explicitly says “From Five Winter Landscapes” and names soprano saxophone and piano. The [inspected first solo-part page](https://grothmusic.com/images/product/large/ae00-121714_2_.jpg) begins Andante at quarter note 84 in 4/4. Sustained slurred melody, chromatic notes, later sixteenth-note runs and meter changes are visible. The 1991 copyright is edition information. No movement number or original composition date was verified. [Edition listing](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-140113.aspx).

## Unconfirmed Lead

The [Dorn April 2025 catalog](https://www.grothmusic.com/images/DP%20Catalog%20April%202025.pdf), printed page 8, lists Twilight Pine Shadow, order 216, under baritone saxophone and piano. This verifies the separate title and instrumentation. It does not establish that this piece belongs to Five Winter Landscapes.

## Remaining Gaps

- All five movement titles, complete order and full instrumentation.
- A single complete edition or an exact full-work library catalog record.
- Original composition date for the entire cycle. Movement I’s 1990 date is kept separate.
- Whether one player doubles saxophones or different performers play the movements.
- A complete-cycle recording and documented whole-cycle performance context.

Exact-title web searches, Groth’s public Worley and cycle searches, the publisher catalog, and a NYPL published-score finding aid were checked. The three component editions above are verified. No full-work listing was found in those sources. No difficulty level is assigned or proposed.

## Research

Catalog: [[Leonard Price - Big Sur Triptych]]

- Identity: Deon Nielsen Price’s Big Sur Triptych is a 1985 work for soprano saxophone or B-flat clarinet and piano, published by Southern Music in 1996. It lasts about nine minutes and has three movements: Sea Otters, Redwoods, and Crags. The composer’s performance archive and contemporaneous reviews document saxophone performances.
- Composer’s site gives instrumentation, date, duration, movement titles, 1985 first performance and recordings. [Culver Crest Publications, composer site](https://culvercrest.com/?p=2507) (search_index_only).
- University library record confirms 1996 score and separate part pagination. [Seitoku University library](https://www.seitoku.jp/lib/newhp/newbooks/2019/shinchaku201905.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Composer performance archive lists Bill Wilson saxophone performance of Big Sur Triptych. [Culver Crest Publications, composer site](https://culvercrest.com/deon-nielsen-price/performances/) (opened).
- Composer-hosted review characterizes the first movement as detached and the second as lyrical, with abrupt phrasing in the third. [Culver Crest Publications, quoted review](https://culvercrest.com/deon-nielsen-price/reviews/critical-comments/) (opened).
- Recording: [Composer archive documents saxophonists Bill Wilson and Paul Stewart performing the work.](https://culvercrest.com/deon-nielsen-price/performances/) Performance credits checked; audio playback not auditioned.
- Collection/use: Alternate-instrument edition for soprano saxophone or B-flat clarinet with piano. The available records establish the paired options but do not establish which instrument was original.
- Review focus: Inference: the documented optional clarinet version and review-based contrasts invite checking the saxophone part for detached articulation and lyrical line across movements.
- Gaps: No public score pages inspected; original-instrument provenance remains unknown.; Composer archive lists performances, but accessible audio for this work was not verified.
- Proposed correction: Add piano to instrumentation and qualify soprano saxophone as an alternative to B-flat clarinet.

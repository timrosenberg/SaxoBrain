## Research

Catalog: [[Jacques Ibert - Symphonie Concertante]]

- Identity: Jacques Ibert, Symphonie concertante, originally for oboe and string orchestra, arranged by Grégoire Nicole for soprano saxophone and piano. The Leduc edition is AL29268 (1951); Ibert’s work catalogue gives 27 minutes for the original concerto.
- Ibert’s official catalogue identifies the original oboe-and-string-orchestra scoring and the Grégoire Nicole soprano saxophone and piano arrangement, Leduc AL29268, 1951. [Jacques Ibert catalogue](https://www.jacquesibert.fr/ibert/Catalogue.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Hal Leonard lists the soprano saxophone and piano setting and 46-page edition. [Sheet Music Plus](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/symphonie-concertante-saxophone-soprano-piano-20749257.html) (search_index_only).
- Wise Music’s work page currently describes an alto saxophone and piano scoring, conflicting with the Ibert catalogue’s edition-specific soprano saxophone listing. [Wise Music](https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/52346/Symphonie-Concertante--Jacques-Ibert/) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: This saxophone edition is a transcription of an oboe concerto. The catalog’s inclusion of orchestra should be verified against its soprano sax and piano edition.
- Gaps: Saxophone arrangement score and saxophone recording not inspected; the current Wise Music alto-sax label conflicts with the edition-specific composer catalogue.
- Proposed correction: Consider removing Orchestra from the instrument fields if the catalog entry is specifically the AL29268 soprano saxophone and piano reduction.

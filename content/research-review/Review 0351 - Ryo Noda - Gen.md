**Identity and findings.** Ryo Noda’s Gen is the author’s piano transcription of his alto saxophone concerto, Leduc AL27329. The Philharmonie de Paris guide gives an approximate 18-minute duration and reports a high altissimo E-flat; Wise Music lists 15 minutes for the orchestral work, so the duration figures are version/source specific.

**Sources checked.**
- [Wise Music Classical](https://www.wisemusicclassical.com/work/51441/Gen---Concerto-for-Alto-Saxophone--Ryo-Noda/) (opened): Leduc AL27329 is a reduction for alto saxophone and piano of Noda's concerto.
- [Presto Music](https://www.prestomusic.com/sheet-music/products/7380188--ryo-noda-gen) (opened): A retailer identifies the 24-page Leduc AL27329 edition.

This is secondary pedagogical description, not a notation inspection.

## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Remove from the catalog. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Listening / source description. **Scoring:** Soprano or tenor saxophone and piano; Dave Camwell, Dorn DP1408. **Duration:** Status unknown for the catalog edition..

- Source context only: Edward Neill's 1974 specialist article describes a soprano-voice aria with four variations and abundant florid embellishment of sustained melody notes. This is not observation of Camwell's saxophone notation.
- Camwell's register, breath points, articulation and preservation of the four variations remain unknown.

**No notation inspected for this card.** Source descriptions and recording identification are not direct score analysis. Use Hold if the evidence does not support a decision.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Vocal variation transcription with an attribution caveat. The vocal model provides phrasing/ornamentation context; it does not establish saxophone difficulty.

**Edition or identity note:** The 2021 Paganini Genova Festival booklet attributes the vocal aria to Giacomo Cordella, explaining that Paganini transcribed it for Antonia Bianchi. Keep that caveat beside the circulating Paganini/Camwell title. The older 1974 article reflects the earlier attribution.

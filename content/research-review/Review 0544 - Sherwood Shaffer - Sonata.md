## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Third Year. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Listening / source description. **Scoring:** Catalog says alto saxophone and piano; verified sonatas are for tenor or baritone. **Duration:** Catalog alto work unknown; alternate baritone Sonata publisher estimate 16 minutes.

- The composer’s publisher list identifies Sonata: When Mountains Rising for tenor saxophone and piano and a separate Sonata for baritone saxophone and piano. It does not identify an alto Sonata.
- Subito identifies the baritone work as a 2016 composition, approximately sixteen minutes, E.B. Marks 80603970. Abbi Stewart’s linked recording belongs to this baritone work.
- Stephen Pollock describes the tenor Sonata as three movements that can be performed separately. Its middle movement is Past Singing Springs. These facts belong to the tenor work.
- No alto edition or notation was established. None of the alternate works’ ranges or technical demands can be transferred to this record.

**No notation inspected for this card.** Source descriptions and recording identification are not direct score analysis. Use Hold if the evidence does not support a decision.

**Edition or identity note:** The generic alto Sonata record remains unresolved. The tenor Sonata and the 2016 baritone Sonata are distinct works. No instrument, title or composer data were changed.

- Identity: Arthur Frackenpohl's *Sonata* for soprano or tenor saxophone and piano is a three-movement work (Fast, Slowly, Fast) dedicated to James Stoltie, according to the Groth catalog record. The associated Dorn Publications number is DORN0141. The 1982 date appears in a specialist saxophone program-note compilation and requires edition confirmation.

- Collection/use: Original saxophone sonata dedicated to a saxophonist is indicated; no evidence of youth simplification.

## Chat batch 06 follow-up, 2026-10-12

J.W. Pepper identifies Dorn no. 141 as Sonata-Tenor or Soprano Sax/Piano and specifies copyright 1990. That is an edition date, not secure composition evidence. https://www.jwpepper.com/sonata-tenor-or-soprano-sax-piano-7932619/p Western Illinois University’s 15 February 2015 Honors College Project Recital documents Steven Olson, tenor, and Liang-Yu Wang, piano. Its notes describe sonata-form first movement with playful and lyrical themes and considerable syncopation; a ternary D-major ballad with 9/8 B section; and a lively finale with 5/8 interruptions and active ensemble interaction.

**Tim’s decision:** Third Year. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Source description. **Scoring:** Alto saxophone and piano. **Duration:** 9 minutes, reported by Karlien Gerber, pp.74-75.

- Gerber reports changing 4/4, 5/4, 3/4 and 2/4 meters, with quarter-note tempos 63 and 72.
- Gerber identifies short-note disjunct passages with wide leaps and a free passage after a fermata rest.
- Gerber reports trills and grace-note ornaments, with dynamics extending from ppp to fff.

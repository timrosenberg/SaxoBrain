## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Hal Leonard verifies the CH78969 soprano saxophone and piano edition. Wise Music’s Spring 2012 publication list distinguishes CH78969 piano from CH78958 organ. Naxos’s exact track 12 on Living Voices 8.572737 is the organ version, 7:33, performed by Jeremy Powell and Ken Cowan. The prior review’s suggestion of a Powell/Lakeland piano recording was incorrect: the label assigns Lakeland’s piano to track 20, not Peter Abelard. Naxos’s back cover dates the work 2006/11. Existing composed: 2011 was preserved; the paired date does not safely establish an original single year for a blank-field addition. The University of Utah’s March 23, 2025 student recital program lists Trevor McFarland, saxophone, with Sean McFarland, organ, in this piece. This documents college student performance use. James Whitbourn’s publisher-supplied description identifies contrasting narrative sections, Dance of Peter and Héloïse, Revenge of Canon Fulbert, O Quanta Qualia and In Paradisum. These titles document programmatic design rather than proving specific instrumental techniques.

Collection context: Original narrative saxophone chamber work with separate piano and organ editions. Professional label recording and college student recital use documented. No youth simplification established.

Open questions: No notation inspected; saxophone part range, exact rhythm, ornaments and special techniques remain unknown. Existing Spotify link is the organ version with McChrystal/Wilson, not the piano edition. The 2006/11 date requires edition-history clarification before treating either number as the original composition year.

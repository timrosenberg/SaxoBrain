Score observations: Inspected the partly masked piano-score page 2, headed Le Cheval de bronze pour Saxophone et Piano. The solo staff is explicitly labelled Saxophone son réels, so the displayed pitches are concert pitch and do not establish the transposed alto or soprano part range. The visible opening is in 4/4, Moderato, quarter note 76. It uses beamed eighth notes with short slurs and detached notes, then longer accented notes and a crescendo from mp toward f. The piano initially doubles or closely supports the moving solo rhythm in both hands, then changes to sustained chords. The lower portion shows a transition to Adagio, quarter note 60, with solo rests and a piano interlude. The visible tasks are changing between connected and detached eighth notes, shaping the crescendo and accents, and keeping rhythmic alignment with the piano. A large diagonal mask prevents inspection of most of the lower page.

Collection context: Documented Cycle I teaching work for alto or soprano saxophone and piano. No evidence established that it simplifies an earlier work.

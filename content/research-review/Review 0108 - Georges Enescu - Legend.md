## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Stanton's exact catalogue listing identifies George Enescu, arranger Sharon Davis, publisher Western International Music, soprano saxophone series, WIM601. It marks the item permanently out of print. This is a useful exact soprano-edition lead. The listing supplies no score preview, duration, or explicit accompaniment. Other Western International listings offer Legend for alto saxophone and piano or English horn and piano. Those are separate editions and do not establish the accompaniment or saxophones of WIM601.

Collection context: Saxophone transcription edition found. No teaching simplification or young-player designation verified.

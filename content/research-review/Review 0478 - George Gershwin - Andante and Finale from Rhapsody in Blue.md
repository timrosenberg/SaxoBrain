# Review 0478: George Gershwin - Andante and Finale from Rhapsody in Blue

[[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 03|Score batch 03]] · [[George Gershwin - Andante and Finale from Rhapsody in Blue|Catalog work]]

## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** First Year. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Notation. **Scoring:** One B-flat, E-flat or bass-clef solo instrument and piano; B-flat part accommodates soprano saxophone. **Duration:** Unknown for this excerpt arrangement.

- Opening Allegro con brio uses accented triplet repeated notes followed by a chromatic eighth-note descent and molto ritardando e diminuendo.
- Andantino moderato con espressione begins with stepwise quarter notes and whole notes tied over barlines, requiring sustained tone and planned breathing.
- The soloist must coordinate rubato, a fermata, tempo transition and long held notes over a moving chromatic piano accompaniment.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Visual inspection limited to the retailer public preview, piano score page 1 with solo line. This supports expressive and ensemble observations only; the finale and transposed B-flat part were unavailable.

**Edition or source note:** Beeler transcription, copyright 1924/1946 on inspected page. This is an excerpt arrangement. The printed solo line above piano is a score reference, so its displayed pitches do not establish soprano written range. Its slow lyrical opening does not establish elementary difficulty for the complete arrangement.

**Identity and findings.** Advance Music ADV7032 is a three-movement work for alto saxophone and piano: Black Light, Heavy Water and Slam Dance. The publisher-distributor description characterizes its jazz harmony and modal material. One page of the E-flat alto saxophone part for movement I, Black Light, was inspected; the piano part and later movements were not.

**Sources checked.**
- [Presto Music, Advance Music listing](https://www.prestomusic.com/sheet-music/products/7477099--yasinitsky-g-new-wave) (opened): ADV7032 is listed for alto saxophone and piano. The description says the first movement uses driving 6/8 and imitation, the middle alternates cadenza-like saxophone writing, transparent themes and a darker section, and the finale builds.
- [Alfred Music public preview](https://content.alfred.com/catpages/01-ADV7032.pdf) (score_inspected, preview p.1): Alfred public preview page for the alto part, movement I Black Light, was visually inspected. It confirms E-flat alto saxophone and provides the opening excerpt.

**Score observations.**
- The preview is explicitly the E-flat alto saxophone part for movement I, Black Light, so its notes are in the saxophone part rather than concert pitch. After a full-measure opening rest in 6/8 at Vivo, quarter=120, the line moves into a sustained passage with slurred groups, frequent chromatic accidentals, and dynamics ranging from forte to piano. The excerpt later shows register expansion with ledger-line notes and renewed faster note groups. Only this one solo-part page was inspected; it does not show the piano accompaniment or later movements.

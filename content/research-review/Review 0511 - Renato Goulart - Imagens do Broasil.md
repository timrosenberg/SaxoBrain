# Review 0511: Renato Goulart - Imagens do Broasil

[[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 04|Score batch 04]] · [[Renato Goulart - Imagens do Broasil|Catalog work]]

**Batch decision number: 18.** Decision recorded October 20, 2026.

## Decision evidence

[Recording selected by Tim](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uVPrlP2Ky4I).

**Tim’s decision:** Second Year. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Notation. **Scoring:** Alto saxophone and piano. **Duration:** 7:57 publisher-linked performance video; 7:26 Garza recording listed in thesis, p. 39. These are recording lengths..

- Inspected figure 22, mm. 1–14: Moderato opening; saxophone enters after piano introduction, with long slurred/tied phrases and pickup gestures over a syncopated piano pattern.
- Inspected figures 25–26: sustained lyrical saxophone writing in mm. 87–98 contrasts with an Allegro piano texture built from syncopated repeated rhythmic figures in mm. 134–143.
- Garza, pp. 28–32, attributes the sectional character to choro, baião and batuque influences based on composer correspondence. He identifies unison rhythmic passages and saxophone/piano interplay as rehearsal concerns.
- Garza describes phrase planning and clear direction as demanding in the slower melodic sections. His p. 31 account says the final five measures form a declamatory cadenza; that ending was not inspected here.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Selected saxophone/piano notation excerpts in Gilbert Garza’s 2020 DMA thesis, printed pp. 29 and31; figures 22, 25, 26. These are scholarly excerpts, not a complete retail score inspection.

**Edition or identity note:** Catalog typo Broasil is contradicted by composer cover and thesis title Brasil. Thesis identifies a Dorn 2009 edition; current composer-published ArrangeMe edition A0.1741457 has not been compared note-for-note. Earlier research cited 1999; thesis bibliography and recording list say 2009, so composition date remains unresolved.

## Research

Catalog: [Colin Labadie - Strata.md](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/content/works/Colin Labadie - Strata.md). Full evidence log: [research record](/Users/trosenberg/Documents/GitHub/SaxoBrain/research-review-2026-10-11/lane-3/records/0058.json).

**Identity and findings.** The cataloged Strata is Colin Labadie’s 2012 unaccompanied tenor saxophone work, originally conceived as a solo movement. A 2015 score copy presents Sonder for solo tenor alongside Opia for alto and piano; its program note is internally inconsistent about later planned movements, so the cataloged solo version should remain distinct from the expanded edition.

**Sources checked.**
- [Scribd-hosted score copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/775613570/Labadie-Strata) (score_inspected, cover, pp. 1-3): The 2015 score copy’s cover identifies Strata for saxophone and piano, with I. Sonder (solo tenor sax) and II. Opia (alto sax and piano); its program note says the piece began as a 2012 solo-tenor work. The score copy is an unidentified user upload on Scribd, not a publisher or composer record.
- [Scribd-hosted score copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/775613570/Labadie-Strata) (score_inspected, pp. 1-3): Visually inspected the score’s transposed solo-tenor opening page and a later passage. Opening has continuous fast repeated-note motion with accents and contrasting f/p dynamics at q=80; later pages retain dense patterned motion. No exact pitch/range claim was taken from the low-resolution preview.
- [Laura Kerslake, University of Alberta DMus thesis (2015), third-party Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/606847526/Dis-analyz-sax) (search_index_only, printed pp. 20-21): Laura Kerslake’s University of Alberta Doctor of Music thesis (2015), Without Ceasing: A Performance Analysis of Six Solo Works for Saxophone, discusses the 2012 solo-tenor version. Its indexed excerpts identify uninterrupted sixteenth-note texture, circular breathing for most of the work, accented altissimo introduced on score page 2, and wide register leaps; the exact analysis appears around printed pp. 20-21. The university repository copy was not located; accessed only through an uploaded Scribd copy/search index.
- [University of Western Ontario saxophone jury requirements](https://music.uwo.ca/departments/music-performance/juryRequirements/SAXOPHONE%20jury%20requirements.pdf) (search_index_only): University of Western Ontario’s 2024-25 saxophone jury requirements list Strata-Opia in upper-level saxophone study and require consultation with the studio teacher for repertoire selection. This supports academic use of the expanded version, not a level for the solo movement.
- [International Jean-Marie Londeix Saxophone Competition regulations](https://londeix2014.adolphesax.com/index.php/es/el-concurso-londeix/reglamento-londeix) (search_index_only): The 2014 International Jean-Marie Londeix competition repertoire regulation lists Strata for tenor saxophone in its unaccompanied first-elimination round.
- [Allison Balcetis, Relay. Launch.](https://allisonbalcetis.bandcamp.com/album/relay-launch) (opened): Allison Balcetis’s Bandcamp release includes a 7:54 track titled “Strata I. Sonder” by Colin Labadie. This is a verified track listing from the performer’s account; playback was not auditioned.
- [Colin Labadie, Strata page](https://www.colinlabadie.com/strata.html) (search_index_only): Composer’s cited strata page was linked by the 2015 score copy and competition listing, but direct access to https://www.colinlabadie.com/strata.html failed.
- [Sandra Joy Friesen, performance highlights](https://www.sandrajoyfriesen.com/performance-highlights/) (opened): Sandra Joy Friesen’s performer page documents Bent Note’s 2015 commissioned premiere of Strata-Opia at SaxOpen. This corroborates the distinct saxophone-and-piano version.

**Score observations.**
- In the visually inspected transposed tenor-saxophone opening (printed p. 1), a long, tightly repeated rhythmic stream uses written accents and marked contrasting f/p dynamics at q=80. A later inspected solo-tenor page continues the dense repeated-note texture and patterned accents. This is a visual observation from an unofficial, low-resolution third-party copy; exact pitches and range were not recorded.

**Score/sample.**

**Collection and audience context.** Original contemporary solo-tenor saxophone work, documented in an international saxophone competition’s first unaccompanied round and a doctoral performance-analysis project. The separate expanded Strata-Opia edition adds an alto-and-piano movement; do not conflate it with the cataloged solo version.

**Review focus.** Inference from the inspected transposed score and Kerslake’s indexed analysis: review breath planning for the unbroken pattern, rhythmic steadiness, dynamic separation between accented and non-accented notes, and facility in large register changes/altissimo. The altissimo and circular-breathing claims come from the thesis excerpt, not independent score pitch reading.

**Open questions.**
- The composer’s official Strata page and publisher-issued score were not accessible; the notation inspected is an unidentified Scribd user upload.
- Original University of Alberta repository copy of Kerslake’s 2015 thesis was not located after focused exact-title, author, and repository searches; pedagogical details remain search-indexed excerpts from a Scribd-hosted copy.
- The 2015 score copy has an internally inconsistent program note about the number and sequence of additional movements. Confirm the cataloged version with an authoritative source if possible.
- Bandcamp track is identified by the performer and title, but playback and instrument-specific recording details were not auditioned.

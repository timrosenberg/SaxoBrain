**Proposed decision:** First Year. **Confidence:** medium. **Approval:** Applied 2026-10-15.

**Reason:** Provisional First Year. The Royal Conservatory syllabus places Allegro in Grade 8 alongside selected Heiden, Lunde and Wilder sonata movements. Tim assigns Wilder First Year and Heiden/Lunde Second Year. The actual opening adds tonal projection, interval control and flexible pacing without showing advanced techniques. This comparative evidence supports First Year, with the usual limitation that movement-level exam lists cannot define whole-work difficulty.

Alto saxophone and piano edition BMC 382; inspected cover and score show Boston Music imprint and General Music copyright 1966. University of Scranton composer archive lists publication 1966 and duration 4:30. Neither establishes composition date. The syllabus PDF is titled 2013 Edition despite 2016 appearing in its download filename; the precise source is cited accordingly.

**Score observations:** Opening score page visually inspected: Allegrocon bravura; prominent sustained and separated quarter/half-note figures, slurred leaps, strong accents and rests against a more active piano part. No multiphonics or other special techniques visible in this opening. Later technical passages and whole-work range unverified.

**Collection context:** Original concert repertoire; no evidence of a simplified youth transcription.

**Remaining gaps:** Full score and exact recording not verified. 1966 is publication/copyright, not confirmed composition.

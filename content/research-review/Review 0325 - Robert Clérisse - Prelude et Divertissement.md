## Chat Batch 16 Follow-Up, October 15, 2026

**Proposed decision:** First Year. **Confidence:** medium. **Approval:** Applied 2026-10-15.

**Reason:** Inference from authoritative curriculum rather than unseen notation. The Royal Conservatory’s 2016 syllabus places the tenor version in Grade 9, described as standard pre-professional repertoire, alongside Tcherepnin’s Sonatine sportive in the same grade. This supports provisional First Year. The publisher verifies an E-flat edition and a multi-instrument edition, so the existing soprano-only record is incomplete.

Billaudot AF 0049-D is the E-flat saxophone edition; publisher category allows piano or organ. Billaudot AF 0049 covers B-flat and E-flat saxophone versions plus other wind instruments with piano. CDMC’s exact AF 0049 holding names tenor OR baritone saxophone and piano. Royal Conservatory 2006 and 2016 syllabi list the tenor version in Grade 9. Soprano performance is permitted generically for tenor repertoire by the Royal Conservatory; no specific soprano part was verified.

**Collection context:** Original concert work distributed in alternative instrument editions. Retain; identify the chosen edition.

**Remaining gaps:** No actual notation or saxophone recording inspected. Composition date and a dedicated soprano edition unverified.

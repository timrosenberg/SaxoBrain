Catalog entry: [[Robert Planel - Prelude et Saltarelle]].

Planel's Prelude et Saltarelle is a two-movement alto saxophone and piano work, published separately as Leduc AL21824.
[Groth Music listing](https://www.grothmusic.com/p-38036.aspx) (search_index_only): A retailer result identifies the two-movement work and gives an approximate seven-minute duration.
[CDMC / WindMusic library catalog](https://windmusic.org/index.php?lvl=categ_see&nbr_lignes=14489&page=741) (opened): An opened institutional catalog lists the distinct 1944 Suite Romantique as six pieces, helping distinguish this separate work.

This is a guide description, not direct score inspection.

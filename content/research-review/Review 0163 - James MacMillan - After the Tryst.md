## Chat batch 11 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Boosey identifies a three-minute soprano saxophone or clarinet and piano version premiered by Gerard McChrystal on July 7, 1995. Hal Leonard confirms the soprano edition, McChrystal’s editorial credit and its origin for violin. The composer’s published note describes the melody as elongated and ornamented into a virtuosic and highly expressive miniature over simple arpeggiated piano chords. That is direct composer evidence of ornamentation and expressive demands, without an inspected saxophone range or tempo. Boosey recommends Sue McKenzie and Ingrid Sawers on Delphian DCD34201. The performers’ YouTube upload explicitly identifies an 80-second extract with McKenzie on soprano and Sawers on piano. The full commercial track is listed as 2:47 in Presto’s indexed track list. Existing composed: 1995 is the saxophone-version date. Boosey distinguishes the original violin work as 1988; this nonempty catalog value was preserved.

Collection context: Concert transcription of the violin miniature, professionally premiered and recorded. No youth simplification documented.

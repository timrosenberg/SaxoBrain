## Chat batch 10 follow-up, 2026-10-14

Fresh Groth browser inspection verifies Hermann Reutter, alto saxophone and piano, Alphonse Leduc AL21768 and a retailer count of three pages. Page count is not an established playing duration. The Arkansas School Band and Orchestra Association required solo/ensemble list, revised April 21, 2025, lists Reutter's Elegie under ALTO SAX SOLO III with list identifier SG39054 and LEDUC. This documents school contest circulation. WorldCat indexes an exact alto-saxophone/piano score published by Leduc in 1957. This is edition publication dating; the composition year remains unknown. Reutter's Piece Concertante on Joseph Wytko's recording page is a separate composition. Karel Husa's Elegie et Rondeau on the same album is also distinct. Neither supplies a recording for this Élégie. A same-composer Elegie movement in Epitaph für Ophelia is for violin and piano/orchestra and must also be excluded.

Collection context: School solo/ensemble contest use is verified in the Arkansas association's required list. No explicit youth simplification or original educational commission located. This source classification is not a SaxoBrain level.

Open questions: No publicly readable notation preview located. No exact playable saxophone performance verified. Composition year, duration, complete range and phrase/coordination demands remain unknown. The 1957 library date is publication, not composition.

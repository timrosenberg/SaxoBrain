**Identity and findings.** The catalogued alto/soprano saxophone piano version is not verified. Thierry Escaich's original Le Chant des ténèbres (1992) is for soprano saxophone and 12 saxophones or strings; Billaudot GB9831 is a later soprano saxophone and organ transcription by Tobias Willi, 15 minutes. A separate Billaudot BILL5367 listing for B-flat saxophone and piano appeared in a retailer catalog and needs identification.

**Sources checked.**
- [Thierry Escaich official catalogue](https://www.escaich.org/medias/files/catalogue-escaich-alpha-new-eng.pdf) (opened): Escaich's catalogue distinguishes the 1992 original saxophone concerto versions and a separate 2008 clarinet arrangement.
- [Éditions Billaudot](https://www.billaudot.com/le-chant-des-tenebres-1.html) (opened): Billaudot identifies GB9831 as soprano saxophone and organ, arranged by Tobias Willi, 15 minutes.
- [United Music Publishers catalogue](https://johanvanderlinden.com/saxophone_catalogue_2010.pdf) (opened): A Billaudot retailer catalog lists BILL5367 as B-flat saxophone and piano.
- [YouTube performance listing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iBXvSfOWZkY) (opened): A performance video listing credits Evgeni Novikov with soprano saxophone and the Amsterdam Conservatory Saxophone Ensemble.

## Research

Catalog: [[René Duclos - Piece Breve]]

- Identity: René Duclos's Pièce brève is an E-flat alto saxophone and piano work, Leduc AL20943 (1951). The Philharmonie guide describes three connected sections: melodic Allegretto in 6/8, Allegretto scherzando in 9/8, then a return to Allegretto; it lists low C to high E, a 3-minute duration, and fifth-year use in its conservatory sequence. The pitch convention for the range is unspecified.
- Guide entry gives instrumentation, 1951, 3 minutes, three connected sections in 6/8 and 9/8 with a tempo return, range low C to high E (pitch convention unspecified), and fifth-year placement. [Philharmonie de Paris, 10 ans avec le saxophone](https://catalogue.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (PDF p. 41, printed p. 44; opened).
- IMSLP publicly lists a complete score and separate E-flat saxophone part. Direct retrieval of both listed PDF paths returned an HTML verification page instead of notation. [IMSLP scan catalog](https://imslp.org/wiki/Pi%C3%A8ce_br%C3%A8ve_%28Duclos%2C_Ren%C3%A9%29) (opened).
- Luxembourg government syllabus lists the piece in Division inférieure, 1ère mention. [Luxembourg Ministry of Education](https://portal.education.lu/Portals/82/Documents/prog-saxophone.pdf) (search_index_only).
- Quebec conservatory syllabus includes the piece in its preconservatory repertoire. [Quebec Conservatory](https://www.conservatoire.gouv.qc.ca/media/0oulvph0/cmadq-progexternes-saxophone.pdf) (search_index_only).

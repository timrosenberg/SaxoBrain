# Review 0496: Erich Wolfgang Korngold - Much Ado About Nothing

[[Remaining Repertoire - Score Batch 04|Score batch 04]] · Catalog work (removed)

**Batch decision number: 3.** Decision recorded October 20, 2026.

## Decision evidence

**Tim’s decision:** Remove from the catalog. Recorded October 20, 2026.

**Evidence:** Source description / gaps. **Scoring:** Historical Masters M2774: soprano, alto, or tenor saxophone and keyboard; Leonard arrangement. Current Alfred listing labels alto saxophone or clarinet and piano.. **Duration:** Unknown.

- Rockford Symphony program notes describe March of the Watch as a deliberately awkward march and Masquerade-Hornpipe as a dance with an irregular beat. These are original-suite character descriptions; transfer to Leonard's saxophone arrangement requires the score.
- Two excerpt identities are newly established for Masters M2774: March of the Watch and Masquerade. Treating this edition as the complete four-movement violin suite would be unsafe.

**No notation inspected for this card.** Source descriptions and recording identification are not direct score analysis. Use Hold if the evidence does not support a decision.

**Inspected scope and limits:** Historical publisher catalog visually inspected, printed p. 32 / PDF p. 30. It is a catalog page with no musical notation. Original-suite program notes provide attributed character evidence only. No exact saxophone recording verified.

**Edition or identity note:** The cataloged soprano-only title has no edition identifier. Masters M2774 is a plausible newly corroborated soprano candidate, but the owned edition remains unknown. Current Alfred SKU 36-M277491 has conflicting instrumentation wording. Do not change instrumentation or assume the entire suite.

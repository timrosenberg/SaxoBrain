Catalog: [[Marcel Dautremer - Emotion]]

- Identity: The Philharmonie de Paris saxophone guide identifies Dautremer’s Émotion as a 1969 alto saxophone and piano piece in two parts: Andante in 4/4 at quarter=72, then a triple-meter poco scherzando in 6/8. The printed tempo for the second section is described as slow; the guide lists the piece at 3:15 and the range from low B to high E, without specifying written or sounding pitch convention.
- Guide entry gives date, sections, meter, tempos, duration and range descriptor. [Philharmonie de Paris, 10 ans avec le saxophone](https://catalogue.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (PDF p.40, printed p.43; opened).
- Lemoine/retailer edition metadata gives E-flat saxophone and piano, HX24250, and a publisher/vendor “easy” descriptor. [Sheet Music Plus, Lemoine edition listing](https://www.sheetmusicplus.com/en/product/emotion-19471595.html) (search_index_only).

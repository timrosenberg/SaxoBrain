## Chat batch 12 follow-up, 2026-10-14

The Southern Music / Hal Leonard product page verifies ST520 / HL03775239 and Fred Hemke’s arranger credit. Groth’s cover identifies the full Introduction and Variations title, piano accompaniment and Frederick Hemke as editor. The piano edition is now established directly. Southern Music’s R212 is the separate band accompaniment, with the saxophone solo edited by Hemke and Herbert Clarke / R. Mark Rogers credited for orchestration. Its publisher duration is approximately six minutes. Southern Music states that Demersseman originally wrote the piece for Adolphe Mayeur, a pupil of Adolphe Sax. This establishes saxophone-origin context, while the present solo and accompaniment retain their named editorial and arrangement identities. Van Cott Information Services describes the ST520 piano edition as a showpiece with a final variation to be played as fast as possible. This is a retailer’s technical description; no matching notation was accessible. The Andrew Harrison performer upload explicitly credits the Hemke alto-and-piano version with pianist Jason Lo. Its duration metadata is 4:58. Strath Haven High School’s program identifies junior Dae Taylor and the Hemke version played with wind ensemble. This documents substantial youth performance use with a named teacher, without evidence that the score was simplified.

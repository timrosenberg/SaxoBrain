**Identity and findings.** Dietrich Erdmann’s Akzente A is an alto saxophone and piano work published by Breitkopf & Härtel, EB 9138. EDA Records lists three movements: Allegro con fuoco, Adagio espressivo, and Presto grotesco. The Philharmonie guide also lists a distinct tenor-and-piano work titled Akzente.

**Sources checked.**
- [Stretta Music edition record](https://www.stretta-music.fr/erdmann-akzente-a-nr-1688005.html) (search_index_only): Retailer lists Akzente A for alto saxophone and piano, Breitkopf EB 9138, ISMN.
- [EDA Records](https://www.eda-records.com/177-1-CD-Details.html?cd_id=42) (opened): Label page identifies the 1989/2003 work and its three movement titles within an album of Berlin alto saxophone and piano music.
- [Noten Roehr retailer](https://www.noten-roehr.de/en/scores/0005/woodwind-instruments/0507/saxophone/0504/woodwind%2Bpiano-harpsichord/022046) (search_index_only): Composer site search lead lists separate Akzente for tenor saxophone and piano; distinguish it from Akzente A for alto.
- [Claude Delangle et al., 10 ans avec le saxophone (Philharmonie de Paris digital scan)](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p.93; printed p.98): The guide’s separate tenor/piano Akzente entry is dated 1989 and has two movements.

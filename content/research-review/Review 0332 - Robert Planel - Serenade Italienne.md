Catalog entry: [[Robert Planel - Serenade Italienne]].

Planel's Sérénade italienne is Suite Romantique no. 1 for alto saxophone and piano, Leduc AL20246.
[Michel Musique listing](https://www.michelmusique.fr/planel-r-suite-romantique-na-1-saxo-alto.html) (search_index_only): A French music retailer identifies Sérénade italienne as an excerpt from Planel's Suite romantique for alto saxophone and piano.
[CDMC / WindMusic library catalog](https://windmusic.org/index.php?lvl=categ_see&nbr_lignes=14489&page=741) (opened): An opened institutional catalog records the Suite romantique as six pieces for alto saxophone and piano, Leduc, 1944.
[Philharmonie de Paris, Dix ans avec le saxophone](https://mediatheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/AloesSynchro/stockage/10-ans-avec-le-saxophone.pdf) (opened, PDF p. 44 (printed p. 47)): The Philharmonie de Paris ten-year saxophone guide describes no. 1, Sérénade italienne, as a slow recitative joined to a 3/4 serenade at quarter note = 160; listed duration 2:40. This is a guide description, not direct score inspection.

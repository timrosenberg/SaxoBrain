- Identity: Frederick Koch’s *Apocalyptic* exists in separate Southern Music editions for soprano saxophone and piano (1995) and alto saxophone and piano (1996). - Collection/use: Two separately published saxophone editions are documented; intended audience and performance use are unknown.
- Gaps: No readable score, recording, duration, or publisher difficulty descriptor was found.; The exact soprano-edition item number and relationship to the alto-edition purchase link remain unverified.
- Proposed correction: Keep soprano saxophone as a supported edition option; consider linking both distinct editions or disambiguating the current alto purchase link.

## Chat batch 09 follow-up, 2026-10-13

Both exact Stanton’s edition pages state Permanently Out of Print. General digital-delivery boilerplate on those pages does not establish that this item has a digital edition. An opened copyright-record transcription identifies soprano and piano, 12 pages plus part, created 1995 and published February 1, 1996 (PA0000809884). It identifies alto and piano, score plus part, created 1996 and published May 1, 1996 (PA0000811911). Correction to the older review: the transcription does not say the soprano edition was published in 1995. Its 1995 field is creation. The original composition year has not been verified in a primary score or composer source. No musical demands could be established from exact score or performance evidence. Same-title Apocalyptic Dreams and unrelated works were excluded.

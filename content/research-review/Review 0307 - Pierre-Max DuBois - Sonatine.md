## Research

Catalog: [[Pierre-Max DuBois - Sonatine]]

- Identity: Dubois's Sonatine is the Leduc AL23742 alto saxophone/piano work, distinct from Deuxième Sonatine (AL23963). The Royal Conservatoire Antwerp list dates Sonatine to 1966 and gives an eight-minute duration; a retailer matches AL23742 to the title. The 1968 date belongs to the separate Deuxième Sonatine and should not be applied here.
- Royal Conservatoire Antwerp library list identifies Sonatine AL23742, 1966, eight minutes, dedicated to François Daneels. [Royal Conservatoire Antwerp library catalog](https://www.libraryconservatoryantwerp.be/lijsten/BSAX.pdf) (opened).
- Retailer identifies AL23742 as Dubois's Sonatine for alto saxophone and piano; date was not independently confirmed there. [Sakupara sheet music store](https://sakupara.stores.jp/items/645cf17539cb4e002bea31b1) (search_index_only).
- Windmusic records a distinct Deuxième Sonatine as AL23963, an exam work, 1968, 12 pages plus separate parts. [Windmusic library](https://windmusic.org/index.php?id=5389&lvl=notice_display) (search_index_only).
- Collection/use: Published saxophone/piano sonatina with library-listed conservatory contest use.
- Review focus: Inference: a conservatory library’s duration and contest note make formal pacing relevant; the distinct second sonatina is a separate work.
- Gaps: No score or recording inspected; verify date and dedication against Leduc's primary score page if available.
- Proposed correction: Retain 1966 for Sonatine AL23742 from the conservatory library list. Keep 1968 attached to the distinct Deuxième Sonatine AL23963.

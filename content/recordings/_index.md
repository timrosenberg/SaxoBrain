The following list is a short, and by no means exhaustive set of saxophone-focused recordings for students to know. Students should take time with these recordings to truly know them.

The list is also represented in the [Saxophone Curriculum](/curriculum/). Unless otherwise noted, all links are directed to Odesli (album.link, song.link) so that listeners can choose their preferred steaming service.

# First Year

## Required Listening

- Intro to Classical Saxophone
    - [*Saxophone and Piano*](https://album.link/i/1118489646) by Arno Bornkamp
    - [*The Historic Saxophone](https://album.link/us/i/330892843)* by Claude Delangle
    - [*Recital*](https://livestetson.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/StetsonSaxos2/IgCTsdBYfwWRTKFaAwyy1rTGAUcQqZkRp3y68s6lTnCTy1o?e=C5FAkg) by Otis Murphy
    - [*Maslanka: Concertos*](https://album.link/us/i/195157616) by Joseph Lulloff
    - [*An Exhibition of Saxophone*](https://album.link/us/i/1493753584) by Nobuya Sugawa
    - [*Saxophone Classics*](https://album.link/us/i/363166027) by Diastema Saxophone Quartet
- Intro to Jazz Saxophone
    - [*Stitt Plays Bird*](https://album.link/us/i/1488827497) by Sonny Stitt
    - [*Cannonball Takes Charge*](https://album.link/us/i/724424981) by Cannonball Adderley
    - [*Saxophone Colossus*](https://album.link/us/i/1440952935) by Sonny Rollins
    - [*Dexter Calling*](https://album.link/us/i/724840234) by Dexter Gordon
    - [*Inspiration](https://album.link/i/863872574)* by Max Ionata
    - [*The Inside of the Outside*](https://album.link/i/1187384687) by Jeff Coffin

# Second Year

## Required Listening

    - [*Notturno*](https://album.link/us/i/1520663218) by Timothy McAllister
    - [*A la Française*](https://album.link/us/i/331278809) by Claude Delangle

    - [*Grieg, Glazanouv, Dvorak*](https://album.link/i/1255924858) by Quatuor Habanera
- Next Steps: Jazz Saxophone
    - [*Blue Train*](https://album.link/us/i/1468202477) by John Coltrane
    - [*Kind of Blue*](https://album.link/us/i/268443092) by Miles Davis
    - [*Wish*](https://album.link/us/i/305687509) by Joshua Redman

    - [*Two of a Mind](https://album.link/us/i/525523977)* by Paul Desmond and Gerry Mulligan

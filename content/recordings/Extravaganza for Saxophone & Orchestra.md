Debra Richtmeyer, alto and soprano saxophones, with the Slovak Radio Symphony Orchestra, conducted by Kirk Trevor. Released by Albany Records (TROY0593).

The program includes Richtmeyer's soprano saxophone transcription of [[Richard Strauss]]'s oboe concerto, David Ott's alto saxophone concerto, [[Alexander Glazunov - Concerto|Glazunov's Concerto]], and [[Sergei Rachmaninoff - Vocalise|Rachmaninoff's Vocalise]].
